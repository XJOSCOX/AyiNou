# C1.5 reviewer operations

| Requirement | Classification | Operating boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ouest triage authority | Implemented and verified | Keep active Ouest membership and the existing community staff role, explicit scoped and expiring triage grant, recent authenticator, original moderation workspace and a case assignment. |
| Evidence-review assignment | Implemented and verified | Existing verification reviewer plus current scoped review grant and bound workspace. Case access is scoped and acceptance confirms independence. The triager and author cannot be the reviewer. |
| Publication and association review | Implemented and verified | Assign separate eligible reviewers after checking need, evidence, publication, project and team conflicts. Approval is bound to the exact material reviewed. |
| Challenges | Implemented and verified | Evidence/publication challenges follow their specified windows and separate assignments. Protect the original decision, use a reviewer free of the contested work, and record outcome and reason in the private ledger. Existing independent enforcement appeals retain their own procedure. |
| Eligible named roster and coverage | Requires external infrastructure or staffing | Operations must name a queue owner and enough independent reviewers to cover evidence, publication, association, challenge and conflicts, plus alternates. No real roster was appointed. |
| Expiry and recertification | Implemented but not operationally verified | SQL rejects expired or revoked grants and workspaces. An operating owner must define approval, maximum term, recertification evidence and removal when duties or membership end. |
| Conflict disclosure and recusal | Implemented but not operationally verified | Server conflict rules, review acceptance and existing case conflict handling apply. Document personal or financial conflicts unknown to the system. When unsure, stop reading and return/escalate case assignment through the existing safe lane. |
| Queues and support escalation | Requires external infrastructure or staffing | Designate a daily queue owner, emergency coverage, challenge lead, privacy contact and approved member help process. Set staffing and turnaround commitments after a rehearsal. |

Before each shift verify authority and workspace freshness, unassigned and aging cases, clarification responses, challenge deadlines and reviewer availability. Assign from current scoped grants and cross-check prior assignments and material project interests. If no independent reviewer is available, leave the case pending and escalate for alternate staffing or pause intake. Existing governance decisions, partial evidence and audit history remain protected throughout reassignment.

Before approval, compare the allowed snapshot to the canonical revision. Use safe coded member feedback where supported. Keep evidence and written case reasons in authorized records and keep general operations reporting aggregate. On privileged departure, revoke the grant and case access, reconcile workspace/session status, preserve events, and transfer queue ownership.
