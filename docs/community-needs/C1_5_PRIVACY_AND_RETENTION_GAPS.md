# C1.5 privacy and retention gaps

| Data or control | Classification | Current behavior and outstanding decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Private needs and original revisions | Implemented and verified | Private RLS tables and author/session-scoped RPCs. Authorized evidence reviewers receive necessary revision material. General moderation, project and community views receive restricted projections. Approve necessity of each evidence field and prohibited submissions. |
| Reviewer observations, challenge evidence and audit | Implemented and verified | Original events, actor references, receipts and reasons stay in scoped ledgers. Member feedback reveals safe codes. Staff reads and audit exports have separate purposes. Review the incident path for sensitive material submitted against instructions. |
| Publication snapshots and attribution | Implemented and verified | Exact approved snapshot, community membership, privacy-by-default attribution. Withdrawal and invalidation affect fresh projections and preserve prior records. Text screening and human review must both support field-level minimization. |
| Project associations, consent and notices | Implemented and verified | Decisions pin revisions, include separate author consent, and require read access to both resources. Notices are recipient-only and omit private text/time-sensitive evidence. Scoped ledger records withdrawal and revocation. |
| Withdrawal, hiding and removal | Implemented and verified | Visibility is withdrawn and linked references invalidated through native paths. Ledger entries and immutable prior content can remain; visibility removal is distinct from deleting stored content or prior copies. |
| Account deletion dependencies | Implemented but not operationally verified | Several Auth references are set null on account deletion, and scoped authority or conflict rows have independent cascade behavior. Needs, revisions, publication and association relations retain cross-record provenance and private text. Losing live access or deleting an account does not guarantee erasure of textual identifiers, retained evidence, discussion copies or notifications. Inventory each FK and ownership case in an isolated rehearsal before any real closure. |
| Export and erasure | Missing | Existing privacy workflows remain synthetic. C1.5 verified no end-to-end authorized export or erasure for C1.1–C1.4. Define redaction for third-party evidence and staff, evidence-preserving disposition, secure delivery, verification and propagation into backups and caches. |
| Retention and legal holds | Requires legal/privacy approval | Existing historical rows remain private. Define separate retention triggers and dispositions for drafts, evidence, observations, challenges, publication snapshots, consent, links, notices and audits. Approve lawful holds, custody, overrides, releases and backups. No statutory or product retention period is assumed. |
| Restore after database recovery | Requires external infrastructure or staffing | Rehearse on approved isolated infrastructure. Restore with all production intake/review/publication/association entry points paused, reconcile recent legal holds, auth and role revocations, consent withdrawals, notices and blocked records, then verify evidence and audit history with the operations and privacy owners. |

The Founder and qualified privacy/legal reviewers must confirm the purposes and allowed subjects, eligibility and age rules, jurisdiction, authorized reviewer disclosure, notices and translation, request rights, retention schedule, legal hold authorities and provider handling. Define how to identify and remove prohibited or incidental sensitive information while maintaining approved accountability for decisions. Document member channels for correction, challenge, safety reports and rights requests with escalation times approved by the operating and privacy owners.
