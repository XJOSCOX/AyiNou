# C1 nationwide department policy

All ten department scopes are independent and disabled by default. Eligible preparation is an explicit per-department setting. A member must be an active account holder, finish onboarding, belong to the department, be eligible under existing moderation restrictions, meet the enabled identity requirement, and be enrolled by the Founder. Identity verification uses the existing standard and Founder Verified parity. Joining a department grants no enrollment or review authority.

The current policy can specify between 1 and 20 concurrent participants, a seven-day capacity between 1 and 10 submissions (default 10), and a term of up to 30 days. The existing member quotas still apply. A selected cap cannot fall below the currently valid enrollment count. The first activation pins the duration and approved release. While active, existing C1 restrictions on stage changes apply and only the weekly capacity can be lowered without pausing. Increasing the safe limits requires a separately authorized policy revision.

Prepared scopes require at least one eligible triager and four distinct independent reviewers. Grants, assignments, deadlines, case conflict screening and evidence stay in the existing native workflows. An independently held, current record for each of privacy and retention, support, database recovery, private Storage recovery, matching release and launch blocker closure is mandatory before activation or resumption. The Founder can inspect their status and prepare the department. Independent requirements cannot be waived from the workspace.

A department can be paused individually. The Founder can also pause selected active departments or apply a nationwide emergency pause. The receipt records the actor, workspace, time, reason, affected department, before/after policy and resulting revision per scope. Other departments remain untouched for a selected pause. Each affected department requires its own resumption decision and current evidence. Completed or expired scopes cannot be reopened through these controls.
