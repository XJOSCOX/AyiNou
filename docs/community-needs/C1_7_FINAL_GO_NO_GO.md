# C1.7 final go/no-go

**NO-GO for live Ouest intake, evidence review, publication and project association.** AyiNou Community Needs is nationwide in scope. All 10 department communities remain closed for live processing. Ouest is the only department eligible for the current protected pilot activation mechanism. The planned first pilot is a maximum of 20 participants, 10 submissions per rolling seven days, and 30 days.

| Decision needed from the Founder | Evidence required for a later go decision |
| --- | --- |
| Identify the operational lead, local support lead, triage and independent review roster and alternates | Named eligible leads, approved grant terms and shifts, coverage and escalation record |
| Approve the proposed scope, limits, term, invitations and success measures | Ouest eligibility, private notice and onboarding plan, capacity and service targets, term and exit arrangements |
| Authorize qualified privacy and legal review | Approved purpose, translated notices, retention and hold rules, redaction, rights and reconciliation procedures |
| Identify the provider recovery operator and approve recovery objectives and isolated target | A usable database recovery point, approved object backup, independent restoration and authorization verification |
| Identify authorized Next.js and Fastify hosting and release procedure | Verified operator, matching deployed revision, health, security and rollback results with routing closed |
| Assign independent operational custody and require closure of critical and high blockers | Six valid attestations scoped to Ouest and the exact release, with a verifier, expiry, and safely held source evidence |
| Record any later activation or resumption decision in the protected workspace | Current independent approvals and evidence, active staffing and enrollment, supported stage configuration and separately authorized go decision |

After Ouest closes, compare the limited rollout with agreed outcomes and publish only approved aggregate results. Review recovery, staffing and privacy before proposing another departmental wave. The [operational readiness plan](C1_7_OPERATIONAL_READINESS.md) defines the future department-specific controls. C1.7 introduces a read-only readiness projection and operating plans.
