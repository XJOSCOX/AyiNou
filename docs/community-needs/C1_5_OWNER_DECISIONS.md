# C1.5 owner decisions

These decisions remain open for any real pilot. The database repair and read-only compatibility checks do not authorize any of them.

| Decision owner | Classification | Decision and acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder and product operations | Requires Founder approval | Approve Ouest-only cohort, admission and eligibility criteria, capacity, workflow stage switches, scope and expiry, escalation authority, exit criteria and go/no-go owner. Record a dated decision and stop conditions. |
| Founder and security | Requires Founder approval | Approve separately reviewed production activation and emergency pause mechanisms. Require approved scope, recent MFA, change receipts, independent review where applicable, and a disposable enable/pause/restore rehearsal. Reject any use of fixture rows in production. |
| Reviewer operations and safeguarding | Requires external infrastructure or staffing | Produce the staffed role and alternate roster, independence checklist, recertification process, evidence/challenge coverage, contact routes and queue response commitments. |
| Qualified privacy/legal lead | Requires legal/privacy approval | Approve purpose, notice and consent, eligibility/age policy, retention and hold categories, export/redaction, erasure, backup suppression and individual support. Approve safe metrics audiences and suppression. |
| Release and recovery owner | Requires external infrastructure or staffing | Verify actual web/API destination, release and rollback procedure, provider backup entitlement, protected restore rehearsal and compatibility evidence. |
| Founder and operations | Requires Founder approval | Close the pilot to new subjects on expiry or any approved stop condition, reconcile unresolved and challenged cases, publish approved summary and separate consent/retention notices where appropriate. Explicitly approve any continuation under a new decision. |
