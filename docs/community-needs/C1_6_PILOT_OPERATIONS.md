# C1.6 Ouest pilot operations

## Enrollment and capacity

Use the Founder cohort control to search an exact or partial existing Ouest username, select an eligible account, choose a bounded term and record a written reason. Eligibility requires onboarding, current membership, an active account and no applicable Community Needs restriction. The maximum concurrent cohort is 20 time-valid enrollments. The term cannot exceed the configured duration or the open pilot window. Membership and appropriate standing are rechecked. Removal stops new pilot work while authorized private case access and safe actions remain available. Enrollment conveys no reviewer, moderator, project, community or institutional privileges.

The default maximum is ten submissions per rolling seven days. The Founder may lower it to one and may pause intake entirely. The native per-member policy remains in effect, so the stricter limit applies. The database counts only successful native submissions recorded for pilot cases. The cap decision and submission commit together, and failures receive an explicit capacity message. Use the dashboard to monitor withdrawals, existing workload and remaining capacity without exposing case narratives.

## Reviewer operations

Use existing C1.2 grants and case assignment workflows for the Ouest room. A qualified triager needs current membership, scoped staff status, the existing grant and a valid moderation workspace. Independent reviewer coverage counts active scoped verification reviewers who are outside the cohort, are not platform leadership and do not fill triage. The existing system continues to reject material conflicts, prior incompatible assignments and approval without a current assigned lane. The current grant term is managed by the existing C1.2 policy, which issues a 30-day grant subject to revocation and recertification.

The Founder view shows each recorded grant, its expiry, current eligibility, counts of open assignments and pending challenges, and aggregate conflicts and backlog. Review case-specific conflicts, recusals and assignments in the native review workspace. Confirm real shift coverage and alternates through the separate support attestation. If eligible reviewers are unavailable, retain existing case deadlines, escalate to the named support owner and pause new work when warranted. No real reviewers are appointed by this release.

## Emergency operations

The Founder pauses in the protected administration workspace and records the reason. Preserve current cases and evidence. Use existing support and enforcement channels for incidents. An unresolved challenge without a valid assignment requires escalation while assignments remain paused. Review freshness, staffing, backup reconciliation and all operational attestations before a distinct resumption decision. If the duration expired, prepare a separately approved subsequent release rather than changing the expired term.
