# C1.6 launch readiness

| Item | State at delivery | Evidence required before real activation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ouest configuration, quota, cohort and pause controls | Implemented, default-disabled | Founder separately chooses an exact configuration and records the decision in the native protected workspace. |
| Real cohort | Pending approval | Approve whom to invite, eligibility and consent rules. Enroll eligible members individually for approved terms. |
| Reviewer and challenge coverage | Pending staffing and approval | Document primary/alternate reviewers, shifts, conflicts, recusal and renewal. Issue justified scoped grants separately. |
| Privacy and retention | Pending qualified sign-off | Approve processing purpose, evidence minimization, permitted public reference handling, private submission history, reviewer notes, publication snapshots, project associations and consent. Confirm notices and age/eligibility rules. No legal retention schedule is presumed. |
| Export, erasure and holds | Missing live procedure | Extend the existing synthetic privacy workflow with an approved Community Needs case/dependency inventory, third-party redaction, authorized delivery, retention and holds. Evidence and attribution may survive a visibility change. Administrative history stores purpose-limited logical actor and subject identifiers so that role/account changes do not rewrite decisions. |
| Database recovery | Pending verified rehearsal | Demonstrate an authorized isolated restore, verify the recovery point and permissions, reconcile sessions, grants, withdrawals, private decisions and legal holds, and document cutover controls. |
| Private Storage recovery | Pending verified rehearsal | Restore approved private objects and integrity checks separately from database metadata. Verify object authorization and reconcile copies and deletion/hold requirements. |
| Matching application release | Pending verified hosted destination | Verify authorized web/API origins, matching release, test and rollback procedure. Keep runtime release routing disabled until an approved launch sequence. |
| Critical/high launch blockers | Pending independent review | Record accepted closure of all critical and high findings for the exact release. Ambiguous or stale operational evidence must block resumption. |

Public references use the C1.1 restricted HTTPS format and remain in private case evidence. They are stripped from the C1.3 community snapshot contract. Approved snapshots, private ledger entries, review notes, notices and links use separate privacy scopes. Withdrawing consent removes an applicable projection without erasing the underlying history.
