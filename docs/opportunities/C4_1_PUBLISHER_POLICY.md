# C4.1 Publisher policy

| Authority | Private read | Create | Edit and withdraw | Submit or publish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Current eligible organization owner or administrator | Yes | Yes | Yes | Closed |
| Current recognized representative with `opportunities.draft` | Yes | Yes | Requires edit permission | Closed |
| Current recognized representative with `opportunities.edit` | Yes | Requires draft permission | Yes | Closed |
| Representative without either scoped permission, affiliate alone, platform role alone | Denied | Denied | Denied | Closed |
| Revoked representative or archived or verification-revoked organization | Denied | Denied | Denied | Closed |

Each representative needs a current member session, account standing and identity eligibility. The existing organization role catalogue handles grant and delegation authorization. Previously reserved `recruitment.manage` remains reserved. `opportunities.submit` and `opportunities.publish` are reserved until those workflows receive their own review. Neither permission confers applicant access.

An eligible unverified organization may prepare private drafts while the preparation gate is open. Future public eligibility requires current independent verification under existing organization policy and a current active owner. Organization verification establishes organizational identity. Every opportunity needs its own review of accuracy, legitimacy and any claim about qualifications, compensation or access.

Fraud reports should use the established moderation and appeal systems with opportunity-specific categories for fraudulent listings, misleading scholarships, impersonation, unlawful recruitment, exploitation and unsafe destinations. Future reporting requires defined reviewer scopes, notice, appealability, evidence preservation and retention. Public listing, candidate discovery, applicant messaging, collection of resumes and credentials, background checks, youth mentorship and regulated placement require separate approved scopes.
