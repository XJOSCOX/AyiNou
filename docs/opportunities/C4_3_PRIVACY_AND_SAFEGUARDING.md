# C4.3. Privacy and safeguarding

| Data | Access and lifecycle |
| --- | --- |
| Role selections, goals, skills, experience, availability and preferences | Owner-only. Keep one current role record and selected catalog reference. Cascades on an authorized account deletion, subject to existing lifecycle and holds. |
| Adult and safeguarding confirmations | Owner-only timestamps. No age verification claim, date of birth, supporting evidence or background checks. |
| Purpose-specific choices | Owner-only current choices and recording time. Recommendations, discoverability and requests are separate for each role. Public disclosure has no storage toggle or read route. |
| Pause and withdrawal | Pause excludes that role from future discovery. Full withdrawal clears that role’s choices and participation consent. Partial withdrawal affects only the selected purpose. Withdrawal never alters volunteer or opportunity data. |
| Retry receipts | Current owner command hash and receipt ID per role. No duplicate private preference snapshot. Keep the profile revision for retry and stale-write protection. |
| Administrative preparation | Restricted to the original, MFA-backed Founder workspace. History preserves the version, reasons and operational settings. No member preferences are exposed. |

Profile data is private. A future recipient must satisfy account privacy, blocking, muting and standing as well as mentorship-specific consent. The Founder can narrow supported categories and caps but cannot enable a member's consent. Do not enter residences, credentials, academic, medical or financial evidence in the optional fields. C4.3 has no public recipient, message, marketing or external transfer route.

Before activation, the Founder and safeguarding lead must approve an adult eligibility process beyond self-attestation, response coverage, reporting and escalation, safe on-site contact rules, and restrictions on off-platform solicitation. C4.4 must propose narrowly scoped mentorship moderation capabilities and appeal handling. C4.3 reuses account restrictions and creates no new moderation powers.

The privacy lead must review record classification, purpose and processing, consent history, owner export, erasure, documented retention, audit evidence, account deletion and legal-hold interplay through the existing privacy system. This release enables no live export or erasure. Final retention and live safeguards belong to the G-series review.
