# C4.2. Applications and privacy

- **External application.** Submission records the organization’s ownership or authorized-use evidence. A different eligible reviewer checks the exact destination. A signed-in member explicitly requests the approved address after a warning and chooses whether to follow the link. The platform stores no external credentials and performs no fetch or redirect. A review evidences a decision at a point in time. Report a changed or unsafe destination.
- **Native expressions of interest.** Members preview their current public display name and username. An adult-confirmed submission records the exact opportunity revision, explicit consent and a bounded statement. It is an invitation to communicate, without a formal application, offer or commitment. Organization-scoped recipients can acknowledge, record follow-up or close their own authorized records. There is no candidate ranking or automatic rejection.
- **Withdrawal and blocking.** Own history remains separate from the organization inbox. Withdrawal clears the shared identity and statement and blocks future access in AyiNou. Previously copied information can remain with its recipient. Blocking or a privacy change can hide current recipient access. Generic in-app updates carry no statement or applicant identity.
- **Events.** Only compatible native events from the same organization can be associated. Their public eligibility, exact schedule and participation are rechecked. Links open the existing Event page and reuse native registration, capacity and RSVP. No event venue, private meeting link or participant data is copied into the listing.
- **Restricted data.** This release collects no resumes, transcripts, documents, private address, health information, background checks or finances. Volunteer profiles are separate. Adult self-attestation is a disposable workflow protection. Operational safeguarding, youth eligibility and regulated placements need an independent plan.

Draft and publication history retains submitted content, reviewer appointments and decisions retain scope and reasons, and destination ownership evidence remains restricted. Applicant consent and withdrawal, notifications, moderation and administrative receipts should receive record-specific authorized export and retention decisions. Legal holds must be resolved before erasure. This release enables no real exports or erasure and sets no final retention schedule.
