# C4.2. Publication policy

An eligible, currently verified organization with a qualifying representative and the scoped submit permission can submit an exact draft. The publishing representative separately needs the publish permission and its current approval. A valid organization identity is one requirement. It confers no approval on an opportunity.

| Decision | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Submit | Binds private type-specific details and official ownership evidence to one revision |
| Assign or release | Limits review to a current scoped independent reviewer or returns it to the queue |
| Approve | Records the exact reviewed revision, checklist and reason |
| Request changes or reject | Keeps the listing private and gives the organizer a reason |
| Challenge | Allows one challenge of a reasoned adverse decision on the revision within 14 days |
| Publish | Explicitly publishes the approved current revision |
| Edit after approval or publication | Creates a new private draft revision and suppresses the old publication |
| Close, withdraw, expire, restrict or archive | Stops applicable disclosures while retaining restricted records |

Haiti supports all ten department identifiers. International review is country scoped. Location and participation are explicit. An opportunity does not convey work authorization, immigration permission or visa sponsorship. The expiry date is evaluated on Haiti local time.

Display a verified organization and the review status separately. Compensation, cost, tuition and funding are informational. Zero, unknown and undisclosed are distinct. The approved workflow contains no payroll, contracts, recruitment fees, payment collection, fund distribution or escrow. Integrated project payments and custody are deferred indefinitely.

Report categories address fraudulent opportunities, misleading scholarships, impersonation, unlawful recruitment, exploitation, harassment, unsafe destinations and privacy. A geographically scoped independent reviewer can restrict an eligible reported listing with a reason. Restricted records preserve challenges and history. Renewal requires a new eligible review before publication.
