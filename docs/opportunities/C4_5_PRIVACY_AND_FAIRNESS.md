# Opportunity privacy and fairness

Purpose-specific opt-in authorizes AyiNou to use private preferences for recommendations. It grants no sharing with employers, third parties, mentorship, volunteer matching, recruiting or marketing. Pausing stops recommendations. Withdrawing also clears dismissals; clearing preferences removes consent, settings and dismissals. Bookmarks are independent.

Imports preview only the owner’s current eligible source skills. Copying requires selected codes and confirmation bound to the source revision. The stored copies belong to Opportunity preferences, and never update volunteer or mentorship profiles. The copy stores a skill and self-reported level, without a permanent import history.

Bookmarks store the owner and opportunity ID plus the saved time. Saving creates no message, application, or publisher notification. Dismissals store the owner, exact publication revision, and expiry, with a bounded maximum. The latest owner command receipt stores only a request identifier, fingerprint, action name, and version. Founder configuration history records the actor and workspace, version, time, reason and receipt. Account deletion detaches the Founder actor reference and cascades member preferences, bookmark, dismissal and owner receipt records.

Preferences, active dismissals and bookmarks belong in future owner export and erasure inventories. Operational legal hold, final retention, inactive dismissal cleanup and audit retention are G-series decisions. C4.5 does not enable real privacy execution.

Ranking uses disclosed opportunity facts and explicit preferences. Donations, payments, recognition, role, popularity, health, protected characteristics, residence and citizenship are outside the ranking inputs. A member can explicitly prefer a location independent of residence. The service infers no visa or immigration status.
