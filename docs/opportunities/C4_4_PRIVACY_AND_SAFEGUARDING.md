# C4.4 privacy and safeguarding

Mentorship is free, adult-to-adult and educational. Adult self-attestation is recorded separately from identity. Qualifying independently reviewed identity and valid Founder Verified provenance follow existing AyiNou policy. Neither constitutes verified age or expertise. Youth, dating, regulated advisory, financial solicitation, paid placement and credential issuance are outside the program. Real in-person arrangements require separately approved safeguards.

| Data | Access and lifecycle |
| --- | --- |
| Matching preferences | C4.3 owns the current role, skills and independently selected consent. C4.4 derives a private topic index only in the disposable cohort. Candidate search stores no profiles or results. Role removal cascades its index and dismissals. |
| Requests | Two participants have access to safe request state. A pending introduction is disclosed only while the request is current. Terminal transition clears it. Abuse limits use timestamps and actor references, without ranking. |
| Agreements, learning objectives and sessions | Participants can review mutually agreed revisions and bounded progress/history when current safety rules permit. Former participants retain safe history. Safety restriction suppresses content access, including after termination. |
| Notices | Existing in-app notifications contain an opaque reference and action. A current visibility check runs before projection. Opening the destination repeats session and participant authorization. No goals, schedules, messages or addresses are copied. |
| Reports | The reporting participant files a bounded reason. The Founder may assign an eligible and independent administrator to a named case. The queue exposes metadata. Only the assigned administrator with the current MFA workspace can inspect the submitted explanation. Referral and closure do not issue a sanction. Native enforcement and independent appeals apply to any subsequent account action. |
| Lifecycle and preparation receipts | Domain-only receipts preserve command fingerprints and minimal outcome metadata. Administrative versions retain the reason and workspace in restricted history. |

Account deletion cascades participant coordination records through participant foreign keys. Report evidence, joint record export, redaction of the other participant’s data, approved holds, retention schedules and backup recovery require G-series decisions before live processing. Never interpret a cascade as operational approval for erasure. No real export, erasure or hold workflow is enabled by this migration.

Before activation, designate and train independent reviewers and incident leads. Approve age assurance, safeguarding recertification, report escalation, survivor privacy, recovery, operating hours and cross-border procedures. Restrict review to the reported facts and support existing independent appeals. Never ask users to place contact details, precise meeting locations or sensitive evidence in the coordination workspace.
