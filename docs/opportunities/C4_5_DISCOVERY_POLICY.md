# Opportunity discovery policy

Latest opens on the current approved directory in reverse publication order. Closing soon sorts by deadline and omits undated listings. Saved is private and shows an unavailable placeholder when eligibility fails. An owner can remove the placeholder.

Only an exact approved current revision may appear. The C4.2 publication guard checks provider verification, standing, eligibility, current dates, restrictions, and invalidation. Discovery additionally checks account blocks and mutes and requires a currently available approved application method. It carries no draft content, external URLs, or evidence. C4.2, the organization profile, and Events provide the existing application surfaces. Changing or closing an invalidated listing follows the prior publication workflow.

Keyword search covers the approved title and description. Filters are restricted to the configured vocabulary, including type, Haitian department, country, category, participation mode, catalog skill, language, deadline, and type-specific employment, payment or scholarship coverage. Optional compensation appears as stated by the approved revision, with distinct unknown, undisclosed, zero and disclosed range states. The service performs no conversions or transactions.

Every cursor binds to the original member, filters, ordering and active configuration. For You also binds to the preference version. The returned page can be empty while a later page still has results.
