# Opportunity recommendation policy

For You is optional and advisory. With permission, deterministic rules compare the member’s structured selections to approved public listing facts. Eligible results explain actual category, type, skill, language, location, mode, experience and compensation matches. Self-reported skills or experience remain self-reported. A published experience requirement counts toward matching only when the approved field is an unambiguous year value such as “2 years.” Other experience text is advisory. Objectives are stored privately for the member and have no automatic text interpretation.

A missing detail contributes no positive match. An informative reason can identify additional qualifications, unavailable or zero pay, differing currency or period, and disclosed amounts below the preferred minimum. A partial overlap can qualify as a recommendation. No preferences change eligibility to apply.

Candidate selection uses an indexed publication cohort and bounded keyset traversal. On relevance sort, positive explainable signals reorder the eligible results within the traversal page. Publication order and record ID break ties. The engine does not compare a member with all opportunities or return competence scores. A dismissal applies only to the current approved revision and expires after the configured interval.

Publication eligibility, organization verification, privacy and safety are mandatory. Founder preparation can narrow types, fields, criteria, search dimensions and limits. It cannot override consent or adjust mandatory rules.
