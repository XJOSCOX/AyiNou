# C4 final privacy review

| Data | Authorized use and lifecycle |
| --- | --- |
| Drafts, revisions, review grants, decisions and ownership evidence | Representatives and assigned independent staff under original scope. Keep exact decisions and challenges for review. Public listings exclude evidence and raw external destinations. |
| Expressions of interest and applicant consent | Exact application revision, deliberate snapshot and current recipient authorization. Withdrawal removes the shared identity and statement while preserving minimal history. |
| Professional preferences, imported skills, consent, saves and dismissals | Owner-only. Import is explicit and copies selected values into a separate record. Saving sends no notification. Withdrawal stops personalization and clears dismissals. Saved records use opaque placeholders when eligibility fails. |
| Mentor and mentee roles and adult confirmation | Purpose-specific and private. Participation and discovery choices remain independent for each role. |
| Requests, agreements, relationships, objectives, sessions and reported progress | Only the participants can inspect permitted current records. Safety restrictions suppress interaction and protected content. A block or release cannot recreate consent. |
| Safety reports, notifications, Founder receipts | Case-scoped staff and relevant recipients. Notices omit introduction and agreement text. Administrative receipts preserve the actor, workspace, reason, revision and affected stages. |

The synthetic lifecycle preview combines the established direct record inventory with a conservative shared-data review blocker. It exposes no partner names, IDs, applicant statements, private agreement terms, or destination evidence. Account erasure needs a third-party redaction policy and legal hold reconciliation. Audit records may require detached identity or preservation. Automated production export, erasure, storage cleanup and a final retention schedule remain separately authorized operations.

Recommendations compare only approved opportunity facts and participant selections. Donations, payments, status, popularity and protected personal characteristics are outside the ranking inputs. No skills, mentorship data or preference profiles are automatically transferred to employers.
