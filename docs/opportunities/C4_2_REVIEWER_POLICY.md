# C4.2. Independent reviewer policy

The existing verification reviewer directory and original-session workspace are prerequisites. A separate opportunity scope grant binds an explicitly appointed eligible reviewer to a Haitian department or an international country. Grants expire and can be revoked. The existing Founder workspace records appointment, release and reassignment with a reason, recent MFA, explicit confirmation and a receipt. Appointment and workflow administration confer no independent decision authority.

Each decision requires current verified identity and standing, recent MFA and a live case assignment. Existing institution conflict checks include historical creators, owners, representatives and affiliates. The opportunity boundary also checks prior author or applicant activity, reporting, recusal and the previous deciding reviewer for a challenge. Reviewers should recuse for material personal, employment or financial relationships that are outside stored platform evidence. The platform does not collect protected screening documents.

The checklist requires organization eligibility, accuracy, location, financial statements, application requirements, fraud checks, safeguarding and destination ownership and safety. Decisions retain reason and exact revision. Organizer-facing explanations must omit internal deliberations and confidential evidence. A second eligible independent reviewer decides an allowed challenge. The report restriction lane requires a current scoped grant and a report, and excludes prior decision-makers on that revision.
