# C4 public launch dependencies

**NO-GO for public launch.**

- Appoint and train independent reviewers by jurisdiction. Cover challenges, conflicts, recusal, response deadlines, handoffs and moderation escalation. Review capacity in each intended department or international scope.
- Establish destination ownership revalidation, impersonation detection, recruitment and scholarship fraud response, and participant reporting workflows.
- Approve applicant consent and access procedures, shared mentorship privacy, evidence holds, redaction, export, account deletion, retention and legal review.
- Approve adult eligibility and mentorship safety protocols, incident response, prohibited advice boundaries, and abuse escalation. Youth mentorship remains deferred.
- Exercise provider-backed restoration for data and private Storage. Reconcile eligibility, consent and revocation, formalize recovery targets and staff monitoring before activation.
- Provide an exact separately approved live release and independently open only ready stages. Confirm rollback and pause authority. A prepared configuration alone supplies no operational approval.

C4 does not authorize app hosting, paid placement, public mentorship records, employer candidate discovery or automated hiring. The revised C6 scope excludes integrated project payments and leaves financial custody and escrow indefinitely deferred.
