## Case types and authority

| Kind | Scope and outcome | Decision boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access | Locate eligible information and disclose a subject-specific view with approved exceptions and safe reasons. | Verified subject or approved agent sees a claimant projection. A scoped reviewer decides exceptions. |
| Correction | Route ordinary fields through current authorized editing. Review disputed managed facts separately. Use a versioned correction or annotation where provenance must remain. | Domain owner approves contested changes. Ballots, reviews, evidence and shared history have separate controls. |
| Portable export | Approve scope, generate package, recheck access at release and download, revoke or expire grant. | Worker has narrowly granted projections. Reviewer sees status and exclusions without package access. [Export design](DATA_EXPORT_SPECIFICATION.md) |
| Closure | End the ability to use an account under approved terms. Identify outstanding dependencies, retained categories and separate erasure status. | Verified subject initiates; independent review resolves continuity and confirms any recovery option. |
| Deletion | Review dependencies, approve the plan, execute in checkpoints and verify per store. | Separate approval and execution. General administrator access grants neither visibility into raw data nor authority to erase. [Deletion design](ACCOUNT_DELETION_ARCHITECTURE.md) |
| Restriction, exception or escalation | Limit handling, document retention exception, ask for review of an outcome or refer a preservation need. | Holds and rights appeals have separate authority and records. Existing app restrictions and appeals stay distinct. [Retention design](RETENTION_AND_HOLDS_DESIGN.md) |

Propose append-only `privacy_case_events` with old/new state, versions, actor/service category, structured reason and safe request ID. ## States, transitions and retries

`received → verification_required → accepted → reviewing → approved → processing → completed` is the ordinary flow. A direct verified member may cross the initial verification state atomically. `awaiting_information` returns to reviewing on a scoped reply. Review may end `declined` or `out_of_scope` with a safe written decision. A verified requester may withdraw before the operation-specific irreversible boundary. `blocked_by_dependency`, `held` and `failed_retryable` require a reason, assigned next action and review date. `partially_completed` distinguishes verified steps from pending stores and approved retained classes. A rights appeal opens a linked review case assigned to an independent reviewer and preserves the original decision. Record notices through a transactional outbox. Generic previews carry no evidence or access link.

A command names its action and expected version. In one database transaction lock the case, verify actor, assignment, subject, proof and applicable policy, then update the version and append audit/outbox events. Stale versions conflict. Bind idempotency to actor, operation, case, key and canonical payload digest. An identical replay returns its safe receipt; a different payload under the same key conflicts. Serialize duplicate creation by subject, kind and relevant scope without merging legally distinct requests. Rate-limit per actor and case and use a bounded guest bucket for approved untrusted intake. Async steps use limited leases, fencing versions and unique step keys. A transient dependency failure leaves a retryable record. Client polling never starts or repeats an operation.

## Identity, verification and permissions

- **Direct subject.** Reuse Fastify's validated token/session boundary. It checks issuer, audience, role, expiry and live Supabase session UUID. The privileged path requires AAL2 and a recent TOTP AMR within the existing 900-second check, with limited future-clock tolerance. Ordinary intake and reading one's own case use a current session and the applicable MFA policy. [Fastify verifier](../../apps/api/src/services/auth.ts) · [Session RPC and grants](../../supabase/migrations/20261120030000_phase2e_administration.sql)
- **Sensitive actions.** Require recent purpose-bound verification for export release/download, accepting an irreversible plan or editing special identity fields. Design a one-use challenge bound to subject, live session, case, operation, policy version and expiry, verified by the server from approved Auth proof. Recheck session and factor revocation at action time. A refreshed access token or MFA enrollment alone cannot assert recent proof. A reviewed alternative is needed when an appropriate factor is unavailable. Keep passwords and one-time codes out of rights endpoints and logs.
- **Loss of account access or restrictions.** Use a separate reviewed intake channel and generic acknowledgement that reveals no account presence or status. Prefer existing Auth recovery and minimal alternative evidence with trained review. Rights requests remain possible when ordinary product access is restricted; they do not lift the restriction. Ordinary support email never authorizes disclosure or deletion. If post-closure access is required, approve a limited authenticated receipt channel that exposes only the claimant's safe outcome and expires.
- **Agents.** Check the agent's identity and mandate, exact subject and allowed operations, expiry, revocation and independent confirmation where appropriate. Bind approvals to this scope and resolve conflicts. Keep proof references restricted. Unsupported or unverifiable credentials yield a generic response while the request is routed appropriately.
- **Staff.** Distinct capabilities for intake, assigned review, appeal, hold decisions and worker execution. Check current grants and assignment, conflict rules and recent MFA for privileged humans. Require secondary independent approval for high-risk identity exceptions or destructive action. Scope administrative lists to assigned queue and minimum metadata. Provide no export contents, ballot access or blanket override to a platform administrator.

## Proposed application boundary

Fastify owns explicit collection and per-case command routes under `/api/v1/me/privacy-requests`. A restricted `/api/v1/admin/privacy-requests` allows scoped review commands only. Protect action-bound proof and sensitive transitions behind narrow service-only functions so direct PostgREST cannot skip step-up. Require RLS and explicit direct table grant revocation in a non-exposed schema. For SECURITY DEFINER routines require safe explicit search paths, fully qualified names and exact caller roles. Caller-bound routines use authenticated identity; service wrappers accept only server-verified identity and recheck domain authority. Never trust an ID, context switch or eligibility field from the client.

Next.js can link Privacy Requests from Account Settings, with localized accessible forms, case status, review reasons and escalation.
