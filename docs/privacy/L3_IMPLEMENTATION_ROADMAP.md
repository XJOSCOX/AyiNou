## L3.2 Secure privacy request intake

The implementation supports synthetic authenticated submission, status, history and withdrawal. Real intake and reviewer commands remain closed. The detailed L3.1 proposal below preserves the original design scope and prerequisites for activating any additional workflow. [Implemented L3.2 scope and validation](L3_2_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md)

- **Model and candidate migration.** Private case, subject binding, state event, assignment, scoped assurance and idempotency tables. Add case-scoped reads/actions, restrictive grants, unique deduplication keys and transition CAS. Keep out-of-band recovery and proposed representative evidence in controlled design until their proof and handling are approved. Start with authenticated self-service intake and minimum metadata.
- **API and experience.** Proposed `POST /api/v1/me/privacy-requests`, `GET /api/v1/me/privacy-requests` and `GET /api/v1/me/privacy-requests/:id`. Add explicit confirm/withdraw and restricted reviewer assignment, triage and escalate routes. Show purpose, safe status, progress, correction to the request and next action, with accessible errors and redacted policy-versioned receipts. Never offer a working download or destruction control, or solicit raw identity documents in this phase.
- **Authorization and recovery.** Verify live subject session, fresh proof by purpose, scoped independent reviewer authority and current case version on each sensitive transition. Configure generic denials and actor/abuse limits. Audit previous/next state, opaque actor/subject references, decision reason code and policy version. Return an existing result for matching retries and reject conflicting replays. Use transaction-bound events and a minimal retry queue for notifications; silence a notification whose access changed.
- **Acceptance and dependencies.** Test concurrent intake, stale versions, session revocation, cross-account/role isolation, reviewer conflicts, duplicate requests, localized mobile/desktop status and unauthenticated enumeration. Use Haiti/US policy placeholders in synthetic testing. Before any real intake, approve deadlines, abuse/escalation runbooks, authorized review staffing, handling of lost access, case metadata and retention for that metadata. Four locales are Haitian Creole, French, English and Spanish. [Rights architecture](RIGHTS_WORKFLOW_ARCHITECTURE.md)

## L3.3 Personal data export

L3.3 implements a limited synthetic projection, encrypted temporary artifacts, same-session release and scoped artifact cleanup. Production disclosure rules, key custody, recovery, and activation remain pending. [Implemented L3.3 scope](L3_3_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md)

- **Model and candidate migration.** Add export runs, versioned allowlist projections, restricted artifact and download-grant records with scope, key version, expiry and cleanup state. Bind snapshots to authorized cases and make audit independent of object contents.
- **API and experience.** Proposed `POST /api/v1/me/privacy-requests/:id/export`, `GET /api/v1/me/privacy-exports/:id` and an authenticated download stream. Show securely available, expired, revoked and retryable states in all four locales. Provide structured data and a readable manifest in the saved language. Ordinary administrators handle safe status and escalation only, with separately scoped policy decisions where approved.
- **Authorization and recovery.** Recheck live proof and rights at generation and delivery; short-lived purpose/session-bound grant with bounded reuse; redact logs and avoid credentials in URLs. Audit only artifact status, policy scope and release metadata. Resume or replace failed runs with stable generation/manifest versions. Revoke access before cleanup and reconcile orphaned artifacts without browsing archive contents.
- **Acceptance and dependencies.** Test leakage, revoke races, expired artifacts, provider interruption, bounded dataset paging, manifest counts, encryption/key rotation and isolated cleanup. Synthetic read-only work can precede the general retention schedule once L3.3 is authorized. Live export waits for approved field projections, confidentiality and ballot decisions, proof thresholds, archive expiry/cleanup and production key and worker custody. Prohibit cross-account exports, unrestricted table dumps, public or emailed archives, and reviewer access to ballot choices. [Export specification](DATA_EXPORT_SPECIFICATION.md)

## L3.4 Account deletion and data lifecycle

L3.4 implements read-only dependency planning, a synthetic unapproved policy, scoped holds and reversible marker execution in disposable databases. Live erasure, approved retention enforcement and provider operations remain outside the implementation. [L3.4 implementation report](L3_4_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md)

- **Model and candidate migration.** Add versioned dependency plans, independently approved holds, item eligibility and lease/case journals, write fences and minimized erasure receipts. Use restricted service interfaces for verified actions. Design separately reviewed additive reference/tombstone migrations for actual conflicts and an external restore ledger.
- **API and experience.** Proposed closure/deletion preview, confirm and cancel operations scoped to the case, and separate authorized plan/reconcile/hold endpoints. Expose pending, blocked, fenced, completed and incomplete states, safe dependency explanations, and completion receipts in all four locales. Secure post-closure communication needs approval and operational testing.
- **Authorization and recovery.** Bind consent and reviewer approval to version, proof, authority and resource generations. Independent roles place/release holds, authorize execution and reconcile failures. Audit dispositions, effective legal rule, actor, holds and provider acknowledgements. Require storage and rewrite fences, exact dispatch intent IDs, verified outcomes and failure recovery, with Auth removal last. Prohibit blanket cascade, destructive action from ordinary support, silent orphaning, scope expansion and unfenced cleanup.
- **Acceptance and dependencies.** Test last-admin continuity, multi-owner/transfer constraints, other-member data, voting, evidence/appeal holds, storage scope, duplicate jobs, provider timeouts and quarantine restore. Synthetic planning and dependency tests can run with candidate rules. Real erasure and retention automation require approved schedules, exceptions and preservation authority, verified provider operations, independent staffing, support readiness and tested rollback/reconciliation and restore release. Restricted operations use narrowly scoped reconciliation, audit and health views. No customer-facing operational controls or sensitive log content. Use disposable environments and synthetic fixtures. Any hosted operation needs separate authorization. ## L3.1 baseline validation

The baseline was `235138dc6eb3c34da8c6f6d71fbb3c307c797392` on `main`. A disposable local Supabase instance replayed the 56 existing migrations. The read-only application-schema catalog returned 241 foreign key constraints across `public`, `private` and `feed_data`, including 118 direct links to `auth.users` (46 CASCADE and 72 SET NULL). It also returned 96 relations with RLS enabled and 209 application-schema SECURITY DEFINER functions. Selected grants and search paths were inspected, including separate authenticated and service-only paths. `storage.objects` has owner metadata, a foreign key to its bucket, and no foreign key to `auth.users`. The three application bucket policies deny ordinary direct reads. The disposable stack was removed after inspection.

The schema and application review covered session and MFA enforcement, direct RPC authorization, organization roles and institution succession, community ownership, moderation and verification appeals, project ownership and shared image references, private ballot authorization, member settings and the isolated restore design. Documentation and policy review also identified the prior inventory's project-image storage mismatch. The catalog reflects migration replay, with no new hosted catalog query, hosted schema change, provider erasure, destructive fixture test or live workflow in this phase.
