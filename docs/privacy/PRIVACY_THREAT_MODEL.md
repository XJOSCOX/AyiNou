# Privacy rights threat model

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR IMPLEMENTATION OR PUBLICATION**

Protect subject identity, request substance, archives, third-party evidence, authority decisions, ballots and restore records. Treat the Next.js client, browser state, inbound requests, external provider responses and restored environments as separate trust boundaries. The proposed Fastify and database layers enforce subject, purpose and role at each boundary. Use a separate execution identity for workers, and pin approved actions to a case version. Even a signed-in staff member with a general administration role needs explicit scope and action authorization.

## Threats and acceptance tests

Run these future tests with synthetic accounts and resources in a disposable environment. Assert response denial or safe recovery and independently inspect database, audit and object state.

| Threat | Proposed control | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account takeover before export | Require live session and recent purpose-specific verification at approval and download; bind grant to verified subject and session; revoke on restriction, recovery or factor/session change. | A password-only session and a revoked previously approved session cannot create or use a grant. A grant from a second session fails. |
| Malicious deletion request | Require explicit action approval, fresh step-up, independent execution authorization, versioned dependency checks and a pre-execution cancellation boundary. | Attempt the destructive transition with stale proof, changed scope, requester-as-approver or a hold; verify no deletion or provider call. |
| Fraudulent representative | Independently validate scoped, time-bound delegation through a reviewed secure channel; record withdrawal and review different request types separately. | An expired, revoked or export-only authority cannot progress another request. An unsupported delegate gets no subject details. |
| Account enumeration | Keep recovery and agency intake responses generic, limit by verified actor as applicable and by abuse signals, minimize telemetry, and obscure your own case identifiers from others. | Known and unknown identifiers and inaccessible case IDs receive equivalent public shapes and throttling behavior. Check logs and timings for account-dependent disclosure. |
| Export-link theft | Stream via approved authenticated proxy with short artifact/grant expiry, live policy/version checks and no shared caching. Store encryption keys independently and scope the worker decrypt permission. | Copy a locator to another browser, try revoked and expired combinations, and inspect headers, redirects and logs for replay, session bypass or leaked tokens. |
| Third-party data disclosed by export | Define per-field export projections and provenance. Exclude unrestricted free-text evidence, private recipient identities and relationship graphs by default. | Seed posts, messages in review records, referrals, reactions and nominations involving two people. Check archive data, metadata and attachments for restricted content. |
| Unauthorized access to review evidence | Separate case summary permissions from evidence access; require assigned scope, verification and independence for restricted material. Place minimum metadata in audit and notifications. | Test direct database/API access for an unassigned or conflicted reviewer, recycled assignment, subject or support operator. Verify signed references and audit details do not leak. |
| Deletion races with account activity | Fence writes, upload claims and sensitive changes across Fastify, RPCs and workers before plan execution; serialize version, holds and cancellation. | Run new posts, invitations, media, custody changes, hold placement and cancellation alongside a planned step. Check the fence or a blocked versioned plan and preserve newly protected material. |
| Duplicate workers or partial provider failure | Lease steps, version resource generations and use stable idempotency keys. Track dispatch intent and reconcile provider outcomes before replay. | Simulate duplicate claims, lease expiry and a timeout after external success. Verify a single effective outcome and retained case continuity. |
| Deletion removes someone else's media | Use a server-validated relationship and exact bucket-and-key manifest, with fresh hold/ownership checks and separate paths for project database bytes. | Mix identical filenames and digests across members/projects, another institution's logo, historical references and both post variants. Confirm only eligible targeted objects disappear. |
| Private ballot reconstruction | Never give reviewers or administrators individual choices. Separate eligibility and vote linkage from aggregate readers; check small cohorts, repeated snapshots, provenance and own receipt scope independently. | Inspect exports, admin and API reads, logs and aggregate comparisons for single-voter or timing disclosure. Verify baseline and after-erasure finalized aggregates, frozen quorum and re-vote prevention. |
| Records reappear after restore | Quarantine Auth, application database and Storage until protected ledger replay, hold reattachment, suppression and independent sign-off. | Restore a snapshot that predates deletion with altered or missing ledger cases. Require successful replay and checks for the valid copy. Reject release for the invalid copies. |

## Baseline findings and gates

- The effective schema has both cascades and conditional deletion guards. Blanket Auth removal affects other people's content and can erase case evidence or destroy owned projects. The future execution engine must prove minimum impact with relation-specific tests.
- Bucket metadata has no Auth ownership foreign key. Restrictive bucket policies protect direct access, but service-side disposition needs association checks and exact variants. Project imagery has independent database references and must be checked separately.
- A ballot's surviving choice and deleted identity can coexist. Frozen electorates, small cohorts and prior read snapshots create inference risks regardless of nominal anonymity. The export and reviewer projections must remain independently restricted.
- Configurable legal rules, external delivery, counsel-approved evidence decisions and safe provider restore remain operational gates. Fail closed until prerequisites for the relevant operation are verified.
