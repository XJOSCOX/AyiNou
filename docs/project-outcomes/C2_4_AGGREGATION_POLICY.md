# C2.4 aggregation policy

## Population and indicators

The denominator for completed projects, reviewed coverage and evidence-supported coverage is each distinct currently public, eligible Haitian project that has a current project execution and a native recorded completion date in the chosen calendar year. Only one unambiguous department may resolve from the approved project geography. An archived project contributes only if a completion was recorded. Each result belongs to one sector. Education, healthcare, agriculture, environment and infrastructure retain their native categories. Entrepreneurship, employment and technology belong to economic development. Peace and culture belong to community development. Projects outside these constraints stay outside reporting.

- **Completed public projects** counts the distinct project IDs in that population.
- **Projects with a reviewed result** counts population projects with at least one current published C2.3 disclosure linked to a finding of any class.
- **Projects with an evidence-supported result** counts population projects with a current published supported or partially supported finding. This denominator is also the completed-project population.
- **Participating departments** counts the configured departments represented in the eligible completed-project population. It is national only. A national figure covers the configured departments that satisfy the release policy.
- **Reviewed needs linked to projects** counts distinct reviewed C1 publications with a current approved association to an eligible completed project in the same department and separate valid consent from the need’s author. The denominator is those reviewed publications across the selected project population. The public aggregate carries neither association nor need pointers.
- **Standardized measurements** sum the exact C2.2 independently supported quantities from approved mappings whose C2.3 public evidence and consent remain current. The denominator is the qualifying project observations for that definition, calendar year and geography. A reviewed zero can contribute zero. Unsupported and qualitative results do not contribute a quantity.

## Deduplication and integrity

Use at most one competing observation per project, definition and calendar year. When several eligible observations share that allocation or the same source, all competing observations are excluded. A confirmed delivery reference is evaluated across projects for the same definition. Coincident accepted delivery hashes exclude all competing contributions across projects and years. The ledger retains an earlier decision when an organizer submits a correction. Only a current approved mapping tied to the exact native source ID and revision, finding ID, permitted C2.3 disclosure revision and digest is eligible.

The reference is a non-personal delivery record checked independently. Partners must identify the same real delivery consistently. Similar text, separate reported totals and separate organization ownership provide no evidence of independence. Undetermined identity or overlap calls for exclusion. No person-level identifiers are collected for deduplication. Figures count documented outputs and coverage. Unique beneficiary counts are unavailable.

## Approval and refresh

Each public release has one fixed sector, a completed calendar year and a reviewed cohort. A published or suppressed cohort cannot be reissued under another year or sector with the same projects. Each public read derives the eligible inputs and validates their exact digest. A change withholds the affected release. The original snapshot and reasons remain private. Combined national figures sum a partition of independent department statistics under the disclosure rules.
