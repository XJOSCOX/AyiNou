# C2 final privacy review

| Record category | Purpose and access | Withdrawal, export, erasure and hold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C2.1 requests, source fingerprints and receipt history | Organizer workflow and explicitly authorized case review. Native sources retain their existing lifecycle. | Withdrawal closes the pathway. Export should separate the subject’s material from third-party evidence. Erasure and holds must retain narrowly justified receipts according to approved policy. |
| C2.2 case revisions, release manifests, grants, findings, conflict and challenge records | Restricted evidence, decision and appeal accountability. Reviewer access is case-, assignment-, version- and session-bound. | Revoke media and manifest access immediately at the workflow boundary. Preserve authorized decision history. Define evidence dependency, retention and hold behavior before enabling real processing. |
| C2.3 drafts, consent, disclosures, recusal and review receipts | Private disclosure governance. A current snapshot only reaches public Project Progress after independent approval. | Withdrawal or upstream invalidation suppresses the public snapshot. Archive private approvals within the eventual review and hold policy. |
| C2.4 definitions, measurement mappings, need consent, releases and receipts | Restricted governance and statistical assurance. Public uses fixed safe projections. | Invalidation permanently withholds affected seals. Delivery references remain restricted. Subject export, erasure scope and restoration after erasure require a policy before launch. |
| Browser, logs and metadata | Localized workflow and operational diagnosis. Protected API responses use no-store and public projections omit private narratives and identifiers. | Browser content retention is bounded for reports. Logs must exclude raw private artifacts. Provider retention remains to be set. |

The generic privacy lifecycle and export regression cases passed locally. No new real export, erasure, processing or legal period is activated by C2.5. Account deletion and eventual review receipts need a category-by-category retention and hold decision. The existing auth foreign keys and source relations are catalog mechanisms. They are insufficient grounds to conclude subject erasure is complete.

## Inference limits

The five-owner floor reduces narrow disclosure. Complementary suppression and fixed, non-overlapping slices reduce subtraction opportunities. Public project links use eligible independently public projects and do not mark a specific input as evidence-supported. Exact statistics can still reveal information when outside knowledge identifies nearly all participants or a dominant quantity. Thresholds and governance are not differential privacy or a guarantee of anonymity. Separate review of outliers, dominance, public link policy, metadata and disclosure monitoring is required before enabling real reports.
