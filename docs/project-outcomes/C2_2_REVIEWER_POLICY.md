# C2.2 Reviewer policy

## Authority

- A recognized Founder with a current authenticated workspace and recent MFA appoints, suspends, revokes, or renews a verified member. Initial review, challenge review, and administrator coordination are separate grants for individual Haitian departments. Each grant expires, and a changed grant version immediately invalidates the assignments pinned to its previous version.
- An administrator may coordinate only in a department for which the Founder explicitly granted assignment authority. Neither a Founder nor an administrator may decide on the basis of rank. All decision makers require their own eligible assignment and recent MFA in its original session.
- The coordinator can assign one independent member at a time, release the assignment, and replace an expired or invalid assignment. A coordinator cannot assign themselves. The case owner can coordinate under existing authority but must select an independent person. Reviewers accept with an explicit conflict declaration and can recuse using a restricted reason.

## Independence

Project creation, submission, direct ownership, representation, organization relationships, native internal reviewers and source contributors, project team participation including revoked memberships, project proposal decision participation, linked C1 case participants and explicit conflicts disqualify a reviewer. Former participation is conservatively disqualifying when the project record shows it. The reviewer must disclose additional financial or personal relationships through recusal. The original reviewer or another substantive participant cannot decide a challenge or the reassessment. The same restrictions apply to Founder-owned projects.

## Sources and release

Each case belongs to the single Haitian department supported by the current approved proposal and any native department associations. With conflicting, unspecified or international geography, no scoped case is opened. C1 links do not establish scope or unlock C1 evidence.

The project organizer explicitly releases the exact manifest for the submitted revision. The assigned reviewer must separately accept and inspect it. Evidence URLs are recorded pointers. Project images are streamed only after a new authorization check. The app requests no external content. Advancing the review revision or revoking the release invalidates the old evidence authorization. Historical findings continue to identify the original manifest and release.

## Findings and lifecycle

Supported, partially supported, insufficient evidence and not supported are separate judgments. Supporting or partially supporting requires released evidence. Numeric findings are confined to an existing metric observation with the same unit and period, an independently described method and a known quantity constrained by the reported value. A qualitative result has no numeric requirement. Zero remains a legitimate measurement. A finding is historical when its source or release loses applicability.

The review holds while project work is paused. Completed projects and projects archived after completion are eligible. Restricted projects or revoked organizer rights block private access. Source withdrawals or supersession block current decisions. A changed native source requires a fresh C2.1 request. A clarification or material correction to the evidence of the same native result creates a new C2.2 review revision and retains the prior revisions. The original C2.1 withdrawal path closes assignment and evidence access.

A requester can challenge one adverse initial finding within 14 days of its availability. A separately authorized challenge reviewer can uphold, revise, or return it. Reassessment must be performed by another independent initial reviewer. Audit records retain each challenge decision. A reassessed finding cannot be challenged again in C2.2. Neither a coordinator nor a Founder can reopen or override a finding. Independent evidence review must stay disabled until separately activated.
