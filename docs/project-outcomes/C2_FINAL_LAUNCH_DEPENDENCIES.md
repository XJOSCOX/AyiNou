# C2 final launch dependencies

## Staffing and independent review

Provide explicit departmental outcome, challenge, publication and mapping reviewers plus alternates in each of Haiti’s ten departments. Assignment coordinators triage workload and replace recused or expired reviewers. Governance reviewers independently examine catalog definitions and reporting cohorts, with enough coverage to avoid assigning people who participated in a project or its evidence, association or disclosure decisions. Cross-department staff require an appropriate grant. Define escalation contacts and expected handling time, leave coverage and queue pause rules before pilot activation.

## Operational gates

Require approved retention and legal holds, export and erasure handling, documented incident response, monitoring for withheld reports, service timeouts and expired reviewer grants, provider recovery and restricted-media restoration, and privacy review for external-knowledge and dominance scenarios. Exercise recovery and operational tools on designated non-production infrastructure. For an initial controlled launch, agree population limits, reviewer capacity, safe reporting periods and alert thresholds. The Founder can govern configuration using existing role and MFA checks. Live activation and hosting require separate authorization.

Absence of live staffing alone does not prevent development closure. No real reviewer appointments, intake, evidence review, publication, Impact reporting, hosted web/API deployment or C3 work is included in this audit.
