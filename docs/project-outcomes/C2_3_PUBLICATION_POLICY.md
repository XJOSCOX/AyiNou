# C2.3 controlled publication policy

A currently eligible public project may publish a current independent C2.2 finding after the applicable challenge period and while the exact native source, review revision and evidence release remain valid. A project organizer supplies privacy-safe text, previews the exact public disclosure, and consents to the versioned snapshot.

A coordinator assigns a separately appointed departmental publication reviewer. Verified identity, recent MFA, an original-session workspace, declared independence and exact preview are required. The organizer, native project participants, substantive evidence participants, and recused reviewers are excluded where applicable. Reviewers approve, request changes or reject with a recorded reason. A rejected request may be challenged once within 14 days. A different eligible reviewer handles the challenge.

The public result is limited to the approved title, summary, limitations, classifications, period, reviewed and publication dates, compatible approved quantities and a safe correction notice. Project Progress labels independent assurance at the result level. Authoritative findings and evidence remain in the existing review system. Changing the disclosure requires renewed consent and approval. Whole withdrawal, lost eligibility and source invalidation remove the disclosure. The private ledger keeps restricted decisions and immutable snapshots under the applicable retention rules.

The C2.3 hosted migration established the schema with all C2 fixture gates empty. See the [verified rollout record](C2_3_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md).
