# O1 owner decision brief

The owner proposes one personal account with two entry paths, personal verification before completing ordinary organization setup, optional organization verification for ordinary use, distinct Founder Verified provenance, and a tightly scoped Early Partner pilot. O1 records the design. Current enforcement remains the baseline described below.

| Decision needed before implementation | Recommended direction | Current technical enforcement or open issue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signup eligibility and future pilot | Keep one shared eligibility authority for both paths. Decide when invitation-only and 18+ gates are activated and what minimal proof to accept. | Current signup and referral attribution do not enforce the proposed pilot. An Auth metadata hint supplies no authorization. |
| Founder Verified | Approve proof ceremony, consent, conflicts, neutral appeal route, retention and amber seal copy. Limit parity to ordinary organization setup. | Existing bootstrap is one-time and excluded from independent review. Current independent approval needs two distinct eligible assigned reviewers. |
| Legacy organization transition | Preserve existing scoped relationships; require new completion and transfer actions to satisfy the new gate. Decide how to handle active disputes and unclaimed duplicates. | Current institution creation immediately grants ownership; old active rows cannot be assumed to have completed new review. |
| Registration and government | Approve classification, which claims may appear publicly, authoritative registries and official mandate rules. Keep unconfirmed charity recognition private. | Existing institution type and verification fields encode neither jurisdictional registration nor a public claim-review outcome for each legal attribute. |
| New ownership and claims | Approve minimum proof for newly established ordinary institutions. Leave sensitive claims pending when neutral reviewers are unavailable. | Current claim approval can revoke existing memberships and invites. A label alone cannot establish legal authority. |
| Governance and succession | Name recovery custodians and eligible successor process, approve dual-control triggers and emergency deadlines. | Current role/MFA checks and last-admin protection exist. Admin count does not provide an approved governance transition. |
| Reporting and visibility | Approve adjudication, evidence retention and rules for showing substantiated restrictions. | Existing impersonation decisions and appeals provide a base; popularity grants no authority. |
| Early Partner program | Approve selection criteria, terms, support, pilot entitlements, opt-in directory and exit procedures. | Current L6.2A policies exclude the Early Partner policy. Public legal registries are empty and real privacy processing is disabled. |

No policy or legal status is inferred from an example organization. Organizational and personal verification are separate from eligibility to publish projects or cast ballots. The [capability matrix](O1_CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md) records the explicit boundaries, and the [roadmap](O1_IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md) lists decisions by dependency.
