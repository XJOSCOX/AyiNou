# O1 Early Partner Program design

Early Partner is a proposed organization pilot enrollment. Offer guided setup, feedback sessions, feature-scoped early access, support and optional limited visibility. The label conveys program participation only. It carries no identity or organization verification, project approval, fundraising authority, extra votes, or access to private member, health or beneficiary data.

## Enrollment and operations

1. An approved program operator nominates an already established eligible organization using documented pilot criteria and a structured reason. Review conflicts, safety and capacity. Verify the accepting actor’s existing authority and disallow public-government scenarios unless separately approved.
2. Provide scope, allowed features, start/end, support contacts, exit and visibility terms. A live authorized owner accepts the exact approved program version. Public listing is separate and opt-in, subject to current visibility eligibility. No signup acceptance change.
3. Recheck enrollment and organization access on every feature action. Grants expire or suspend with participation and scope. When an organization leaves, stop access, revoke grants, preserve auditable receipts and offer safe feedback or an appeal. Review flagged misuse separately from verification.
4. Provide separate invitations for each authorized person through existing institutional invitation and role flows. Use test or fictional examples for feedback tasks.

Propose private `early_partner_enrollments` with organization FK, program version, status `nominated → offered → active → ended` with `declined`, `withdrawn` or `suspended`, scope, participation window, accepting actor, version, visibility consent and event history. One live offer/enrollment per organization and program version. Feature entitlements are enumerated and expire with the program. Lock enrollment and organization scope on acceptance/revocation and audit actor, approval version, reason and timestamps. Proposed endpoints under `/api/v1`: admin partner nomination and scoped transitions, member organization enrollment read/accept/leave and a safe opted-in directory only after policy approval. Apply the same RLS and RPC conventions as [onboarding](O1_ONBOARDING_ARCHITECTURE.md).

Screen copy: “Early Partner pilot”, “Review pilot participation”, “Your organization is participating in a limited pilot”, and “Choose whether to appear in the pilot directory.” Keep distinct localized text and visuals from the Founding Ambassador workflow. Phase 5C already separates nomination, acceptance and consent and requires independently reviewed personal identity for public ambassador recognition. Reuse those interaction patterns without reusing its designation or case tables.

**Anonymized example.** A Haitian family-support group with six staff chooses the organization path. Its authorized contact uses their own account, completes the personal identity gate and sets up the organization with the appropriate claimed registration status. Each of the six staff uses their own account and accepts only a scoped relationship. The owner may accept an eligible pilot offer for onboarding feedback and test volunteer scenarios. Its legal status requires evidence, and any later organization verification follows the independent route. Pilot enrollment confers no access to beneficiary records.

## Compatibility with later programs

Use organization IDs and scoped roles as references when designing programs, configurable applications, beneficiary waitlists, evaluations, volunteer coordination, provider directories and authorized portals, education/counseling support, referrals and funding/impact reports. Use separate domains, minimized projections, individual consent and purpose-specific access. Require approved data handling and provider governance before confidential evaluations or medical referrals. Keep onboarding fields limited to account setup, organization identity and mandate. Intake for health or financial assistance requires separate authorization and data governance. The existing [L6.2A legal controls](../legal/L6_2A_ADMIN_PUBLICATION_CONTROLS.md) exclude the Early Partner policy from publication settings. Program launch depends on independent legal, policy and pilot approval.


## O6 Early Partners

O6 implements scoped nomination, independent offer review, synthetic owner acceptance, expiry, withdrawal, suspension and private feedback. Real enrollment and public visibility remain closed. A future verified terms release must specify consent, eligibility, termination, appeal, support, data protection and optional public wording. Connect its immutable approved version and digest to participation only after publication authority and readiness are approved. The suggested public label is “Early Partner · Participating in Ayinou’s development pilot.” A future projection must require current eligibility, active participation, explicit opt-in and the institution’s existing visibility checks. See [O6 implementation](O6_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md).
