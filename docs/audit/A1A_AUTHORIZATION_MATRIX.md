# A1A authorization matrix

Every page and detail verifies the provider session in Fastify and checks live account eligibility, S2A and S2B session policy in PostgreSQL. Platform and verification routes also read the correct S2C workspace in PostgreSQL, binding its membership, step, account, original session, expiry and recent TOTP. The audit RPC is service-only. The private source view has no direct application grants, including service access.

| Scope | Effective reader | Native adapters | Explicit exclusions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Platform | Active Administrator with an open admin workspace and recent TOTP. Recognized Founder qualifies through trusted provenance. | `platform_admin` from `private.platform_admin_audit`, plus general-feed `post_moderation` and `comment_moderation`. | Ordinary Administrator excludes Founder bootstrap and governance adapter. No private case reasons or reviewer grants. |
| Founder additions | The same admin workspace and the active trusted Founder predicate. | `governance` from O5 proposal events, including F1.1 authorization and activation when recorded. | Claim holds, corrections, recovery, authority configurations, F1 credentials and all raw audit payloads. |
| Personal | Verified own session. | `session_policy` from S2A, `session_security` from S2B, `username` from U1 assignment and changes. | Other members, service credentials, privileged workflow locators, provider session IDs and MFA time. |
| Organization | Current foundation owner or organization administrator of the exact active organization. | `organization_role` from the existing Phase 5D history. | Cross-tenant data, custom assignments without foundation authority, roster identities and raw state. |
| Verification | Active independent reviewer with a recent-MFA review workspace, current work assignment and no conflict for the exact case. | `verification` from ordinary review decisions. | Unassigned cases, rationale, case evidence, applicant details, other reviewers’ IDs and Founder Verified cases. |
| General feed moderation | Live platform leadership or current platform Moderator, with recent TOTP. Moderator route requires a single post or comment. | General-feed `post_moderation` and `comment_moderation` only. | Department and group actions, report content, appeal records, post bodies and moderator identities in both audit scopes. |

The platform actor filter operates only over rows already authorized for the viewer. The API performs no account lookup for it, and authorized resource filters match against the authorized projection. Other scopes reject actor filters and don't substitute another subject. Scoped results hide another person’s actor reference. Verified system identities are absent from the exposed sources unless a native event explicitly records that provenance. Raw ballots, medical data, confidential documents and all legal and privacy evidence stay outside the projection. See the [source inventory](A1A_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md).
