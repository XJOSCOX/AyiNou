# Founder authority and powers

The platform recognizes one Founder from the committed first-account bootstrap singleton and its matching security audit event. The server compares the authenticated account UUID with that provenance. A name, email, verification badge, ordinary role assignment or profile setting has no part in the decision. The eligible founding account becomes Founder after the one-time reserved identity enrollment commits the first-account bootstrap. The setup validates the configured email, confirmed account ownership, recent MFA and a private credential. [Enrollment and badge design](FOUNDER_BOOTSTRAP_AND_BADGES.md). The roles migration recognizes that trusted record. A future governed transfer would carry the new Founder’s routine powers and leadership mark automatically after issuance of new trusted provenance. Verification badges continue through their own proof and review rules. A missing or orphaned provenance receipt leaves the role vacant. Ordinary administration cannot assign, discard or transfer it.

An eligible Founder has the routine Administrator capabilities without needing a separate Administrator assignment, including platform account management, moderation through current safety checks and initiation of eligible O5 governance proposals. The Founder authenticates through normal Supabase sessions, uses MFA where the operation requires it, and remains subject to account restrictions. An inactive Founder retains historical designation and loses its operational privileges. Protected status changes retain O5 controls. Credential and account containment can interrupt access while a separately governed recovery is evaluated. There is no automatic alternative login or succession.

Founder Verified issuance additionally requires formal Founder provenance, an active scoped O3 assignment, recent MFA, live case state, conflict clearance, member consent and current proof. Scoped administrators can open consent requests, record eligible case decisions and perform permitted safety containment. The issue and release commands require the formal Founder. The Founder needs separate ordinary reviewer eligibility and work assignment for any regular identity vote. Neither role can act as two independent reviewers, execute protected governance unilaterally, assume an organization's ownership, read private ballots or bypass the legal and privacy approval paths.

## O5 compatibility proposal

Reserve the unused Super Administrator concept. The owner named Founder as the only top-level platform designation. Keep the O5 bootstrap, distributed and recovery model, scoped authority tables, proposals, approvals, acceptance, execution gates and historical values. The hosted baseline has zero O5 governance authority grants. Retiring a legacy authority code, if needed, requires an inventory of references, a revisioned compatibility plan, a preservation review and separate deployment approval. This change leaves those O5 records and execution protections in place.

## Deployment and continuity

The migrations are exercised on disposable Supabase. Hosted schema changes require separate authorization. Hosted Founder issuance and O4 Founder parity remain gated. Approved real-world proof, a neutral appeals process and successor recovery policy are separate work.
