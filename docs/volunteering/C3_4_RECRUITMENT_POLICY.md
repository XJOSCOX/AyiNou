# C3.4 Recruitment policy

An invitation offers a chance to apply to one native volunteer opportunity. An organizer selects a result from their authorized discovery page, reviews the exact published requirements, and sends up to 400 characters of plain text. The server rejects arbitrary handles, changed published versions, incompatible or screened opportunities and unavailable recipients. A fresh eligibility check includes native recruitment permission, verified organizer identity, account standing, blocks and mutes, profile visibility, current availability, project scope, compatible participation, current catalog and separate contact and discovery opt-ins. All ten department codes and eligible remote participants are supported without deriving eligibility from nationality or residence.

Pending invitations may become application initiated, declined, withdrawn, expired or invalidated. The recipient can hide the invitation. Hidden and terminal invitations do not disclose the original invitation content. Declining or hiding has no account or matching consequences. The platform records attribution only for a new native application with a current, visible invitation. Automated status transitions never create an application, offer, membership or task. Restored eligibility cannot reopen a recorded invalidation.

| Operational default | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Seven-day expiry, capped by the opportunity deadline | Keeps outreach current and limits outstanding invitations |
| Five invitations per organizer in 24 hours | Makes individual outreach the normal workflow |
| Eight invitations per opportunity in 24 hours | Prevents a shared opportunity from bypassing organizer limits |
| Two invitations per recipient in 24 hours | Limits repeated contact across projects |
| Fourteen-day cooldown for repeat contact | Gives people time after declining or leaving an invitation unanswered |
| Fifty outstanding per project, five per recipient, one per opportunity and recipient | Caps simultaneously pending records |

Founder configuration can narrow supported departments and modes and adjust bounded thresholds. Native report categories, independent moderation permissions, blocking and current session protections apply. One report per reporter and project is preserved by the existing moderation queue.
