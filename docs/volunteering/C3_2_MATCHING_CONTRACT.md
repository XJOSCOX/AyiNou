# Future matching contract

C3.2 exports the typed `OpportunityRequirementRevision` and `OpportunityRequirementsView` contract. The latter is closed in hosted environments. In the disposable state, each opportunity is keyed by its existing opportunity ID plus the containing project ID in the request. Its exact publication version, approved project revision, authority-derived department, native capacity and deadline, mode, weekly hours and duration, beginner and screening flags, skill IDs and definition revisions, priorities, levels, ranges and language requirements are bounded. Per-opportunity eligibility metadata is advisory, with no authority over native applications. It does not consume volunteer preferences or data. Historical views may contain criteria that no longer qualify for future use.

A later approved matcher must re-read the exact current requirements and catalog, confirm the published version and approved scope still match, check both account standings and applicable restrictions, and recheck native opportunity visibility, project/community eligibility, opportunity status, deadline, remaining places, supported safeguards and regulated exclusions. An independent profile path must also check current purpose-specific consent, profile privacy, bilingual or localized proficiency semantics, member availability, expiry and blocking in both directions. It may not infer qualifications, rank identity holders above others, automatically reject an application or bypass the human decisions in Project Work. Revocation must invalidate downstream availability. There is no mismatch penalty and no C3.2 profile join, organizer search, recommendation or invitation automation.
