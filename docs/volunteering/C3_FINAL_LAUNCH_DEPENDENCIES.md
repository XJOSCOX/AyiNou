# C3 final launch dependencies

**Public launch decision** NO-GO.

- The privacy and legal owners must approve collection notices, minimization, hold-aware retention, export and erasure procedures.
- The safeguarding owner must approve adult eligibility, organizer screening responsibilities, excluded sensitive and regulated work, safety notices, and incident escalation.
- Operations must assign moderation and escalation coverage, configure monitoring and recruitment abuse thresholds, rehearse provider-backed backup and recovery, and verify protection for deployments with denied or stale sessions.
- Release owners must approve a separately scoped real readiness mechanism and rollout, followed by actual load and resource measurements, concurrent abuse testing and a production launch decision.

Public skills disclosure is deferred to its own consent, privacy and launch review. Youth recruitment, regulated placement authorization and professional credential certification remain outside this implementation.
