# C3 final activation policy

| Stage | Prerequisites | Effect of pause |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intake | Valid volunteer catalog and configuration, privacy, safeguarding and operational readiness | Stops new profiles and consent additions. Preserves private reads and withdrawal. Pauses matching, discovery and recruitment. |
| Requirements | Valid catalog and configuration, native project authorization | Stops drafting and publication while preserving authorized frozen history. Pauses matching, discovery and recruitment. |
| Personalized matching | Intake and structured requirements | Stops recommendations while preserving native work and personal pause. |
| Organizer discovery | Intake, requirements, profile privacy and separate purpose-specific consent | Stops organizer discovery and pauses recruitment. Personalized matching is independent. |
| Recruitment | Discovery, separate contact consent and native eligible opportunities | Stops outreach and invalidates pending invitations. Existing native records, historical access and reports are retained. |

Paused dependent capabilities need individual resumption. Saving shared configuration may pause affected capabilities and never enables them. Emergency pause is one audited decision. Protected decisions require current account and session, verified Founder authority, recent MFA, original workspace, configuration digest, expected version, reason, explicit confirmation and a durable idempotency receipt. Only a disposable environment can satisfy the operational readiness release gate in this phase. Real authorization remains a separate deployment decision.
