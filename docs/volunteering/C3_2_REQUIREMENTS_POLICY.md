# C3.2 Opportunity requirements policy

An opportunity is unpaid volunteer participation. Organizers may record relevant required and preferred skills, multilingual preferences, minimum self-reported skill levels, experience ranges, hours per week, weeks of participation and a compatible mode. A beginner-friendly opportunity cannot make intermediate or advanced skill proficiency, or positive years of experience, mandatory. Native available positions and deadlines remain authoritative. A structured skill is never an applicant score, a reason for automatic rejection or a replacement for an organizer's human decision. Existing applications need no volunteer profile or structured skill.

A screening flag explains that an organizer is responsible for lawful qualifications and safeguarding before someone participates. It collects no credential documents and opens no regulated, youth or sensitive placement pathway. Identity verification does not establish professional competence. The feature accepts no demographic eligibility criteria and grants no work, travel or community authorization. Real launch requires a safeguarding, age eligibility, lawful role-screening and privacy lifecycle review.

Requirements are optional and can be prepared after the existing opportunity is created. The organizer saves a private draft and reviews it alongside the native opportunity terms before publication. A separate explicit confirmation publishes the exact reviewed snapshot. The first native application freezes structured requirement changes forever on that opportunity, including when that application is later withdrawn. Existing accepted work and decisions remain valid under native Project Work rules. To use materially different requirements after that point, close the opportunity through Project Work and publish a new opportunity through the native flow. No existing applications are moved.

Administrative category, field, mode and size changes only affect future drafts and publication checks. Historical snapshots keep their skill code, revision and four labels without silent substitution. The existing profile privacy model and moderation controls retain their authority. Opportunity snapshots are linked to native lifecycle handling, and any legal hold must preserve both the original opportunity and its required revisions. The hosted fixture stays empty.
