# C3.1 Volunteer privacy and lifecycle

Private owner data comprises the member identifier, selected skill identifiers, self-reported competence and optional years, languages, preferences, availability and distinct consent choices. The latest consent record retains its timestamp. The owner receipt stores one request fingerprint and version for safe retries. Replacing the profile replaces previous selections rather than collecting a history of private summaries and schedules. The form requests no credentials, documents, employer evidence, contact fields, residential address or travel eligibility. Members should leave private contact and identity information out of the optional professional summary.

Private owner access requires a live account session. Creating, editing or resuming a profile also requires an active onboarded member and the disposable intake fixture. Where the native account permits a current session, existing owners can read, pause and withdraw consent even when the intake fixture is closed. Additional consent can only be recorded explicitly. The three switches default off and remain independent. Disclosures, organizer search and matching have no C3.1 endpoints. Availability expiry and pause never remove a team role or accepted commitment. The Founder workspace has no profile or consent lookup.

## Export, erasure, holds and retention

Private profiles, selections, consents and the latest owner receipt are linked by database foreign keys and cascade when the underlying Auth account is lawfully deleted. Existing privacy workflows control whether the account is still held or reviewable. C3.1 introduces no new expiry job, automated account deletion or legal-hold override. The existing export renderer has no volunteer data adapter, and account lifecycle operations other than actual Auth deletion require an explicit C3 integration review before launch. No real export or erasure processing is activated. The fixture is disposable, and hosted intake is closed until the required lifecycle handling is verified.

For a future pilot, use account-linked retention only while the owner maintains the private profile, with an explicit expiration and withdrawal schedule approved before activation. Restricted catalog snapshots are kept for definition provenance and prevention of alias reassignment. They are separate from member consent. Founder audit reasons should contain no private evidence. Before operational launch, approve a time limit and a hold-aware minimization process for actor-linked audit and reason text while preserving the non-personal definition lineage. No real profile data is retained by the current closed feature.

## Safeguarding dependencies

Native platform eligibility continues to apply. C3.1 introduces no youth participation, age exception, regulated screening, credential review, background checks, sensitive placement or cross-border travel authorization. Before opening real intake, specify adult eligibility, an escalation and incident process, applicable notice and consent language, and responsibilities for regulated work. Organizers remain responsible for qualifications and safeguarding under the existing project process. Any feature-specific enforcement capability needs a separately reviewed scope, auditing and appeals design.
