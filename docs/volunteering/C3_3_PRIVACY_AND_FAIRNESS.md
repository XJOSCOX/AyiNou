# C3.3 Privacy and fairness

Purpose-specific consent and availability are evaluated with current data on every request. The personal pause affects matching only. The member can withdraw matching consent via C3.1 without changing organizer discovery consent, profile disclosure, existing applications, commitments, teams, tasks or invitations. C3.1 pausing of volunteering also removes the member from discovery. Discovery results may contain only an already visible public name and handle plus broad compatibility for that specific opportunity. Organizers cannot filter by arbitrary individual identities or read a volunteer's private profile or reasons.

Matching does not consider race, ethnicity, gender, religion, politics, disability, nationality, contributions, payments, followers, badges, rank, or sponsorship. The existing organizer identity and authorization checks are an access requirement, never a volunteer ranking signal. No recommendations are sent outside the application, no external models process data, and no notification or automatic application is created.

No private profile generations or discovery histories are stored. A recommendation dismissal is linked to its published revision for at most 90 days of effect and expired entries are removed during owner writes. The pause preference belongs to the C3.1 profile and cascades with its lawful deletion. The hosted system is closed, so no operational records are created. Before launch, approve a hold-aware retention and purge schedule and integrate owner export and erasure with the existing privacy lifecycle. Restricted Founder configuration history preserves reasons and provenance for governance and contains no volunteer data. The browser clears visible results during revalidation on focus and replaces rather than accumulates pages.
