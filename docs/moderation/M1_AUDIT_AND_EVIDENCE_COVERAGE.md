| Platform admin and governance | `platform_admin_audit` stores actor, subject, role/status action, reason and time. | Profile, username, membership | Username assignment/change events and self session/security history have A1A projections. | Organization, verification, impersonation and badges | Native institution audit, review work, verification reviews/appeals, impersonation decisions, Founder identity/authority, badge/recognition and public-badge rules each have scoped data. | A1A organization role/assigned verification projections are narrower than native workspaces. | Events | `event_audit` holds event, actor, version, action and reason for moderation. Preserve ballot privacy and avoid inclusion of voter or private volunteer details. |
| Platform policy history | F2.5 event and multilingual switches use the Founder write path and native platform policy audit. | Existing Founder policy history supplies homepage statistics and registration entries. F2.5 flags are written to native history without appearing in that projection. |

## A1A and evidence authority

A1A is a read projection over native events. Platform mode requires a live private admin workspace, recognizes Founder-only governance, and covers platform admin plus global post/comment moderation. Member mode is self-only. Organization mode requires an active institution and owner/admin role. Verification mode requires the review workspace and scoped assignment with conflict checks. Moderation mode requires fresh step-up and a specific post/comment reference within supported sources. Results redact reasons and other sensitive fields, expose stable native references, and use a bounded cursor/date window. A 90-day query window is not a retention guarantee. Use a source-policy inventory for actual retention and distinguish a readable event summary from authorized case evidence. [A1A authorization and projection](../../supabase/migrations/20261121145000_a1a_unified_audit_reads.sql#L108) · [Integration register](../audit/integration-register.json)

Recommend adding redacted case summary projections and stable links to authorized native detail. Record access to high-sensitivity evidence, its retention class and any narrow legal or appeal hold. Keep raw report explanations, private profiles, media objects, proofs, attendee data and ballots out of general history. Record modifications as reasoned events, keeping original authoritative content in its existing store or an approved case-bound evidence store. This audit includes no private evidence.
