# M3 authorization matrix

| Action | Permitted authority | Defensive verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create, extend, or lift ordinary account suspension | Eligible Founder or Administrator in original administration session with recent MFA. The configured Administrator maximum and historical renewal rules apply. | Account enforcement and policy disposable suites check the allow and deny cases, original term, old sessions and invalid revisions. |
| Issue or lift indefinite ban | Recognized Founder with live authority, recent MFA and bound workspace. Existing protected-target governance applies. | Account suite denies other roles and verifies access and reauthentication. Appeals suite checks protected release after independent findings. |
| High-impact feature restrictions | Recognized Founder. The native high-impact catalog and protected targets remain fixed. | Feature suite checks assignment and restriction denial to an Administrator plus Founder decision and evidence separation. |
| Ordinary feature restriction | Eligible Founder or Administrator, or Moderator with a live matching capability, scope and duration grant. | Feature and policy suites exercise absent, wrong scope, revoked and expired grants, limits, independent cases and overlap. |
| Founder policy management | Recognized Founder, current authority, fresh MFA, original admin workspace, expected revision and confirmed reason. | Policy suite exercises Administrator and Moderator denial, replay protection, concurrent revision conflict, historical restoration and prior decision preservation. |
| M2.4 appeal intake | Decision subject or eligible event organizer. Restricted account access is limited to the existing notice session. | Appeals suite checks subject and organizer scope, deadline, duplicate intake and expired time. |
| M2.4 appeal review | Assigned independent reviewer with the live review role and original review workspace. Protected release has a separate Founder gate. | Appeals suite checks material conflicts, reporter and decision maker, reviewer assignment privacy and exact-scope reversal. |
| A1A and native audit reads | Live authorized role and original private workspace for platform views. Source-specific subject and redaction gates apply. | Audit suite checks copied workspace denial, restricted scope, date boundaries, redaction, pagination and concurrent decisions. |
| Identity eligibility | Founder Verified satisfies the audited personal identity prerequisite where the effective migration applies. | Founder scenarios check existing identity, project, organization and reviewer boundaries separately. |
| Public project listing and new project review revisions | Current owner scope and normal workflow checks, including the applicable existing project feature restriction. Reviewer assignments and approval proofs remain in the review workflow. | Founder/project regression checks historical public visibility, authorized edits, scoped denial and successful continuation after restriction release. |
