## Related decisions and sensitive workflows

Ambassador and Early Partner enrollment, onboarding corrections, and account lifecycle requests have separate intake and decision routes. Enrollment decisions and badge/recognition actions are scoped approvals. Onboarding corrections have a dedicated request/correction path. Privacy requests, retention holds and deletion plans have separate purpose-limited approval, conflict, hold and execution constraints. Existing tests and migrations include synthetic lifecycle execution controls. These lanes must remain independent from ordinary account sanctions or content removal. ## Content decisions and access

- `moderate_scoped_post/comment` validate actor scope, recent MFA, version, current state, reason and actor ownership. Hide, remove and restore can be proactive. Dismiss needs an open report. A decision closes pending reports on the target. Remove is terminal in the normal restore operation. Author edit, author delete, moderation remove and reversible hide are distinct paths. No approved moderator command rewrites someone else's body. [Effective content decisions](../../supabase/migrations/20261120132000_phase4a_department_moderation.sql#L1)
- Ordinary direct reads use the same live resource predicates as listing surfaces. Hidden/removed source can suppress quotes, reposts, bookmarks, media links and related notifications without removing every stored relationship. Independently authored quote text follows the quote's own visibility rules. Direct API authorization for object delivery must be checked separately from the page. A previously issued signed link has its own validity window. [Interaction projections](../../supabase/migrations/20261123090000_f21_social_interactions.sql) · [Audience integrity](../../supabase/migrations/20261123125300_f24_audience_integrity.sql) · [Event and social notice visibility](../../supabase/migrations/20261123130100_f25_event_integration.sql#L162)
- Hidden content stays available to the applicable moderation projection. Terminal removal should be treated as a tombstone and decision history, with retention limited to what the native data and cleanup gates preserve. Do not assume exact historical versions or downloadable evidence for every action. Treat safer redaction as a future versioned visibility overlay with a case ID, public explanation and separate access to the original. ## Reuse and duplication

Community and Feed reporting already share content moderation primitives. Intake categories, reasons, conflicts, resolution, evidence and notices are modeled independently for trust, Feed, events and projects. Account restriction, organization restriction, event `moderated_at`, project `restricted_at`, group removal, member mute/block and feature switches are deliberately different concepts, yet they lack a common case/history projection. Planned M2 can reuse the native decision operations and align their case and enforcement semantics. [Unified enforcement proposal](M1_UNIFIED_ENFORCEMENT_PROPOSAL.md)
