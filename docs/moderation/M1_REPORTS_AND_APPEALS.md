## Appeal lanes

- **Content hide.** The current post/comment author can submit within 14 days of a hide through `POST /api/v1/moderation/actions/:id/appeals`. One appeal per underlying action. Same-reason retry returns the existing appeal. Global authority requires recent MFA, with platform Administrator/Founder required for scoped content. The original decision actor and appellant are excluded. The durable outcome is upheld, overturned or closed. Overturn restores only when the action is still current and the target still hidden. The appellant can read status and receives an outcome notice when eligible. [Content appeal intake](../../supabase/migrations/20261120121000_phase3f_appeals.sql#L69) · [Effective appeal decision](../../supabase/migrations/20261120132000_phase4a_department_moderation.sql#L195) · [Safety route](../../apps/api/src/routes/safety.ts)
- **Verification rejection/revocation.** Applicant must be eligible and submit within 14 days of the eligible original decision with written explanation and bounded public references. A unique original decision binds the appeal. Eligible admins assign, with representative-specific Founder queue/assignment scope. The assigned independent reviewer uses recent MFA and original-reviewer, applicant and conflict checks. Uphold or send to reconsideration. Reconsideration reopens review under the applicable criteria rather than directly issuing a badge or claim. Assignment history, decision rationale and member appeal history are native. [Trust routes](../../apps/api/src/routes/trust.ts) · [Verification appeals](../../supabase/migrations/20261120161000_phase5a_identity_cases.sql#L94) · [Effective representative appeal assignment](../../supabase/migrations/20261123123000_founder_representative_review.sql#L84) · [Trust notices](../../supabase/migrations/20261120162000_phase5a_verification_notifications.sql)
- **Founder identity.** An adverse Founder verification case can enter its specialized appeal/advisory process. Eligibility, reviewer conflict, assignment under approved authority and bounded policy state are case-specific. The Founder workspace reads and operates only its permitted portion. This process is distinct from ordinary blue verification, account sanctions and Feed appeals. [Founder case appeals](../../supabase/migrations/20261121147000_fv_final_development_policy.sql#L144)

## Priority gaps

Preserve case linkage from report through final action and independent decision. Intake retention and closure mean different things across domains today. Event decisions lack the explicit conflicts present in project/trust lanes, and event actions record the event and actor but omit the originating report ID. Establish whether multiple reports should share one case, whether reporters should receive status without private evidence, and how restricted users can submit or read an account appeal through a narrowly scoped path.
