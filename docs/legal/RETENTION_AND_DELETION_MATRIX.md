# Ayinou retention and deletion decision matrix

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

This register describes mechanisms in the reviewed application. A visibility window, expiring invitation, signed URL or rate window can end before underlying records are removed. Proposed choices below have no approved duration. For categories marked **open**, the owner and counsel must define the trigger, maximum duration, documented exceptions and eligible removal method. Operations must establish that these choices work across the live database, Storage, vendors and restoration.

## Current state and decisions by category

In the holds column, **review required** identifies a potential exception that has no general implemented legal-hold workflow. **Existing** identifies behavior in current application code. Table and service names are representative of the underlying category rather than a full schema listing.

| Category and actual storage | Current deletion or retention mechanism | Evidence or legal holds | Proposed policy choice, operational requirement and open decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Registration, sessions, credentials, email and MFA** — Supabase Auth users, identities, sessions, factors, and provider logs or email delivery records where configured. | Sign-out revokes access, and sessions and links expire under Auth configuration. Factor and password management are separate from user deletion. Some application rows depend on the Auth UUID. Existing backup and provider log schedules are unverified. | Review required for compromised accounts, legal preservation and administrative safeguards. | Choose separate rules for active credentials, stale unconfirmed accounts, sessions and provider logs. Verify which objects and dependencies must be dealt with before deleting the Auth user. Approve safe exception and recovery handling. |
| **Private identity fields, onboarding, membership and residence** — `public.member_private` and initial names in Supabase Auth signup metadata. | Account holder can edit allowed profile fields. Rows can cascade on Auth hard deletion. Profile edits do not establish removal of the original metadata or historical provider copies. No integrated account erasure job. | Review required for an applicable hold. Broad self-reported geography can inform aggregates. | Verify both Auth metadata and profile copies. Minimize private fields, review stale onboarding and unconfirmed accounts, and decide whether retained aggregate statistics need recalculation or further de-identification. |
| **Public profile, username, biography and avatar** — `public.profiles`, review-backed records and `member-avatars` Storage. | Visibility changes stop eligible projections and issuance of new links. Existing Feed links have a two-minute lifetime and own-profile avatar links use five minutes. Replacement attempts to remove the previous derivative. Account-row cascade is separate from bucket cleanup. | Review required for an authorized complaint or abuse hold. Previously received images are outside backend recall. | Decide closure and username reuse, targeted object cleanup, verification of failed removals and treatment of independently retained copies. Confirm whether public history should reference a de-identified actor. |
| **Settings, interests and device preferences** — `public.user_settings`, `public.member_interests`, profile locale, session/locale cookies and browser storage for language, theme and Feed mode. | Account-linked settings follow database deletion rules. Members can update many preferences. They can clear local storage independently of the account. | Review required if a setting is material to a documented dispute. Routine preferences need limited retention. | Define stale settings inventory, cookies and device lifetimes, and describe how to clear them. Keep retained device state distinct from the account's server data. |
| **Member and referral codes, attribution and activity** — `private.member_codes`, `private.referral_codes`, `private.referrals`, optional signup referral code in Auth metadata, `public.user_activity_daily` and metric exclusions. | Relevant member and attribution records are Auth-linked. Referral relationship can cascade when either user is removed. Qualifying activity markers and exceptions use a separate data path. No general retention or referral cleanup schedule is installed. | Review required for fraud or disputed attribution. Aggregate counts use suppression. | Choose stale pending attribution and daily activity schedules, whether codes may ever be retired or reused, and cutoff criteria for preserving only aggregates. Include submitted signup metadata in any deletion or redaction review. |
| **Verification cases, evidence, badge history, review and impersonation** — private verification applications, evidence URLs, review work/notes, appeals, impersonation reports, assignment audits and `public.verification_records`. | Revocation changes projections, while application and reviewer records can persist. Foreign keys may cascade cases or detach a reviewer. There is no scheduled evidence purge or raw ID upload workflow. | Existing appeal and provenance records matter to review. A general hold, redaction and purge schedule is open. | Prioritize deletion of evidence URLs and sensitive narrative when the approved need ends. Decide what minimal decision provenance may survive, control reviewer access after closure, and cover external evidence hosts separately. |
| **Institutions, private contacts, logos, representative roles and affiliations** — private organization, role, membership, affiliation, invitation, request and audit tables plus `institution-logos` Storage. | Revocation and consent changes affect current projection. Invitations and role terms can expire. Related rows may cascade or actor references may detach. Logo replacement attempts derivative removal. | Review required for ownership disputes, reviewer records or a restriction. | Define inactive institution, contact-change and expired-invite cleanup. Decide owner transitions, preserved audit fields and a verified object removal path. |
| **Groups, memberships, invitations, requests and staff actions** — `feed_data.communities` and related membership, staff and group audit tables. | Leaving, removal and withdrawal change access. Group invitations and ownership offers can expire. Historical discussions and audit events can remain. | Review required for a moderation or membership dispute. Existing scope and historical privacy checks apply. | Decide group closure and archival, which former members may retain relevant receipts, and disposal of join notes, denied invitations and private history. |
| **Posts, drafts, comments, reactions and following** — Feed posts, topics, submissions, comments, reactions and follow tables. | Eligible user and moderator actions can hide or remove projections. Soft deletion and comment tombstones can preserve context. Account or related-row deletion can cascade or remove references by relation. No routine text purge. | Existing report or appeal history can justify retaining relevant content. Design review and release for any broader hold. | Decide when to erase, retain anonymously or preserve under a documented exception. Include drafts, revisions, quoted copies and relationship edges. Verify that deleted or held text is absent from unauthorized APIs. |
| **Safety lists, reports, moderation and appeals** — Feed block, mute, reports, actions and appeal tables, and private impersonation or restriction records. | User controls update safety lists. Audit or reporter information stays restricted. Pending actions or appeals retain relevant evidence and may prevent removal of decision records. | Existing linked evidence holds and independent appeal constraints. Review required for separate legal preservation. | Distinguish a member's current block or mute from a case file. Approve severity-specific holds and release authority, retention of reasons and independent access review. |
| **Post media and alt text** — `feed_data.post_attachments` plus private `post-media` derivatives. | Eligible unattached stages expire after 24 hours. Detached media has a 24-hour grace period where applicable. Bounded upload-triggered or operator cleanup removes eligible objects with recovery on failure. | Existing pending reports or appeals can extend a hold. | Define storage cleanup cadence, and whether and when to erase alt text, detached rows and objects. Inventory before deleting references, reconcile orphan paths, handle retries and verify inability to fetch protected bytes. |
| **Notification inbox and preferences** — `feed_data.notifications`, associated events and notification preferences. | Inbox reads use a 30-day window. Old rows are reclaimed in bounded batches on activity or through operator maintenance. The target has separate retention. | Review required if an alert is evidence. Generic notices omit sensitive case details. | Decide hard-deletion schedule for invitations, generic alerts and settings. Keep event audit retention separate from inbox records. |
| **Feed and community recommendation state** — Feed and community snapshot and feedback tables, ranking settings and interests. | Snapshots expire after ten minutes. Bounded cleanup exists. Community “Not interested” expires after 30 days. Hides can persist until undo/reset and Feed feedback until applicable reset or related deletion. | Review required only for a documented need to preserve specific ranking abuse evidence. | Decide whether to purge expired state proactively, document opt-out/reset behavior and erase subject-specific histories without discarding unrelated ranking configuration. |
| **Founding Ambassador case and publicity** — `private.ambassador_cases` and `private.ambassador_audit`. | Public recognition ends when consent or eligibility ends. Account deletion clears the recipient and public statement/consent where specified. Private notes may remain. Invitations and reconsiderations have separate workflow windows. | Review required for disputed decisions and possible legal preservation. | Separate public profile withdrawal, private eligibility notes and minimal decision provenance. Set reassessment and destruction triggers. |
| **Project proposals, approval and ownership** — `private.community_projects`, related revisions, images and events, and private `post-media` derivatives. | A parent project deletion can cascade linked records. Account removal can delete personally owned proposals or detach actors from surviving records as applicable. Approved records and images can be tied to historical revisions. No integrated purge. | Review required for a proposal dispute, misuse report or ongoing obligations. | Decide how ownership continuity, approved decisions, frozen snapshots and associated objects survive closure. Keep required project history scoped and remove unrelated private material. |
| **Project teams, volunteers and tasks** — private execution, team, invitation, opportunity, application, task and operation event tables. | Withdrawal, revocation and expiry change access or participation. Parent/resource rules can cascade records or detach an actor. Written application notes and history can remain. | Review required for participant safety, complaints and documented operational history. | Set separate rules for rejected or withdrawn applications, assigned tasks, private notes and evidence. Test removal without exposing or deleting another participant's permitted records. |
| **Milestones, metrics, progress evidence and final reports** — private project milestones, metrics, observations, progress entries, reports and project media in private Storage. | Approved snapshots retain their history. Corrections use new reviewed entries. Parent deletion and actor detachment follow individual relationships. No uniform evidence cleanup policy. | Existing permission boundary for team evidence. Review required for a safety, accountability or legal hold. | Decide private evidence purge, historic correction display, redaction of identifying material and preservation of aggregate or published outcomes. Clean derived media separately. |
| **Polls, nominees, electorate, private ballots and decisions** — private polls, options, electorate, ballots, events and decisions. | Nominee withdrawal or ineligibility changes public projection. Account hard deletion can clear a voter's link while a counted choice survives. Parent scope deletion can cascade associated records. Cancelling a poll does not purge ballots. | Review required for disputes and preservation of the outcome. Ballot secrecy applies throughout. | Decide whether and when to remove account-to-ballot linkage, nominee records or old electorate rows. Preserve legitimate aggregate provenance without allowing another party to reconstruct a vote. Assess re-identification risk and lawful export limits. |
| **Restrictions, audits, quotas and technical logs** — private restrictions and privileged event ledgers, Feed read/write counters, Fastify application logs and hosting logs. | Account or resource activity can detach identifiers under schema rules. Old quota windows stop affecting requests and bounded maintenance can remove old rows. Durable audit reasons can remain. Production log rotation is unverified. | Review required for incident preservation, disputes and formal requests. Status and decision timestamps can be material. | Separate high-volume telemetry from privileged audit. Minimize free-text reasons, approve case and log schedules, configure independent retention approval, operator access and auditable deletion. |
| **Backups, provider records and restore copies** — Supabase and any future approved production host, delivery or monitoring provider. Exact backup regions and schedules require confirmation. | Application deletion does not establish deletion in provider logs, email, backups or restore copies. Backup restoration and suppression of erased records have not been operationally tested. | Preserve only narrowly when an approved obligation requires it. Isolate restored or retained copies. | Document actual rotation and provider request mechanisms, access and region, treatment of failed or partial deletion, isolated restore rehearsal and reapply verified erasure before a restored environment returns to service. |

## Approval and verification

For every class, approve the start event for the schedule, what is erased or retained, the maximum age, whether access is restricted during a hold, who approves an exception, when it is reviewed, and how it is released. Define how an operator reconciles failed cleanup and how a requester receives an accurate completion statement. Evidence holds cannot be silently extended into indefinite retention. Release gates, request implementation and policies have separate sign-offs.
