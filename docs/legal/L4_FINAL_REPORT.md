## Factual and editorial reconciliation

The wording follows the committed baseline and L3.5 evidence. Project images in PostgreSQL are distinguished from post media and Storage buckets. The public Terms and Privacy draft describe real controls without using synthetic export time or use limits as entitlements. Technical expiry, removal, closure, provider copies and physical erasure stay separate. The drafts reserve the existing 14-day windows for their eligible product appeals, and leave privacy clocks and other appeal routes open. Institution roles, membership, independent verification, administrator bootstrap trust, corporate office, private ballots and advisory results have consistent meanings.

Limitations for a scoped feature are preserved when the core policies summarize it. The source-backed Cookie Notice distinguishes configuration from live deployed observations. Future founder, partner and program features use explicit future headings and conditional wording. The proposed programs annex is excluded from the present agreement. No prospective partner name, individual records or personal evidence are included.

## Research and open decisions

Reviewed Texas Secretary of State and Business Organizations Code sources, Texas Attorney General privacy, security and charitable guidance, IRS nonprofit and conflict guidance, federal COPPA materials, U.S. Copyright Office materials, HHS and FTC sources, Haiti's official gazette amendment and its state report to the UN. Source dates, scope and counsel questions are in the [research appendix](L4_RESEARCH_AND_EVIDENCE.md). No determination of legal applicability or market clearance was made.

The [approval register](L4_APPROVAL_AND_GAP_REGISTER.md) records open decisions on legal operator and formation, market coverage, assent and disputes, safeguarding, support and complaint handling, reviewer capacity, service providers, security and vendor access, rights and appeals, retention and language approval. Real privacy operations still need a staffed secure process, approved scopes and response rules, production custody and key management, per-store erasure proof and an isolated provider restoration rehearsal. The adult-only and invitation-only pilot still needs implementation approval and validation. Governance drafts need a formed entity and formal adoption.

## Validation and release boundary

Quality review checked 20 legal Markdown files, 111 headings, 118 relative links and heading references, 40 marked approval placeholders and 31 distinct open decisions. The review included draft-label checks, source checks for disputed product claims, terminology and confidentiality boundaries, and a staged source/secret review. Application tests are outside the scope of documentation-only validation. There were no changes to code, dependencies, migrations or runtime gates, and no hosted writes, deployment or policy-page publication. Real privacy intake, export, closure, deletion and retention enforcement remain disabled according to the verified L3.5 configuration and unchanged L4 implementation.

L4 ends with owner and counsel review of this draft suite. L5 translation, review and version preparation is the next legal milestone. Public policy pages require a later separate approval.
