## 1. Official legal sources and issues

| Jurisdiction and classification | Verified source proposition | Question for counsel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Texas formation and governance — conditional on adopting a Texas nonprofit corporation | The Secretary of State describes forming a nonprofit corporation through a certificate of formation. Chapter 22 addresses governing authority, directors, officers, records and fundamental transactions. Formation and tax status are separate. [Texas Secretary of State](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/nonprofit_org.shtml) · [Texas Business Organizations Code Chapter 22](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/Docs/BO/htm/BO.22.htm) | What form, purposes, board independence, membership structure, appointments, financial oversight, transition and dissolution provisions fit the actual operator? |
| U.S. federal recognition and governance — conditional | Form 1023 instructions, revised December 2024, describe the determination process, exempt purposes, disclosure and example conflict controls. Texas incorporation alone does not create an IRS designation. [IRS Form 1023 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023) · [IRS conflict-policy guidance](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/form-1023-purpose-of-conflict-of-interest-policy) | How should the proposed activities, organizing document, compensation, interested transactions and overseas programs be structured? If eligible, which federal application and state exemption filings apply? |
| Texas charitable solicitation — conditional | Texas OAG describes specific registration obligations for certain types of solicitation and distinguishes registration from endorsement. No future payment or solicitation rules can be inferred from formation or platform verification. [Texas OAG charitable registration and filings](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/divisions/charitable-trusts/registration-and-filings) | Which states or countries would a proposed campaign reach, who controls the funds, and what registrations, disclosures, records or restrictions would apply before collection? |
| Texas Data Privacy and Security Act — conditional on operator and activity | Texas OAG identifies the Act as effective July 1, 2024 and lists nonprofit organizations among exempt entity categories. This draft cannot apply that exemption to an unspecified operator or extend it to another law or vendor. [Texas OAG guidance](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/file-consumer-complaint/consumer-privacy-rights/texas-data-privacy-and-security-act) | Determine entity and data scope, applicable requests and exceptions, processor obligations, other states and conflicts. Evaluate accessibility, identity proof, appeal and targeted disclosure separately. |
| Texas breach reporting — separate conditional regime | Current OAG guidance specifies an Attorney General reporting route and a 30-day limit for the qualifying threshold it describes, with electronic filing effective September 1, 2023. Do not use those rules as a nationwide or product response deadline. [Texas OAG reporting](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/data-breach-reporting) · [Texas OAG Chapter 521 guidance](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/file-consumer-complaint/consumer-privacy-rights/identity-theft-enforcement-and-protection-act) | Determine who is responsible, when notice clocks start, affected residency, decision authority, exceptions and overlapping state, federal and Haitian duties. |
| U.S. children's privacy — conditional | FTC guidance discusses child-directed services and general-audience operators with actual knowledge of collection from children under 13. The federal rule amendments were published April 22, 2025, with a general compliance date of April 22, 2026 and exceptions stated in the final rule. [FTC COPPA guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions) · [Federal Register final rule](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-04-22/html/2025-05904.htm) | Determine covered operator and actual audience, state youth laws, applicable consent, age policies, proof minimization, safeguarding and relevant court orders. Owner's intended 18+ pilot is a separate policy choice. |
| U.S. intellectual property — conditional | The Copyright Office describes prerequisites for applicable section 512 safe harbors, designated agents for covered services and notice/counter-notice procedures. [Copyright Office section 512](https://www.copyright.gov/512/) · [Designated agents](https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/) | Which safe harbor, if any, is applicable? Approve a staffed process, contact, notices, repeat-infringer procedures, evidence custody and regional alternatives. |
| Future U.S. health programs — conditional, unimplemented | HHS defines HIPAA-covered entities and business associates. Separately, the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule addresses covered personal health record businesses and partners. Its 2024 amendments took effect July 29, 2024. Counsel must classify each party and activity individually and consider state regimes. [HHS covered entities](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/covered-entities/index.html) · [FTC final rule](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/federal-register-notices/16-cfr-part-318-health-breach-notification-rule) · [FTC effective-date guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-breach-notification-rule-basics-business) | What role, consent or other authority, professional restrictions, consumer-health rules, emergency routing, confidentiality, disclosures and remedies apply to an exact future program in each jurisdiction? |
| Haiti — local applicability unresolved | Haiti's state report to the UN Human Rights Committee identifies constitutional Article 49 correspondence protections. The 2012 constitutional amendments are published in Le Moniteur. These sources establish context and do not establish a general sector-wide data-rights process equivalent to a U.S. state's regime. [Haiti state report CCPR/C/HTI/1](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/751625/files/CCPR_C_HTI_1-EN.pdf) · [Official gazette text archived by Refworld](https://www.refworld.org/legal/legislation/natlegbod/2012/99429) | Have Haitian counsel verify the current consolidated text and implementing laws. Determine operator obligations for privacy, communications, consumer matters, platform liability, minors, transfers, retention, referrals, medical confidentiality, local organizational and fundraising status, notices and remedies. |

No legal regime was adjudged binding on Ayinou on this record. The operator, activities, counterparties, markets and production arrangements remain unsettled. Evaluate jurisdiction at the activity level. Recheck legislation, regulator guidance and court orders at approval. Treat purpose limitation, restricted access, clear notices and independent review as proposed safeguards unless an applicable obligation is established. The initial markets, preferred organizational structure, adult and invitation pilot, and four platform languages are owner planning directions.
