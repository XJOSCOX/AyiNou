# Proposed nonprofit whistleblower policy

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

Internal instrument for future adoption by the nonprofit's board.

## Reporting

Directors, workers, volunteers and other covered people should have a secure route to report suspected fraud, governance violations, serious safety problems, data misuse, retaliation, or destruction of records. Provide a conflict-independent alternative for reports involving the founder, executive or usual recipient. An emergency procedure should route immediate safety risks to approved responders. [Approval required — confidential channels, recipients, backup coverage and availability.]

## Review and protection

Triage reports for conflict, urgency and evidence needs. Assign an independent reviewer and limit information to those who need it. Explain confidentiality limits and avoid promising anonymity when legally required disclosures or due process could identify a person. Keep records separate from public moderation and do not use ordinary administrative access as investigation authority. Preserve scoped evidence, document findings and determine proportionate corrective action. Give affected parties an appropriate opportunity to respond while protecting reporters and witnesses.

Prohibit retaliation for good-faith reporting or cooperation. A mistaken good-faith concern is not grounds for discipline. Deliberate fabrication can be handled separately. This internal process should not interfere with permissible external reporting or legal rights. [Approval required — oversight, documented review targets, reporting rights, escalation, remedies and preservation schedule.]
