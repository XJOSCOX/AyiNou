# Proposed nonprofit corporate records retention policy

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

Internal instrument for a future corporation. It governs corporate records separately from the platform's user-data retention policy.

## Custody and schedules

The proposed records officer coordinates with the secretary, treasury, counsel, program leads and security. Inventory formation and exemption documents, bylaws, minutes and written consents, delegations, conflict and compensation records, audits, accounts, taxes and grants, contracts, employment and safeguarding files, incident records and insurance. For each class record the authoritative copy, owner, access class, obligations and trigger, approved duration and disposition, duplicate and vendor custody, and review date. A durable access log and separate custody are appropriate for sensitive documents. [Approval required — statutory and contractual schedules, permanent records, authorized inspection and backup procedures.]

## Holds, disposition and succession

Suspend routine disposition upon a scoped legal or preservation hold, document who issued it and require an authorized release. Apply holds to relevant paper, digital and provider copies. Use independent checks for overlaps. Document destruction of eligible records and reconcile vendor, backup and transferred copies as approved. Do not apply a corporate schedule to ballot, project or privacy records without approved classification and scope. On office turnover or dissolution, securely transfer records, access and open preservation duties to approved custodians.
