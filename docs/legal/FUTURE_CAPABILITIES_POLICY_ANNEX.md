# Future organization programs and referral capabilities

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

Internal planning annex. Excluded from the current Terms and from live intake or delivery. A prospective partner discussion confers no approval or authority. Do not name interested organizations, their staff or prospective beneficiaries in shared drafts or tests.

## 1. Program operations

A later Organization Programs feature could let an authorized institution publish program capacity and requirements, receive applications, maintain a restricted waitlist, evaluate nonclinical eligibility, coordinate volunteers and refer consenting applicants to vetted partners. Define the responsible organization, decision authority, criteria, accessible alternatives, correction, conflicts, review routes, timely notices and rules for unused places. The interface should distinguish a program listing, an application, a waitlist position, an eligibility result and an actual referral or service offer. A waitlist place should confer no guarantee of help. Use fictional records until operational approvals exist.

## 2. Counseling, education, financial assistance and referrals

Future design topics include counseling support, fertility education, financial assistance, IVF assistance coordination and medical-provider referrals. Education requires appropriately reviewed, labeled material. Administrative staff and ordinary volunteers may coordinate approved nonclinical logistics. Credentialed professionals and approved providers remain responsible for clinical judgments and services. A referral would pass only the information needed for the agreed purpose with approved, specific consent or other applicable authority. A qualified provider makes clinical decisions independently. Do not implement automated clinical diagnosis, medical eligibility algorithms, donation processing or a guarantee of funding, appointments, pregnancy, treatment or any clinical outcome.

## 3. Sensitive-data gates

Before collecting any health or personal application data, assign a responsible operator and partner roles, assess applicable local confidentiality and privacy law, define strict access and logging, and document why each field is necessary. Separate program coordinators, clinicians, volunteers, reviewers and financial staff. Prevent unrelated project teams, platform administrators, recommendation systems and public directories from receiving sensitive application information. Set consent, withdrawal, minors, urgent requests, accessibility and proxy rules. Use protected notices, secure transfer, vetted providers, training, provider contracts, retention and deletion procedures and independent audit. Log disclosures without copying case substance. A paused or discontinued feature needs an approved continuity and disposition plan.
