Do not use a platform-member term for legal nonprofit membership. Authentication proves account control, while identity verification is a separate reviewed claim. A verified organization is not endorsed. A private ballot is not necessarily anonymous. Closure, visibility removal, technical expiry and data erasure are different acts. Project approval is not financial endorsement. Each distinction requires clause-level review in context.
