# Ayinou Voting & Polling Policy

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

## 1. Scope and authority

Ayinou polls are advisory consultations. Community, institution or project poll managers can use eligible results to inform discussion or record a recommendation. Ayinou does not conduct Haitian or U.S. government elections. A poll does not elect a legal officer, approve a project, transfer ownership or amend any independently required decision. A recommendation may be recorded under the published process with a written reason. The authority to take a later action must come from the applicable separate workflow.

## 2. Eligibility, consent and ballot

A poll's audience, scope and eligibility determine who can vote. Eligible votes are limited to one accepted choice per individual account. An institution profile confers no additional individual ballot, and an administrator or badge has no extra voting weight. Ordinary advisory polls use current eligibility. Organization, project, candidate and recorded-recommendation polls use the higher-trust workflow. It requires independently reviewed identity, fixes the eligible account list at publication and rechecks applicable current restrictions, scope and active verification when a ballot is cast. Late entrants cannot join the frozen list. An accepted ballot can remain counted even if eligibility changes later.

A nominee must meet applicable eligibility and discoverability, and must affirmatively consent. A material change to the candidate poll question or description requires renewed consent. A nominee can withdraw. Loss of eligibility or withdrawal suppresses current individual display and prevents new selection. Historical counts can remain attached to an anonymized option. Candidate replacement during drafting must follow the applicable consent process.

## 3. Privacy and result interpretation

Ballots remain private. View the supported personal receipt to resolve an uncertain submission instead of repeatedly trying to vote. Organizers do not receive voters' choices or a ballot identity list. A frozen electorate is a protected eligibility record. Aggregate visibility depends on the poll's settings and stage. A poll can display results after voting where enabled, and final results require an explicit close after the voting deadline. Quorum is an optional minimum ballot count. An optional decision threshold uses the leading option's share of counted ballots. No ballots, a tie, missed quorum or threshold, or an unavailable leading candidate can make a result inconclusive. A chart or ranking is not itself a recorded decision.

## 4. Integrity, disputes and records

Do not buy, sell, coerce, trade or impersonate votes. Do not coordinate fake accounts, seek private ballot screenshots or infer individual choices by combining results with private participant lists. Contest a tally or procedure by identifying the poll and specific issue. Do not publish a person's suspected choice or use the challenge to target them.
