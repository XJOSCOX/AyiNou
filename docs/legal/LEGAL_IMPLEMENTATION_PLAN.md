# Ayinou legal implementation plan

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

Planning deliverable for Legal Phase L1. The items below describe future work after explicit approval. The current phase creates documentation only.

## 1. Establish an operating baseline

1. Confirm the entity, support and privacy contacts, approved markets, eligibility and dispute rules. Assign a named owner for each legal, product, engineering, safety and infrastructure decision. Verify channels and coverage in test cases before describing them in a policy.
2. Confirm the production inventory for web hosting, Supabase Auth/database/Storage, email delivery, backups, logs, subprocessors, operational access and support regions. Record configured features and relevant signed or accepted agreements separately from provider marketing or standard terms.
3. Approve category-specific retention and lawful hold rules, account closure behavior, identity assurance for rights cases and mandatory time limits for each launch market. Define timelines using the approved legal basis and operational capacity. [Decision register](POLICY_DECISIONS.md) · [Retention matrix](RETENTION_AND_DELETION_MATRIX.md)

## 2. Design the account rights workflow

The current application lets people manage account settings and individual content. It has no complete deletion, export, rights-intake or progress-tracking workflow. The reviewed Privacy Policy must distinguish these states until each proposed flow is implemented and tested.

| Workstream | Future implementation | Acceptance before a policy commitment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Request intake | Provide a privacy channel and clear entry point for access, correction, export, deletion and other approved requests. Bind each request to one verified subject or authorized representative. Record only the minimum contact, request type, timestamps, tracking reference and verification state. | A user can find the channel, submit safely, receive a reference and obtain accessible updates. A legitimate requester who lost account access has a controlled recovery path. |
| Authentication and abuse controls | Require a fresh session and recent appropriate verification for sensitive exports or destructive actions, with a separate review path for inaccessible accounts and authorized agents. Use existing MFA where appropriate and require proof only proportionate to the risk. Rate-limit intake and avoid disclosing whether an unrelated account exists. | Test revoked/expired sessions, shared devices, a malicious cross-account request, concurrent requests, false agent claims and minimally invasive alternative verification. Ordinary support email never becomes a channel for passwords, one-time codes or raw IDs. |
| Status and review | Track received, verification required, accepted, processing, held/restricted, completed, partially completed, declined and appeal/escalation states as approved. Use independent reviewer permissions for sensitive exclusions and record a reason. Return only the requester's authorized projection. | Test recent authentication, access boundaries, lock/version behavior, repeat submissions, notifications and recipient identity. Staff can show the last action, owner and promised deadline without exposing protected evidence or unrelated records. |
| Data export | Map the authenticated subject to profile and settings, participation and contributions, independently scoped project records, eligible access history and ordinary media. Generate a readable manifest and machine-readable package where required. Redact third-party private data, security-sensitive records, reviewer material and held items under approved rules. Consider the person's own eligible ballot receipt without providing another voter's data or reconstructing the electorate. | Verify completeness by category, scope filters and redaction. Deliver through protected, time-limited retrieval with expiry and revocation. Test replay, access after expiry, account switch, deletions during export, auditability and purge of generated archives. |
| Account closure and deletion | Determine whether to restrict and revoke sessions during processing, handle cancellation consistently, identify the subject's active role/ownership obligations, and inventory all relevant DB records and Storage objects before unlinking identity. Erase or redact eligible rows, detach identifiers in approved retained records, and lock held records down. Use a recoverable, idempotent order with checkpoints before deleting the Auth account. | A closed account cannot regain access through a stale session or background retry. Test restricted and restricted-by-hold cases, deletion of the same subject twice, mixed ownership, orphan objects, last-administrator continuity, and another person's ability to access their own retained records. |
| Restricted evidence and voting | Separate narrow preserved records from ordinary client projections. Require a documented reason, defined scope, authorized hold placement and release, a review date and automatic eligibility for cleanup after release where approved. Preserve only the minimum identity or receipt data needed. | Pending reports/appeals retain eligible evidence while the requester gets a reason and next step. A removed actor is not re-exposed through old reports, notices, referrals, recommendation snapshots, public directories or poll results. Reconcile aggregate and per-account receipt decisions with ballot confidentiality. |
| Media cleanup | Take an inventory of avatar and logo objects, post variants, pending uploads, detachments, and immutable project revision assets. Confirm bucket paths on the server and distinguish dedicated from shared or held assets. Perform bounded deletion, track retry leases and reconcile failed or orphan removals with database state. | Verify object deletion and authorized read behavior across avatar and logo URLs, private image proxies, pending and public revisions. Explain the lifetime of issued links, caches and outside copies. |
| Providers, delivery and restoration | Review storage deletion APIs, hosted Auth and email or support records, infrastructure logs, project backups and provider-side request handling. Track completion per store, and maintain a minimal approved suppression record for isolated restoration. | Restore to an isolated environment and reapply verified erasure or quarantine before reopened access. Obtain provider evidence or record the exact limitation on delayed backup removal. Test partial failure recovery, including backup rotation and request retries. |
| Completion and communication | Keep a purpose-limited completion record with opaque case reference, decisions, categories handled, exceptions, timing, expiry and responsible operator. Show “complete” only when the agreed operation is verified, with clear remaining provider/backup limitations or retained classes where disclosure is permitted. | Demonstrate independent audit without retaining deleted payloads, credentials or package contents. Provide accessible and accurate completion, rejection and escalation notices. |

## 3. Approve retention and safe operations

- Map each approved schedule to exact fields and object keys, allow exceptions by purpose and case, and document any de-identification or aggregation. Restrict and record access to retained evidence. Measure removal and retry failures with summary counts and safe request IDs.
- Use disposable or rollback-only fixtures first. Test minimum access, safe queue recovery, concurrent activity, retention holds, object permissions, incorrect target prevention, and independently verifiable outcomes across Auth, database, private Storage and backups. Route scope-sensitive data through the existing authorization boundary.
- Start with dry-run previews and human sign-off on each scope. Set operational hold/release and audit duties, response owners and escalation procedures. Confirm legal handling of valid requests and any incident reporting triggers with qualified reviewers. Keep actual privacy routes and secure support intake separate from publication until staffed.

## 5. Publication gates

- **Owner and legal approval.** Confirm the legal operator, governing law, dispute terms, markets, age policy, intellectual-property workflow, permitted content licence, data processing controls and contacts. Resolve every public placeholder.
- **Operational proof.** Staff independent verification and appeals, administrator continuity, moderation, human media review, SMTP, rights intake, retention, incident response, review governance and launch support. Verify monitoring, provider behavior, restoration and capacity. The reviewed environment had zero eligible reviewers and one active administrator.
- **Technical proof.** Field-check features and disclosure text in the intended release environment. Demonstrate rights-request, deletion, export, secure access, retention and restoration behaviors before promising them. Approve any changes to eligibility, policy display or versioned acceptance as separate work.
- **Language and approval record.** Complete qualified translation and legal review for each required locale. Record publication authority, date/version, sign-offs and the notification procedure. Plan staged release and a way to correct published text or pause rollout.

This phase leaves the drafts in repository documentation. Product policy routes, consent UI, eligibility changes, database migrations, and account rights workflows require a separate authorization.
