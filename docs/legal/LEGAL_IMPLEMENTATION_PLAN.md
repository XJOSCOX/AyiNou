# Ayinou legal implementation plan

## 1. Establish an operating baseline

1. Confirm the entity, support and privacy contacts, approved markets, eligibility and dispute rules. Assign a named owner for each legal, product, engineering, safety and infrastructure decision. Verify channels and coverage in test cases before describing them in a policy.
2. Confirm the production inventory for web hosting, Supabase Auth/database/Storage, email delivery, backups, logs, subprocessors, operational access and support regions. Record configured features and relevant signed or accepted agreements separately from provider marketing or standard terms.
3. Approve category-specific retention and lawful hold rules, account closure behavior, identity assurance for rights cases and mandatory time limits for each launch market. Define timelines using the approved legal basis and operational capacity. [Decision register](POLICY_DECISIONS.md) · [Retention matrix](RETENTION_AND_DELETION_MATRIX.md)

## 2. Design the account rights workflow

## 3. Approve retention and safe operations

- Map each approved schedule to exact fields and object keys, allow exceptions by purpose and case, and document any de-identification or aggregation. Restrict and record access to retained evidence. Measure removal and retry failures with summary counts and safe request IDs.
- Use disposable or rollback-only fixtures first. Test minimum access, safe queue recovery, concurrent activity, retention holds, object permissions, incorrect target prevention, and independently verifiable outcomes across Auth, database, private Storage and backups. Route scope-sensitive data through the existing authorization boundary.
- Start with dry-run previews and human sign-off on each scope. Set operational hold/release and audit duties, response owners and escalation procedures. Confirm legal handling of valid requests and any incident reporting triggers with qualified reviewers. Keep actual privacy routes and secure support intake separate from publication until staffed.

## 5. Publication gates

- **Owner and legal approval.** Confirm the legal operator, governing law, dispute terms, markets, age policy, intellectual-property workflow, permitted content licence, data processing controls and contacts. Resolve every public placeholder.
- **Operational proof.** Staff independent verification and appeals, administrator continuity, moderation, human media review, SMTP, rights intake, retention, incident response, review governance and launch support. Verify monitoring, provider behavior, restoration and capacity. The reviewed environment had zero eligible reviewers and one active administrator.
- **Technical proof.** Field-check features and disclosure text in the intended release environment. Demonstrate rights-request, deletion, export, secure access, retention and restoration behaviors before promising them. Approve any changes to eligibility, policy display or versioned acceptance as separate work.
- **Language and approval record.** Complete qualified translation and legal review for each required locale. Record publication authority, date/version, sign-offs and the notification procedure. Plan staged release and a way to correct published text or pause rollout.

This phase leaves the drafts in repository documentation. Product policy routes, consent UI, eligibility changes, database migrations, and account rights workflows require a separate authorization. Each category is **Recommended, awaiting owner approval** and **Requires legal review**. Any missing purge, redaction, vendor handling or reconciliation **Requires technical implementation**. For each approved schedule record the start event, maximum active and backup windows, field/object scope, reviewer, hold rule and release evidence. Physical deletion is separate from expiration. For historic records or backups, also document how remaining copies are access-restricted and expire.

Numerical planning ranges need a supported purpose or an applicable legal rule. The technical windows listed below are observed behavior, not recommended retention periods. Texas nonprofit financial books and reports have a possible corporate-specific minimum of three years after fiscal year end under Tex. BOC § 22.352, subject to its scope and exceptions. If the organization becomes an employer, IRS guidance generally calls for keeping employment tax records at least four years after the tax becomes due or is paid, whichever is later. Apply these only to applicable corporate records after counsel or tax review. The EU storage-limitation principle and the FTC's COPPA retention requirements, where applicable, warrant purpose-based periods, not a universal lifespan. Sources accessed November 20, 2026. ## 7. L3 acceptance criteria to approve before implementation

- **Intake, verification and status.** Test that a legitimate member, a locked-out user and an authorized agent have approved paths; malicious third-party claims do not expose whether a member exists. Operation-level authorization and fresh assurance apply to disclosure or irreversible actions. The user sees jurisdiction-aware deadlines, statuses, reasons and an escalation channel in supported languages. Action state does not rely on client-supplied identity or viewing the status screen.
- **Access, correction and export.** Produce a category manifest against an exact scope and snapshot. Refer disputes about verification decisions or shared immutable records to a recorded correction or annotation path. Ensure approved changes reach Auth metadata where needed, public representations, caches and derivatives. Test expiring, revocable retrieval and purge. Exercise redaction of third-party names, reviewer evidence, organization/group scope, stale tokens, and protected ballots with independent expected-result fixtures.
- **Closure, restrictions and deletion.** Keep account restriction, sign-out, privacy holds and deletion as separate statuses. Participants can request closure even if ordinary content access is restricted through the approved route. Preview effects across sole ownership, related records, pending transactions and last-administrator continuity. Test cancellation before the irreversible boundary, concurrency with uploads and changes, idempotent worker retries and verified no post-deletion access.
- **Evidence and privacy controls.** Authorize hold entry, renewal, access and release independently. Record minimal reasons without disclosing reporter or other voter material; test authorized retained evidence is still available after erasure and ordinary reads cannot reveal it. Prove any de-identification by reviewing cross-record linkage and small cohorts rather than by trusting a null foreign key.
- **Providers, media and restoration.** Track DB, Auth, Storage, provider logs and backup outcomes separately. Reconcile by authorized keys, simulate provider rejection and delayed rotation, and verify that recovery cannot make an erased account, private file or stale grant visible again. Preserve operational evidence of failures and remediation without embedding private data.
- **Completion and release.** Completion records show exactly what was corrected, exported, erased, retained or pending, with minimized audit fields, source attestations, any in-scope review and an appeal path. Test partial and repeated requests, expiry, rate limits, cross-account denial and evidence-redacted notifications. Use synthetic local or rollback-only testing until authorized. Policy/owner approval, counsel review and independently verified operational capability all precede publication.
