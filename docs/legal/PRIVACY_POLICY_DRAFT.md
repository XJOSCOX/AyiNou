## 3. Who may receive information

- **Other users and visitors** may see information published to their authorized audience. Public content can be accessed, saved or republished by people outside Ayinou. Group visibility, audience changes and removal apply to supported service projections.
- **Resource managers and reviewers** may see the records needed for their assigned role in that community, institution, project, verification or moderation process. General platform administration does not automatically confer access to private cases, evidence or ballots. An institutional role should not reveal unrelated staff data or personal credentials.
- **Service providers** may process information needed to run assigned infrastructure under approved scopes and agreements. Current development uses Supabase for authentication, PostgreSQL and private Storage. Cloudflare manages the domain's DNS. Production application hosting and the complete production processor list are undecided. Visiting a linked external site is a separate disclosure to that site.
- **Legally authorized recipients** may receive limited data only under the eventual documented procedure for valid requests, lawful disclosures and necessary safety escalation. ### Publication, consent and audience changes

Submitting something to a review queue does not make it public. For example, a member's public badge can disclose an approved claim while the reasons and evidence remain restricted. An authorized institution and a consenting representative can appear together under the current display rules without publishing the representative's private information. A member can change eligible visibility settings or withdraw applicable publicity consent. Previously visible snapshots, evidence held for review and copies kept by independent parties may have different dispositions.

### Limited processing for trust and safety

Scoped review may require a history sufficient to understand the action, prior decisions or conflicts. Reviewers should use the records that relate to the matter and avoid copying unrelated personal information into decisions or notices. Reporting someone does not ordinarily expose your identity in a public finding. The availability and scope of disclosure in response to an appeal, legally valid request or safety need require approved rules. Report access, moderator permissions and verification-review permissions are separate.

## 4. Cookies and security

Session state supports authentication and sign-out. Site preferences include language, theme and Feed mode. Clearing data on a shared device can remove local preferences or an active local session without deleting account records. Other devices can retain their own state. See the [Cookie & Tracking Notice](COOKIE_AND_TRACKING_NOTICE_DRAFT.md) for named current storage and items pending deployment review.

Security controls include live-session checks, scoped authorization, recent authenticator verification for sensitive operations, limited media access and protected audits. They reduce risk and do not ensure that credentials, devices or disclosures will never be compromised. Do not share passwords, one-time codes or raw identity records with staff or through ordinary support. [Approval required — secure incident channel, provider incident responsibilities, notices, timelines and support for inaccessible accounts.]

## 5. Expiry, retention and evidence

Technical expiry can end access to a link, hide an old notification, close an invitation or remove an eligible temporary attachment. It does not establish an approved legal retention schedule. Moderation removal can leave a tombstone, a restricted report, an appeal record or historical references. Project revisions and voting aggregates have their own relationship to underlying records. An active legal or evidence hold, if separately authorized, preserves records within scope until its approved review and release. Existing product evidence protections and synthetic holds do not establish a staffed general legal-hold program.

The current notification interface uses a 30-day display window, and some recommendation snapshots expire after ten minutes. These windows describe specific product behavior. A notice disappearing from view does not establish that it has been removed from every store. A record subject to an applicable preservation decision may remain restricted beyond ordinary display.

Closure, logical removal, deletion of object bytes, retention exceptions and provider rotation require separate checks. An account deletion at the authentication provider alone cannot establish erasure across the database, Storage, logged or retained evidence, copies held by other people or backups. Provider and backup deletion has not been verified. ## 6. Product controls, synthetic testing and future requests

**Current product controls.** Eligible members may edit profile or account settings, update discoverability, manage interests and recommendation feedback, sign out of sessions and withdraw participation or remove content where supported. Each action applies to the specified account setting, resource or relationship.

**Synthetic testing.** The development system exercises intake, scoped receipts, field-allowlisted encrypted export, dependency planning, test holds and marker recovery on fictional accounts. No current real request intake, full real-user export, live account closure or account erasure service is enabled. Synthetic settings do not define consumer entitlements, export deadlines or universal retention.

**Future requests.** A real process would need accessible submission, proportionate identity and representative checks, approved request categories, exceptions and deadlines, safe status updates, independent appeals where applicable and controlled delivery, retention and erasure. [Approval required — staffed channel, treatment of people who cannot sign in, jurisdiction-specific options, complaints and oversight authorities.] Nothing in the draft restricts rights that apply under law.

**Unresolved legal and operational decisions.** Counsel must review duties in each applicable jurisdiction, including processing grounds, vendor agreements, rights and proof, exceptions, authorized channels, deadlines and retention. The initial assessment markets do not receive identical assumed rights or eligibility rules.

## 7. Age, policy changes and contacts

An adult-only, invitation-only pilot is intended. The current product does not enforce the proposed age or invitation limits. Until approved procedures exist, do not use the service to collect personal information about minors through a project, group or institutional profile.
