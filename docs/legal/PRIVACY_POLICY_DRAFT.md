## 2. Information we handle and why

| Information | Where it comes from and what we use it for |

| **Account, authentication and sessions** | You give an email address and password to register or sign in. Supabase Auth handles credentials, email confirmation, recovery, sessions and any authenticator enrollment. Ayinou uses an account identifier and live session checks to connect activity to your account and protect access. Passwords are handled by Auth and kept out of Ayinou profile tables. |
| **Private and public profile information** | Registration sends first and last names, display name and preferred language to Supabase Auth as signup metadata. Your member profile keeps its own private and public fields. You can provide a membership category and onboarding answers and choose a username, biography, avatar and discoverability settings. Ayinou uses these to set up your account, let you control visibility, and display eligible profile information. |
| **Optional geography and activity** | You can self-report country and applicable department or diaspora information. Ayinou does not require GPS or a street address for a member profile. Broad self-reported information and qualifying daily activity markers help generate public statistics with minimum-cohort and complementary suppression. Your group memberships do not prove where you live. Avoid sharing a precise or dangerous location in free text or media. |

Recommendations keep short-lived snapshots, recheck current visibility and let you provide or reset feedback. Blocks and mutes affect applicable interaction and display. The related private settings are not a public list. |
| **Member and referral codes** | The service generates a private member reference and a shareable referral code. A code supplied as signup metadata can be recorded and later confirmed as a referral relationship. The codes are used for account reference or attribution and cannot authenticate, verify, reward or grant a role. |

Reviewers record assignments, decisions and notes. Group access, contacts and representative authority are managed separately from profile publication. The verification workflow accepts permitted public references. Keep raw identification, private documents, access credentials and other sensitive material out of submissions. |

## 3. Audiences and access

Some pages and records can be viewed without signing in. An eligible discoverable profile may show your public name, username, biography, avatar and current badge. Eligible public posts and approved opt-in projects can appear outside an account. Other material is available only to the selected audience, your account, people in a permitted group or project, or current institutional representatives with the necessary role. Separate consent and eligibility checks apply to representative, affiliation and ambassador directories. An institution may list its own public profile information while keeping contacts private.

Staff access depends on role and case assignment. Restricted functions use recent authenticator checks where required. Reports, evidence links, private contact information and decisions have defined private audiences. Evidence may be read by an eligible assigned reviewer, and an applicant can see appropriate status and decisions for their own case. Notifications go to their recipient and can hide details when the underlying item becomes unavailable. A signed-out viewer can still see eligible public pages after a member blocks an account. People who saw or downloaded content may keep copies.

## 5. Cookies and browser storage

Ayinou uses cookies to maintain sessions and restore authentication. Locale is kept through browser storage and a cookie, and an account can also store its preferred language. Theme and Feed selection can persist on the device. Clearing browser data can remove local preferences or end a local session without deleting server-side records. ## 6. Security

Account, role and scope checks protect private operations. Sensitive actions can require an authenticator. Private files use server-authorized delivery or time-limited signed links. Post and project media can be served only after checking access, and uploaded images are reencoded. The service uses rate limits, moderation controls and auditable actions to help protect accounts and content. Security controls reduce risk. Access changes cannot recall copies already received, and issued signed URLs can remain usable until expiry. Report a security or privacy concern through [L1-02 approved channel].

## 7. Retention and deletion

The service keeps information for operation and account history, with separate behavior for different records. Examples of technical limits are a 30-day notification display window, ten-minute recommendation snapshots, expiring invitations, and expiry or cleanup mechanisms for eligible unattached media. Display expiry and access revocation can happen before physical deletion. Eligible post or comment removal can hide content while preserving tombstones, reports or appeal evidence. Deleting an account through infrastructure can remove some dependent rows, detach some identifiers, or leave records protected by separate rules. Some ballot or audit history may remain with a removed account reference. Backup, provider and legal hold handling require separate procedures.

## 8. Your controls, requests and account closure

You can edit eligible profile information, change discoverability, update interests, manage recommendation feedback, control in-app notifications, and use available account and security settings. You can remove eligible content or withdraw from participation where the relevant workflow allows. These actions affect the specific record or access setting. Ayinou currently lacks a self-service full account deletion, portable data export or privacy request tracking workflow.
