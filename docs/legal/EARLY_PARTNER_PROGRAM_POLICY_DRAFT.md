# Ayinou Early Partner Program Policy

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

## 1. Future proposal and selection

An Early Partner Program is proposed for a limited pilot after explicit approval. No organization is identified as accepted or already onboarded. An eligible formal or informal organization would need an identifiable responsible representative, a credible public or community mandate, a compatible use case and capacity to handle participant privacy, feedback and safety. Selection should use published criteria, conflicts disclosure and a written decision. An interest conversation or invitation to discuss participation is not acceptance. [Approval required — who may select partners, which markets, capacity, selection criteria and dispute handling.]

## 2. Onboarding and testing

A separate pilot agreement would identify allowed features, supported onboarding, designated contacts and permitted data. Partners would use named individual accounts, scoped roles and existing authentication and verification standards. Early access should start with approved test or limited operational data and permit rapid access removal. Participants should report a problem privately through the agreed channel and avoid uploading secret credentials, beneficiary records, private test artifacts or sensitive case material. Confidentiality rules should permit applicable legal disclosures, good-faith safety reporting and independent review.

## 3. Feedback, publicity and limits

Collect only feedback needed to evaluate the proposed pilot. Clarify what can be shared with reviewers and obtain separate permission before using names, logos, testimonials or results publicly. Partner status would not grant a verification badge, project-review preference, extra vote, financial support, health services, sponsorship or authority to use Ayinou branding. [Approval required — participation agreement, feedback use, secure access and a written exit procedure.]

## 4. Suspension and exit

The program would need an accessible way for either party to end participation, revoke pilot grants and account for test copies and applicable preservation duties. Temporary restriction could respond to a safety or security concern under approved procedures. Explain what is ending, who can review disputes and which records remain under approved retention. A partner's eligibility should be revisited when its control, representative, risk or program activities change.
