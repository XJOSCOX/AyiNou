# Ayinou nonprofit governance and transition plan

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

Proposed model for a future board-managed Texas nonprofit corporation. The owner and qualified counsel must approve the structure, governing documents and appointees. Legal sources accessed November 20, 2026. [Structure research and planning facts](NONPROFIT_STRUCTURE_RESEARCH.md)

## Board, leadership and decision authority

| Role or decision | Recommended design and controls |
| --- | --- |
| Board of directors | Reserve responsibility for mission, annual budget, risk, chief executive oversight, major contracts, restricted assets, compensation, policy approval, amendments and dissolution. For a board-managed corporation, Texas requires at least three directors. Recommend a majority independent of the founder, compensated leadership and material vendors, with Haitian community perspective, financial competence, and program or safety expertise. The exact size, initial appointees, qualifications and selection are pending. |
| Legal membership | Recommend no statutory voting members at formation, with language in the certificate if approved. Test this against the owner's desired community voice. An alternative voting-member corporation offers formal accountability but adds election, notice, quorum and member-rights obligations. Keep community input through distinct advisory channels under either option. |
| Founder | Recognize mission and product stewardship. A board appointment or paid executive position would follow the same approval, conflict, evaluation and succession rules as others. Avoid unreviewed permanent control, unilateral appointment/removal, sole signatory authority or guaranteed compensation. |
| Appointment and removal | With counsel, define who appoints directors and fills vacancies, terms and staggering, term limits, grounds and vote for removal, notice, quorum, voting thresholds, written actions, delegation and records. If there are legal members, define their separate powers. Know who can act when recusals leave too few eligible decision-makers. |
| Officers and staff | Texas requires a president and secretary, held by different people. Recommend naming a treasurer and separating finance approval and reconciliation. Executive leadership implements the annual plan within a written delegation. The board retains appointment, evaluation and authorized removal. |
| Delegation | Adopt a register of delegated permissions and financial limits for officers, teams and any committees. Specify approvals for staff, contracts, expenses, rights requests, incidents, reviewer appointment, and vendor access. Require dual control for sensitive actions, backup coverage and documented emergency escalation with later review. |
| Platform roles | A platform administrator, moderator or reviewer has scoped technical powers. Corporate office requires a separate documented corporate appointment. Ambassador status and institutional verification confer no nonprofit authority. Platform membership, affiliation and advisory votes do not elect corporate officers or change signing authority. Corporate leaders also need an authorized app role and normal MFA checks to act inside the product. |

For statutory requirements and exceptions, review Texas Business Organizations Code Chapter 22, including §§ 22.202–22.204 and 22.231. The independence, succession and dual-approval rules above are recommendations. [Texas Chapter 22](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/GetStatute.aspx?Code=BO&Value=22) · [Texas nonprofit FAQs](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/nonprofitfaqs.shtml) · [Platform authorization](../ARCHITECTURE.md)

## Board policies to prepare

- **Conflicts and related parties.** Collect initial and annual disclosures and require updates before each affected decision. Include family, employment, vendor, founder and funding interests. Log disclosure, recusal and departure from deliberation. Have disinterested directors compare market options and document why the transaction serves the charity, the terms, relevant votes and monitoring. Apply this to any transfer, licence or contract involving the founder or another related party. Counsel should review Texas § 22.230 and federal private-benefit and excess-benefit rules. [Texas Chapter 22](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/GetStatute.aspx?Code=BO&Value=22) · [IRS rebuttable presumption guidance](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/rebuttable-presumption-intermediate-sanctions)
- **Reasonable compensation.** Recommend advance approval of compensation and benefits by an authorized disinterested body, with comparable data and contemporaneous records. Review reimbursements, severance, founder IP compensation and in-kind benefits. Compensation serves documented work and must be supportable. Prohibit distributions that impermissibly benefit insiders. Consider recusal where pay changes for related persons. [IRS compensation guidance](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/rebuttable-presumption-intermediate-sanctions)
- **Financial oversight.** Approve a budget, bank signatories, payment limits and restricted-fund controls. Segregate initiation, approval and reconciliation or install equivalent independent review. Require financial reports to directors, expense evidence, gift-acceptance criteria, annual return review and an audit/review decision as scale and applicable requirements warrant. Add screening, agreements and monitoring before funding partners abroad. [IRS public charity compliance guide, February 2024](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p4221pc.pdf)
- **Whistleblower and safeguarding.** Provide a confidential route to an independent director or designated outside recipient, with recusal, anti-retaliation, limited evidence access, investigation and board follow-up. Include staff and volunteer safety. Avoid promises of absolute confidentiality, guaranteed protection or continuous monitoring. Have counsel evaluate duties and applicable protections. [IRS Form 990 instructions, governance questions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i990)
- **Recordkeeping and retention.** Preserve approved governing instruments, resolutions, minutes, disclosures, tax records and supporting financial evidence with access and version controls. Distinguish corporate records from member data. Texas § 22.352 contains a three-year maintenance/public-inspection rule for specified financial records and reports after fiscal year end, subject to statutory scope and exceptions. Confirm applicability and never treat it as a universal purge date. Design a hold and destruction workflow with scope, owner, review date, release and evidence of deletion. Define document access and protected copies. [Texas Chapter 22](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/GetStatute.aspx?Code=BO&Value=22) · [L2 retention planning](LEGAL_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md)
- **Mission control and dissolution.** Approve an annual program narrative, use board review for official civic activity, and track staff, resource use and related-party benefits. On dissolution use counsel-approved processes for liabilities, restricted assets, notifications and distributions consistent with applicable law and exempt purposes. Plan archival continuity or wind-down, remaining privacy requests and secure disposition of application and vendor data. [IRS Form 1023 instructions, December 2024](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023)

## Transition checklist from development to the future entity

| Gate | Required decision or verification | Evidence to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Authorization | Owner authorizes counsel work and confirms independent Texas nonprofit direction. Check candidate legal names, domain and marks independently. Choose registered agent and qualifying office. | Owner decisions, name clearance, agent consent, counsel-approved certificate and bylaws. |
| 2. Formation and organization | File only under separate authorization. After state formation hold organizational action, seat approved directors and officers, approve delegations, compliance calendar and startup budget. Obtain own EIN after legal formation. | Filed formation evidence, signed minutes, roles and terms, EIN confirmation in restricted records. |
| 3. Tax and finance | Ask counsel to assess IRS exemption and public charity classification and Texas exemptions. Arrange banking and documented controls. Plan scope and registrations before any solicitation or cross-border disbursement. | Determinations, approved tax calendar, bank mandate and compliant solicitation review. |
| 4. Asset diligence | Inventory actual holders and terms of the domain registrar/DNS account, GitHub repository, code, mark/logo, images, third-party libraries and contractor contributions. Determine whether assignment or documented licensing is appropriate, including conflicts and consideration. | Proven title or usage rights, signed and disinterested approvals, signed instruments, access recovery. Domain/DNS access alone is insufficient evidence of ownership. |
| 5. Services and responsibilities | Review current individual or organizational terms for Supabase and other current accounts. Select production hosting, SMTP and monitoring. Determine whether new accounts, novation or permission are required and approve authorized signatories. Set least privilege, MFA, recovery access and a vendor register. | Reviewed contracts, due diligence, approved signatories, provider regions and subprocessors, testable backup and restore evidence. |
| 6. Personal data transition | Identify who is responsible for current development data and who will control production processing. Decide whether legacy data can lawfully move and what notice, rights treatment or segregation applies. Verify migration scope, hold coverage, preservation and audit. Follow a separately authorized transfer plan if warranted. | Owner and counsel decisions, approved notices or legal bases, signed data terms, access and deletion verification. |
| 7. Release review | Have directors or authorized approvers confirm rights support, reviewer and administrator staffing, public disclosures, qualified translations, identity continuity, safety and restoration. Record rollout and correction responsibilities. | Signed gates, release packet and approvals. |

Property transfers, account changes and filings require their own approval and execution. [Owner approval brief](OWNER_DECISION_BRIEF.md) · [IRS information for exemption applicants](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/information-for-organizations-applying-for-tax-exempt-status)
