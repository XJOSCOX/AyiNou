## Age and account eligibility options

Current signup sends email/password and user-supplied display name, names, preferred locale and optional referral metadata to Supabase Auth. The reviewed signup has no approved age or guardian workflow. TOTP establishes possession of an account factor. Profile and claim reviews serve other purposes. Neither one is an existing age assurance system. [Registration source](../../apps/web/src/components/auth-screen.tsx) · [Privacy inventory](../audits/PRIVACY_DATA_INVENTORY.md)

| Option | Benefits | Limitations and prerequisites |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Adults-only pilot** | Simpler first launch for mixed public/private groups, volunteers, nominations and community votes. Lower exposure while protection and reviewer capacity are developed. | Excludes younger users. “Adult” may require a market-specific rule. A checkbox or terms statement alone does not resolve child-directed design, actual knowledge, verification, or reporting. Determine proportionate assurance, suspected-minor review, accommodation, learn/browse access and existing-account treatment. |
| **Minimum age 16** | Includes older youth who can benefit from learning opportunities and civic engagement. | Requires child-specific design, restricted projects and geolocation/media handling, human safety review and consent or guardian analysis for each market. Age 16 does not uniformly establish capacity to accept terms or authorize all processing. |
| **Minimum age 13 with restrictions** | Widens educational access. | Highest support and safety burden among the proposed floors. Require child-protective defaults, separate adult/youth interactions, restricted discovery, volunteering and voting, proportionate assurance, reporting, guardian flows where needed, and protections for shared evidence. A 13-year floor is not a general compliance result. |
| **Country-specific floors or controls** | Can reflect local consent, capacity and platform requirements. | Location and travel signals can be unreliable. Policy complexity increases risk of inconsistent treatment and unnecessary location or identity collection. Require a documented market rule table, appeal/correction, consistent localized notices and audit. |

**Recommendation awaiting approval.** Pilot with adults in a specifically approved, counsel-reviewed set of markets and limit invites until staffing and proportionate eligibility controls are tested. Evaluate an explicit reference floor such as 18 only as part of that decision and apply any higher applicable requirement. Support an accessible route to correct an erroneous restriction. Avoid routine retention of exact dates of birth or government ID absent documented necessity and approval. Design a safe workflow for credible reports of underage use, access and data handling, limited exceptions for preservation, and escalation. Treat volunteer safeguarding and project eligibility as additional decisions.

COPPA generally addresses covered child-directed online services and covered operators with actual knowledge they collect personal information from children under 13. First assess coverage, including the operator's status. The amended rule has notice, consent, security and retention requirements where applicable. GDPR Art. 8 concerns relying on consent for an information society service offered directly to a child, sets 16 as the default and allows national floors down to 13. Neither standard supplies a universal account age. Canadian consent and capacity questions also need province-specific review. [FTC COPPA guidance and rule materials](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions) · [GDPR Art. 8](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) · [Québec statute](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cs/P-39.1)

## Data handling before market approval

1. Establish the actual operator, legal roles, market targeting, information flows across users, staff and vendors, and operating locations. Cloudflare DNS management and development Supabase are confirmed for their stated roles. Production DNS/proxy use, host, SMTP, analytics, identity or moderation vendors, regions, backups and contracts need separate evidence.
2. Verify the specific products, provider/subprocessor regions, operator agreements and control of accounts. Evaluate transfer restrictions, required mechanisms, access by support and backup retention. Standard vendor terms do not establish selected configurations or agreements. [Supabase customer DPA](https://supabase.com/legal/customer-resources/data-processing-addendum)
3. Map each purpose to legal authority or basis, market text, verified access, retention and request outcome. Review group membership, verification links, volunteered identity/location, politically sensitive content, project evidence and private votes separately. Confirm whether specific exemptions, representative obligations and complaints routes apply.
4. Verify a functioning operator contact, incident/escalation and rights workflow, staffing in required languages, age/guardian decision, notices, consumer terms and regional rollout controls. Record an approve, limit, or defer decision by market and date the legal assessment. Reassess before introducing new markets, providers or features.
