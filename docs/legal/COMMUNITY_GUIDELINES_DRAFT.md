# Ayinou Community Guidelines

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

- **Proposed effective date and version** [L1-06]
- **Safety and support contact** [L1-02]

Ayinou is for peaceful community discussion and cooperation among people in Haiti, the diaspora and international supporters. These rules apply to profiles, posts, replies, groups, images, institutional activity, project workspaces and polls, including restricted spaces. Community managers may add rules within their authority. These guidelines apply across political viewpoints. Criticism of ideas, public actions and institutions and good-faith disagreement belong here. Targeting people for abuse does not.

## Treat people with respect

- Discuss the issue. Do not harass, stalk, threaten, celebrate abuse against, or organize others to target a member or another person. Respect blocks, requests to stop contact and limits on access. Do not use alternate accounts or institutional roles to get around them.
- Do not attack, dehumanize, threaten or promote exclusion of people based on characteristics such as race, ethnicity, national origin, language, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or other protected status. You can discuss discrimination, quote only what is necessary to document it, and dispute public ideas without endorsing attacks.
- Do not call for violence or share instructions, targets or live updates intended to enable an attack. Do not share or solicit child sexual abuse material, sexual exploitation, nonconsensual intimate material, or material that exploits a person in danger. Use the report option and avoid making or spreading copies. In an emergency, use an appropriate local emergency or safety service.

## Represent people and organizations accurately

- Do not impersonate a person, public figure, employer, organization or public institution. Make any parody or commentary clear to its audience and avoid misleading names, logos or claims of authority. Institutions and representatives must identify their actual role and stay within current permissions.
- Do not present a membership, affiliation, invitation, unreviewed profile, administrator role or ambassador designation as proof of someone's identity, office or authority. A verification badge indicates the status of a specific reviewed claim. It does not certify the truth of the member's statements or confer greater voting power. Official Ayinou department spaces are platform-run community spaces.
- Do not distort a source or materially misrepresent an image, quote or event to deceive people. Offer relevant sources for consequential claims where practical. Label uncertainty, clearly distinguish firsthand observations from secondhand reports, and correct mistakes that materially affect others.

## Protect privacy, consent and safety

- Share only information and media you have permission to share. Keep private group, verification, volunteer and project records within their intended audience. Do not re-publish private messages or records obtained elsewhere, private identity information, protected media or another person's account details without a legitimate basis and permission where required.
- Do not publish private addresses, precise coordinates, identifying images or live movements that could expose a child, survivor, vulnerable person or endangered group. Remove identifying details before posting about a crisis or sensitive event. Consider whether clothing, faces, landmarks or timing could reveal someone even after image metadata is stripped.
- Respect rights in photos, videos, writing, logos and other material. Attribution alone may not provide permission. Do not share copyrighted material without authorization or an applicable exception, or circulate intimate or otherwise exploitative media. [L1-08 approve a rights complaint procedure.] Keep raw IDs, credentials and private documents out of verification links and public posts.

## Participate honestly

- Do not use spam, deceptive links, referral abuse, bought interactions, coordinated inauthentic engagement, or automated activity that overloads the service or evades limits. Do not use misleading reports or manipulated screenshots to remove another member or suppress disagreement.
- Describe project ownership, scope, qualifications, volunteer terms and outcomes accurately. Do not present a proposal as approved before review or represent approval as a guarantee, official licence or proof of financial legitimacy. Do not use projects or volunteer opportunities to defraud, coerce or exploit people. Disclose material conflicts to the appropriate reviewer and keep private volunteer records out of public updates.
- Cast only your own eligible vote and respect the rules of the poll. Do not buy votes, coerce participants, coordinate fake accounts, try to reveal someone's private choice, or pressure nominees to participate. Do not present an Ayinou poll as an official election or as granting a role, appointment or project approval. People may disagree about a result or the rules in good faith.

## Reports, enforcement and appeals

Use a report control on eligible content or profiles where it is available. Give the specific item and relevant context, using the minimum private information needed. Do not post sensitive evidence publicly or send it through ordinary support messages. You can block or mute an account through the available controls. Blocking affects eligible account interactions, and content that remains public may still be visible to signed-out visitors.

Authorized staff may hide, restore or remove eligible content, restrict an account or institutional profile, or revoke a verification claim through the relevant workflow. [L1-09 approve the severity factors, notice process, handling of repeat violations, review targets and examples of urgent responses.] Report counts alone do not decide whether a violation occurred. Moderator access, conflicts of interest and evidence handling are subject to the applicable rules.

Current in-app appeals cover eligible current hides of your own posts or comments and eligible written verification rejections or revocations. Each has an applicable 14-day filing window and independent-review criteria. A verification reconsideration allows fresh review and does not by itself restore a badge or role. Other account or group decisions do not all have in-app appeal paths. Use [L1-02 and L1-09 approved channel and procedure] for matters outside those flows. Staff availability can delay a review.
