# Ayinou Community Guidelines

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

## 1. Scope

These rules apply to posts, comments, media, profiles, institution and group activity, projects, volunteer listings, polls, nominations and use of reports or appeals. They apply to private spaces as well as public ones. Managers can add consistent local rules for their own community. Platform safety rules and applicable law still apply. A local manager cannot grant permission to expose private evidence or retaliate against a reporter. [Terms & Conditions](TERMS_DRAFT.md)

## 2. Debate and criticism

Criticize ideas, policies, institutions and conduct. Share relevant context and distinguish something you observed from an inference, unverified allegation or opinion. Peaceful political, social, religious and professional disagreement is welcome. Criticism of officials or organizations, satire presented clearly, and unpopular opinions are not grounds for sanctions by themselves. Correct an error when you learn of it. Do not turn criticism into a targeted campaign against an individual.

## 3. Harassment, threats and discrimination

Do not threaten violence, call for harm, celebrate abuse to intimidate a target, or recruit others to harass someone. Unwanted repeated contact, targeted insults, threats about someone's family, retaliation, and coordinated pile-ons can amount to harassment. Do not use a person's protected or vulnerable status to dehumanize them, advocate exclusion or violence, or encourage targeted discrimination. Documenting abuse or quoting it for criticism can be appropriate when context is clear and unnecessary details are removed. A moderator should consider context, target, severity and realistic safety risk.

## 4. Identity, institutional authority and manipulation

Use names, affiliations and marks truthfully. Do not impersonate a person, official body, organization, moderator or professional. An ordinary membership, an invited role, an administrator mark or a partnership interest is not proof that you hold a public office or speak for an organization. Disclose parody or commentary when a reasonable reader could mistake it for the real source. Do not buy or coordinate fake accounts, reactions, referrals, votes or testimonials. Do not evade an access restriction with another account, another person's credentials or a nominally separate organization profile.

Coordinating a legitimate civic campaign, asking people to join a discussion or sharing a public call for volunteers can be appropriate. Using accounts under common control to create the appearance of independent support, bribing people for an endorsement, or directing a group to mass-report a critic is manipulation. Do not flood discussions with repeated offers, distribute malicious links or disguise marketing or a payment request as official support. Claims that a channel is endorsed or mandatory should be supported by the actual organization's instructions.

## 5. Privacy, location and consent

Do not publish another person's address, private contact details, precise movements, identifying personal records, credentials or private communications without authority. Avoid identifying survivors, minors, aid seekers or people at a protected site, including through indirect combinations such as an image and a location. Don't post intimate material without consent or redistribute private photos or recordings to embarrass someone. Crop, blur or omit sensitive identifying details when documenting a public-interest event. Consent to attend a project or appear once in a photo does not extend to later publicity or disclosure of private records. [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md)

Use special care with shelter locations, routes used by vulnerable people, schools, medical visits and identifiable reports of violence. Avoid maps, landmarks, timestamps or images that would reveal a location even if the exact address is omitted. A report can identify the content through its link and explain the problem without copying the sensitive information into a public thread. Moderators should remove unnecessary details from any explanation that viewers can see.

## 6. Exploitation and safeguarding

Do not facilitate abuse, grooming, coercion, trafficking, sexual exploitation or exploitation of people seeking assistance. Do not exchange access, a project assignment, votes or claimed services for money, images, personal data or favors. Respect safeguarding instructions and use available reporting tools if someone may be at risk. Do not investigate by soliciting further harmful material or sharing it. The service does not offer staffed emergency dispatch. [Approval required — secure safeguarding channel, escalation authority, legal reporting duties and handling of evidence.]

## 7. Honest projects, volunteering and advice

Do not misstate funding, authority, qualifications, partnerships, intended beneficiaries or results. Do not use a project listing, private message or invitation to solicit unauthorized donations, fees, insurance payments, gift cards or credentials. Do not promise a job, aid, a visa, medical treatment or another result you cannot lawfully provide. Personal experiences and good-faith discussion are allowed. Avoid presenting unqualified medical guidance as professional care, urging dangerous actions, or directing people away from appropriate help based on an unsupported claim. Clearly label estimates and self-reported outcomes. [Project & Volunteer Policy](PROJECT_AND_VOLUNTEER_POLICY_DRAFT.md)

## 8. Honest polls, reports and intellectual-property complaints

Do not coerce a person to show a ballot, purchase or swap votes, submit ballots through multiple accounts, impersonate a nominee or use a poll as proof of a legally binding result. In a report, identify the problem, give the relevant facts and distinguish what you know from what you suspect. Good-faith reports may be mistaken. Fabricated allegations, deliberate omissions to mislead a reviewer, repeated retaliatory reports and pressure on witnesses can be addressed separately. Use the approved intellectual-property process for actual rights claims. [Voting & Polling Policy](VOTING_AND_POLLING_POLICY_DRAFT.md) · [Terms & Conditions](TERMS_DRAFT.md)

## 9. Moderation decisions

Available actions vary by resource and reviewer authority. A proportionate response may include correction, a warning where appropriate, reduced visibility, removal, scoped access restrictions or loss of an account or institution's privileges. A credible immediate safety risk can justify an emergency restriction pending review. Reviewers should consider severity, evidence, how broadly content spread, prior related conduct and the risk of harm. Repeated violations can warrant stronger measures. Popularity, reports alone or disagreement with the operator are insufficient grounds. Keep restricted evidence and identity information out of public explanations. Provide an applicable written reason and a route to challenge when authorized and safe. [Approval required — severity standards, staffing, written notices, escalation and urgent response.]

Where a correction addresses the harm, prefer correcting the disputed material or narrowing its audience to a broad account restriction. A threatening disclosure of a protected location may require immediate concealment and restricted evidence preservation. Document why a limited action would be insufficient before seeking a wider restriction. A temporary measure should be reviewed before extending it. Apply the appropriate privacy boundary when a rule violation affects another person, an organization or a private community. A decision in one resource does not by itself confer authority to act in other resources.

## 10. Appeals and review limits

Eligible current hides of one's own posts or comments and eligible written verification denials or revocations have separate in-app appeal paths, each with its existing 14-day filing window. The applicable workflow governs scope and independence. A verification reconsideration allows fresh review and does not itself restore the claim or any permissions. Other decisions may lack an in-app appeal. [Approval required — alternative channel and independent assignment, conflict handling, language support and reasonable response targets.] Availability depends on staffed coverage. Do not share restricted material publicly to dispute a decision.
