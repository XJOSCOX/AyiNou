## Five distinct approvals and obligations

1. **State formation.** Verify corporate name availability, secure registered agent consent and a qualifying Texas registered office, and approve the certificate, bylaws and initial governance. Texas Form 202 is a starting point. Its general-purpose language may require tailoring for federal recognition. State formation, acquisition of an EIN and board resolutions have distinct effects. [Texas Form 202 instructions](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/instructions/202.shtml)
2. **Federal income-tax exemption.** Counsel should establish both organizing-document limits and a real operating plan for charitable or educational purposes. Protect charitable assets on dissolution, avoid private inurement and impermissible private benefit, evaluate public charity classification and support projections, and document activities, finances and compensation. Use Form 1023 unless the organization qualifies and counsel chooses Form 1023-EZ after reviewing the eligibility worksheet. Confirm application timing and potential effective date before making any statement about deductibility. Calendar the applicable annual filing even while an application is pending as advised. Three consecutive missed required annual filings trigger automatic revocation for organizations subject to that rule. [IRS Form 1023 instructions, December 2024](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023) · [Form 1023-EZ instructions, January 2025](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023ez) · [IRS exemption requirements](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/exemption-requirements-501c3-organizations) · [Automatic revocation](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/automatic-revocation-of-exemption)
3. **Texas tax exemptions.** Match each proposed claim to its basis and to the kinds of taxes it covers. The Comptroller publishes separate qualifying routes, including exemption based on federal status through AP-204 and a qualifying charitable route through AP-205. Applicability can differ for franchise, sales and hotel taxes and for particular transactions. Confirm filings, certificates and effective dates rather than adding a blanket tax-free claim. [Texas Comptroller guidelines](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/publications/96-1045.php) · [AP-204](https://comptroller.texas.gov/forms/ap-204.pdf) · [AP-205](https://comptroller.texas.gov/forms/ap-205.pdf)
4. **Fundraising and solicitation.** Review where and how donations would be solicited, acknowledged and spent. Texas generally does not require most charities to register to solicit, with specific regimes for certain solicitations. Other U.S. states and countries may have their own requirements, including for online solicitation, registrations, renewals and disclosures. Production donations, collection accounts, receipts and fundraising campaigns remain outside the current service. Fiscal sponsorship would require an approved agreement and appropriate control by a qualified sponsor before use. [Texas Attorney General registration and filings](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/divisions/charitable-trusts/registration-and-filings)
5. **International charitable activity.** If operations or grants abroad become concrete, define who holds and controls funds, written charitable restrictions, partner and sanctions checks, monitoring, records, financial controls, local registration and employment or volunteer requirements. Assess the actual Form 990 filer and Schedule F reporting triggers and obtain advice on grant-specific requirements, including when private-foundation rules may apply. Keep digital access approval separate from field operations. [Schedule F instructions, December 2024](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i990sf) · [OFAC program information](https://ofac.treasury.gov/sanctions-programs-and-country-information)

## Proposed mission and operating program

> Ayinou's proposed mission is to advance education and strengthen Haitian communities through peaceful civic participation, skills sharing, volunteer coordination and constructive community projects. It seeks cooperation among people in Haiti, the Haitian diaspora and international supporters, and promotes transparent reporting of project outcomes.

Counsel should decide what belongs in the certificate and draft the required limits and dissolution provisions. The mission above is a working statement. Applying for exemption requires an adequate description of who benefits, how activities serve an exempt purpose and how funds and staff time are used. A public community platform can support educational or charitable work. General social features, a large audience or nonprofit branding alone do not demonstrate exempt operation.

| Program or use | Proposed assessment and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Skills workshops, learning resources and volunteer training | Define intended beneficiaries, selection criteria, materials, accessibility, instructor arrangements and measures of educational outcomes. |
| Community project planning and transparent reporting | Use objective selection and conflict checks, written project scope and separately reviewed publication. Treat approved self-reported metrics as such. Evaluate financial or real-world commitments independently of in-app approval. |
| Civic participation, discussion and polls | Support neutral education and community advisory input. Separate platform users' speech from official organization speech, endorsements, staff activity and spending. Define moderation and recommendations so the charity avoids using its resources to intervene in candidate campaigns. |
| Sponsorship, commercial activity, fundraising, payments, grants or cross-border delivery | Assess relatedness to mission, cost allocation, private benefit and potential unrelated business income. Evaluate separate governance or legal structure when money, restricted gifts, partnerships, regulated services or a materially commercial activity enter the plan. |

A recognized § 501(c)(3) organization is prohibited from intervening for or against candidates for public office. Nonpartisan education is fact-dependent. Lobbying may not be a substantial part of activities under the default test. Ask whether an eligible public charity should elect the § 501(h) expenditure test; keep lobbying records under the selected rules. Apply a review step before official civic communications, candidate events or organized legislative activity. Community polls confer no public office, project approval or political authority.
