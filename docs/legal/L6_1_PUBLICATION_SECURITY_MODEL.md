# L6.1 publication security model

Technical validation does not grant legal approval. The publication gate requires a trusted frozen source digest/version, complete four-language package, legal and jurisdiction review, linguistic review for each locale, product review, owner approval, known legal operator and jurisdiction, operational readiness, approved date, and an independently recorded publication action. Candidate-supplied IDs are insufficient: references must match the separate trusted evidence set for policy, version, locale and digest. Empty production evidence means no candidate can pass. Legal or publication authority is not derived from platform administrator or organization roles.

The gate rejects `DRAFT`, unresolved markers, a limited set of obvious future-feature markers, malformed sections, unapproved links and digest mismatch. Those lexical checks are a defense in depth aid, not a substitute for human product and legal review. They cannot determine the accuracy or completeness of a policy. Future tooling must verify approver identity, separation of duties and source attestation against an independently controlled approval record before filling production evidence. No such authority exists in this phase.

The release JSON is the sole runtime source. L4/L5 drafts and fictional tests are outside it. The registry starts empty, is read-only at runtime, and has no mutation endpoint. `scripts/check-legal-center.mjs` verifies empty production registries, approval failures, source staleness, link restrictions, history immutability and absence of draft or fixture imports from web runtime and public assets. The browser test checks anonymous SSR responses and `noindex`. CI runs both the new gate and L5 source integrity validation on direct pushes to main.

Release activation still needs an operator decision, all 31 open L4 decisions resolved, approved effective dates, human review evidence, and an authorized publishing process. No current policy is published or effective.
