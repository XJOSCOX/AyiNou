# Ayinou Data Rights & Retention Policy

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

## 1. Current controls

Members can edit eligible account and profile fields, manage discoverability, request supported sign-out, adjust notifications or preferences, withdraw an eligible application or affiliation and remove supported content. Each applies to its own record. For example, removing a comment can leave a thread tombstone or restricted appeal evidence. An updated profile does not automatically change original Auth metadata. Hiding a project or institution does not resolve every organizational or project dependency.

Real privacy-rights intake, full export, live closure and account erasure are disabled. Synthetic intake, export, lifecycle, holds and restoration test isolated fictional records. They set no public deadlines, eligibility or delivery rights. Content moderation and verification appeals continue through their own workflows. [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md)

## 2. Future requests and identity proof

The proposed service would receive approved requests through a secure, staffed channel and provide a minimal receipt. Establish the applicable legal rule and whether the requester is the subject or authorized representative. Verify identity proportionately, offer a secure assisted path where normal sign-in is unavailable, confirm agency authority and expiry, record exceptions, limit access, and separate review from execution. Do not request raw evidence by ordinary email. A receipt would confirm the case stage, with appropriate status and independent review or appeal routes where applicable.

Before providing access or exports, review field scope, third-party information, private votes, protected evidence and legal exceptions. Provide only authorized material with approved delivery, revocation, expiry and confidentiality controls. A complete response may require explanation of lawful exclusions and residual records. ## 3. Closure, erasure and provider reconciliation

An authorized future closure procedure should check institutional, project and group ownership, roles, pending appeals, third-party content, historical evidence, shared media and frozen electorate or ballot relationships. Record what is closed or hidden, what can be erased, which references should survive or be de-identified, and what is legally protected. Verify Auth, database, media, logs and relevant external providers separately. Redaction, visibility removal and loss of a live session do not prove erasure. Backups and vendor copies require approved restoration and suppression reconciliation before release. Provider deletion and recovery have not been verified. ## 4. Retention and preservation

| Record class | Question before disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Account, credentials, profile and residence | Which fields serve active access, security, resolution of disputes or another approved purpose? What remains in Auth metadata, logs and protected backups? |
| Content, institution records, groups and roles | Which material belongs to or concerns other people? What access and decisions must survive a transfer, dispute or hold? |
| Verification, safety and privacy case records | Which evidence can be narrowed while preserving independent review, recusal and audit? Which authority can place or release a hold and when must it be reviewed? |
| Project, volunteer and image records | Which history and consent records are necessary? Reconcile project image bytes and links separately from post media and attachments. |
| Ballot, electorate and aggregate history | What may survive with privacy protection? Can a retained combination reveal a private choice or eligibility decision? |
| Providers and backup archives | Which actual retention, restore and deletion mechanisms exist? How is suppression reapplied and independently verified? |

Set a policy version, category, applicable jurisdiction, purpose, approved trigger and duration, exceptions, owner, action and evidence of completion before enforcement. Technical expiry is separate from legal retention. Preservation should be scoped and subject to review. Resolve overlapping holds independently and record release and remaining obligations. Unknown rules, active holds and unresolved cases block destruction until an approved decision. [Retention decision matrix](RETENTION_AND_DELETION_MATRIX.md)
