## 1. About Ayinou and these terms

Ayinou is a community platform for people in Haiti, the Haitian diaspora and international supporters. It gives members places to discuss community issues, participate in groups, propose and organize community projects, and run advisory polls. These terms describe the proposed agreement between you and [L1-01 operator]. The [Community Guidelines](COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES_DRAFT.md) explain the conduct rules. The [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md) explains how information is handled.

## 2. Eligibility, registration and account security

You may create and use an account if you meet [L1-04 minimum age, capacity and any approved guardian conditions] and may use the service in [L1-03 approved markets]. Use your own account, provide accurate information about your identity and authority when requested, and keep your email address current. You may use a public display name consistent with the Guidelines. Do not misrepresent who you are or present another person's information as your own.

Registration uses an email address and password. Email confirmation is part of the registration flow. Use a strong, unique password and protect your account, authenticator and recovery access. Ayinou may require recent authenticator verification for sensitive actions. Use Account Settings to change your password, manage your authenticator and sign out of all sessions. Report suspected account misuse to [L1-02 support contact]. Keep your private member reference private. You may share a referral code as an invitation. Referral attribution carries no reward, account authority or verification status.

## 3. Profiles, identity reviews and badges

Your first and last names supplied in private profile fields are separate from your public username and display name. You can manage profile visibility and discoverability. A visible, discoverable profile can show eligible public information such as a username, display name, biography, avatar and current badges. Review the audience of each profile, post, affiliation or project before sharing. Anyone who already viewed or downloaded material may retain a copy, and an issued signed image link can remain valid until expiry.

You may apply for review of an identity, public-figure or institutional claim or, where offered, a representative or ownership claim. Provide accurate and relevant public evidence references. Keep government ID, private documents, credentials and sensitive evidence out of your submissions. Applications may remain pending when eligible independent reviewers are unavailable. Verification describes the current status of a particular claim. It does not establish truth, government endorsement, general authority, or preferential voting power. An authenticator protects account access. It does not establish a reviewed identity.

## 5. Organizations and representation

Use your personal account to represent an institution only while you have its permission and a current assigned role. Keep institutional information accurate and only use powers within your scope. Do not publish private contacts without permission or claim official authority based on ordinary affiliation, an invitation, or an unreviewed profile. Institution managers can grant or revoke scoped permissions in accordance with their roles. A person may hold multiple affiliations and roles. Ownership review and verification of an institution or representative are separate decisions.

## 6. Communities and groups

Department communities and member-created diaspora and interest groups may set permitted participation and access requirements. Groups may be open, approval-based, or private and invitation-based. Follow posted group rules as well as Ayinou's platform-wide rules. Group managers may administer membership and moderate within their authority. Platform rules and eligibility checks continue to apply to protected discussions and historical content. Joining a community does not confirm your residence, nationality or immigration status.

## 7. Projects and volunteering

Eligible members may create private project drafts. Submitting a proposal requires current independently reviewed personal identity and any applicable institution or community authority. Email confirmation, MFA, an ambassador designation and a public-figure badge alone do not meet the proposal's identity requirement. Proposal review and any approval take place separately from public visibility or rollout. Volunteer applications, task assignments, milestones, and progress or evidence have their own permissions. Published metrics can include self-reported observations.

Project approval means an approved proposal met Ayinou's applicable review criteria. It does not establish financial legitimacy, fundraising authorization, legal status, or a guarantee of safety, delivery or success. Organizers and volunteers must verify their own permissions, safety arrangements, qualifications and commitments before acting in the real world. Avoid sharing unnecessary contact, health, identity or location details in an application, task or report.

## 8. Polls and ambassadors

Ayinou polls are community consultations and advisory votes. They are separate from official government elections and do not appoint officials, allocate authority, or independently approve a project. Eligibility depends on current scope and the poll's rules. Some higher-trust polls require reviewed identity and a frozen electorate. An eligible account can submit one accepted choice for a poll. A voter can see their own receipt, while other viewers see permitted aggregate results subject to the poll's publication rules. A nominee's participation depends on eligibility and consent.

Founding Ambassadors participate voluntarily. An ambassador designation recognizes participation in that program. It does not convey moderation rights, verified personal identity, organizational authority, or greater voting weight. Public recognition depends on the program's eligibility and publicity controls.

## 9. Conduct, moderation and appeals

Follow the [Community Guidelines](COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES_DRAFT.md). Do not threaten or harass others, impersonate people or institutions, expose protected information, manipulate polls or platform signals, distribute unauthorized or exploitative media, or use the service to defraud or endanger people.

Use the report controls where available. Authorized reviewers may restrict content, limit access, suspend accounts, or revoke a claim using the applicable workflow. Available in-app appeals include eligible current hides of your own posts or comments and eligible verification rejection or revocation decisions. Those flows currently allow filing within 14 days under their respective rules. Staff availability and conflicts can affect review timing. See [L1-02 and L1-09 approved support or review procedure] for other concerns. Do not bypass a restriction through another account or misuse reports to retaliate.
