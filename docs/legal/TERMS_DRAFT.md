## 2. Definitions and capacities

- A **member** is an individual with an Ayinou account. Membership on the platform creates no corporate voting rights or position in a legal entity.
- An **institution** is an organization or public body represented by a distinct profile. A **representative** acts through a personal account with current authority to act for that institution.
- A **community** is an official department space or a member-created group. Its managers exercise authority within that space.
- A **verified claim** is a particular claim approved under a defined review process. Its scope may cover identity, public-figure status, an institution or an authorized representative.
- A **project** is a community initiative proposed or operated through Ayinou. An **advisory poll** captures eligible account choices to inform discussion or a recommendation.

See the [policy glossary](L4_POLICY_GLOSSARY.md) for how these terms are used across the drafts.

Review the published profile and contacts when the institution's status changes.

Representatives use individual accounts. Their powers depend on current platform grants and the institution's real mandate. Use the least authority needed. Do not let a representative grant powers outside their own scope, take over another person's account or expose protected contacts. An institution should remove outdated grants promptly, keep an internal authority record, protect separate staff accounts and use approved succession procedures. Custom roles, affiliations, verified authority and public representative consent have separate effects. Access can be restricted during a security or authority dispute. ## 10. Advisory polls and nominees

Only eligible individual accounts may cast ballots. An institutional profile adds no vote. Poll scope and applicable eligibility govern who can participate. A voter can see their own supported receipt. Authorized viewers may see aggregates under the published result settings. Inconclusive results do not appoint anyone or approve a project. Polls are advisory and create no official election, legal appointment or corporate decision. [Voting & Polling Policy](VOTING_AND_POLLING_POLICY_DRAFT.md)
