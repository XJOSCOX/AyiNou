# Ayinou Organization & Representative Policy

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

## 1. Who this covers

This policy covers registered and informal organizations, schools, charities, businesses, media, governments and other public institutions, and the individuals acting on their behalf. An informal organization can describe its work and apply under the supported category without claiming formal formation. A private neighborhood initiative should give its actual name and activities. A school, charity or municipal body should use its correct jurisdiction and distinguish official authority from a private initiative using similar language.

## 2. Creation, ownership claims and accuracy

Create or claim a profile only with appropriate authority from the organization. Use a nonmisleading name, authorized/logo images and contact details approved for the relevant audience. State whether incorporation, charity recognition, licensing or accreditation is actually held. A platform profile or an initial owner grant does not establish legal title, representation or registered status. Use the existing signed-in organization workspace. A competing claimant should use the scoped ownership review or impersonation report rather than creating a duplicate identity. Public review should rely on current, independent sources appropriate to the category and mandate. Informal activity can be corroborated through appropriate independent community references. [Verification & Badges Policy](VERIFICATION_AND_BADGES_POLICY_DRAFT.md)

## 3. Management and delegation

Each representative uses a personal account. Exercise only the current platform grant and actual organizational mandate. A foundational role, custom grant, ordinary affiliation and verified representative claim confer distinct capabilities. Apply least privilege and use the existing consent flow for invitations. A person may decline a role and an institution should revoke access when duties end or are disputed. Custom grants remain scoped to the exact institution and permitted actions. Do not split duties across fake accounts, delegate beyond your own authority, retain credentials for departed staff or manufacture authority through a badge. Use recent MFA when required by the workflow.

| Practical situation | Expected conduct |
| --- | --- |
| Staff member helps edit the public description | Give relevant permissions only, and review what the person will be able to publish. |
| New director or authorized representative | Confirm the underlying mandate, scope and term. Review what appears publicly before seeking the person's consent. |
| Manager leaves or loses access | Remove or revoke their applicable grant through the authorized workflow and preserve continuity through a verified successor. Use restricted review for disputed ownership. |
| Volunteer or member appears in a directory | Obtain the required opt-in and use limited directory fields. Affiliation neither grants management authority nor makes a private contact public. |

## 4. Privacy and accountable public display

Keep staff contact details, employment information, applications, private lists and organizational records in their authorized audiences. A verified representative's public listing depends on current eligibility and separate publicity consent. Do not treat acceptance of an invitation as consent to publicity. Update or withdraw inaccurate public information promptly. Record authorized decisions in the relevant workflow and keep the response evidence accessible only to eligible reviewers. Institutions should handle records they collect off-platform under their own applicable obligations. [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md)

## 5. Disputes, restrictions, removal and succession

Report impersonation, unauthorized control or related wrongdoing through eligible tools. An applicable restriction can hide a profile and suspend roles or outstanding invitations. Evidence and conflicts should be handled separately from the parties' public statements. Existing formal verification eligibility, denial or revocation appeals follow the relevant verification process. Institutional restrictions do not have a guaranteed dedicated appeal or self-service lift. [Approval required — independent route for ownership and restriction disputes, emergency authority, timing and notice.]

A departing owner should use the scoped transfer workflow where available and confirm the recipient's current eligibility and consent. For merger, dispute, closure or dissolution, avoid reassignment where legal control is unclear. Determine which institution records, project obligations and participant rights survive under approved policy. Removal from public view cannot be treated as complete data erasure. [Data Rights & Retention Policy](DATA_RIGHTS_AND_RETENTION_POLICY_DRAFT.md)

## 6. Accountability and limits

Organizations are responsible for the accuracy of their claims, conduct of authorized representatives and actual off-platform operations. Ayinou evaluates specified claims and can restrict use of its service. A profile, review outcome or approved role does not make the platform a guarantor of governance, finances, provider qualifications or program outcomes.
