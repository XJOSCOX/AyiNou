## 1. Claims currently reviewed

| Claim | What reviewers assess | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity Verified | Whether authoritative sources or an approved independent channel connect the holder of a specific account with the claimed identity. | A self-submitted profile alone is insufficient. A badge does not certify statements, character or unrelated qualifications. |
| Notable public figure | The claimed identity and sustained public role, supported by independently credible references. | A public role claim does not create institutional authority. |
| Verified organization | The specific institution's identity or standing under the applicable review. | Review supports only the recorded claim. It is not a general statement that the institution is licensed, accredited, incorporated or tax-exempt. |
| Verified representative | The person's current authority for a specific institution and scope. | Expiry, restriction or revocation can remove the projected claim. Publicity requires its own consent and eligibility. |

The bootstrap route established distinct administrator provenance. It does not count as independent personal identity verification. A trust mark, institution affiliation and the permissions of an active account are different things.

## 2. Evidence and reviewer independence

Provide the permitted public HTTPS references and concise facts needed for the specific claim. Do not submit passwords, secret links, government ID files, medical records or private third-party records. Independent sources matter more than multiple links repeating a self-submitted claim. Institutional review uses two or three public references across at least two hosts and evaluates source independence. Reviewers with a disqualifying relationship, involvement or other conflict should recuse and lose case access where applicable. Sensitive actions require current permissions and recent authenticator verification. A personal application requires two eligible independent approvals and current assignments. Applicable institutional verification and ownership review follow the corresponding independent decision rules. A shortage of eligible reviewers can leave cases pending.

Record a scoped finding and reason. Keep raw evidence references and reviewer assignments out of public badges and notice previews. General administration does not confer unrestricted access to evidence. Evidence links can lead to external hosts with their own practices. [Approval required — reviewer training, operational staffing, proof alternatives, secure exception handling and retention.]

## 3. Corrections, impersonation and appeals

Report suspected impersonation or misleading authority through eligible profile or institution controls. Identify the disputed claim and use permitted evidence. Report counts alone cannot decide a case. An authorized reviewer may limit a disputed claim's display, and approved review can revoke it. An eligible written denial or revocation can be appealed once per decision within 14 days. The independent reviewer excludes the applicant, conflicted reviewers and original reviewers. Reconsideration permits a fresh decision and does not itself restore badges, roles or ownership. No implication of endorsement, platform authority or additional vote follows from a badge.

## 4. Future design — Founder Verified

Founder Verified is a proposed separate designation. It would require approved proof of identity and control of the account, recent MFA, a recorded reason, auditability, conflict handling and independent routes for correction, appeal and revocation. Acquaintance, invitation, a platform administrator role or a privately asserted relationship would be insufficient. Adoption requires explicit approval of badge text, audience, proof criteria, review authority, security controls and eligibility tests.

Identity Verified would remain the existing independently reviewed personal identity path. Founder Verified would not automatically satisfy project submission, institutional management or other higher-trust eligibility. Any equivalence would require separate authorization and technical validation. A future temporary proof challenge, if activated via an approved channel, would establish only the limited fact tested. It could not award either badge directly.
