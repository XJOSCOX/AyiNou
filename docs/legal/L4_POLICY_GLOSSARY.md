# Ayinou L4 policy glossary

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

Use these meanings throughout the draft suite. The planned legal operator should approve terminology and precedence before localization or publication.

| Term | Meaning and usage |
| --- | --- |
| Ayinou / operator | Ayinou is the service. The legal person responsible for the service is undetermined. Use a marked operator placeholder pending the formation and transition decision. |
| Member / account holder | A natural person using their own Ayinou account. Membership does not convey nonprofit corporate membership, employment or legal office. |
| Corporate member | A member under the future entity's governing documents if that structure is adopted. Do not infer membership from a profile or an advisory vote. |
| Institution / organization profile | An organization or public entity and its distinct platform representation. Individuals retain their own authentication when acting for it. |
| Informal organization / registered organization | An informal group can participate without claiming registration. A registered group's authority depends on facts established outside Ayinou. A profile creates neither status. |
| Representative / owner / affiliate | A representative has scoped authority. The owner holds a defined platform grant. Affiliation is a separate relationship and does not confer management rights. |
| Platform administrator / legal officer | A privileged software operator and a corporate officeholder exercise different powers. Neither office confers the other. |
| Verified claim / endorsement | Verification is a scoped, reviewed finding. Endorsement is an affirmative recommendation or assumption of responsibility that a badge does not convey. |
| Identity Verified / Founder Verified | Identity Verified denotes the existing independently reviewed identity path. Founder Verified is a distinct proposal awaiting approvals and tests. Neither label describes first-account administrator bootstrap provenance. |
| Advisory poll / binding decision | A poll records eligible choices to inform discussion or recommendations. A binding appointment, approval or legal vote must come from its independently authorized process. |
| Project approval / financial endorsement | Project approval records an applicable product review. It does not certify finances, qualify donations, offer funding or guarantee outcomes. |
| Technical expiration / removal / erasure | Expiration ends a feature window, removal changes an eligible projection and erasure concerns the approved actual disposition of data across relevant stores. |
| Retention / evidence hold | Retention is an approved disposition schedule. A hold is scoped preservation with its own authority, review and release. |
| Synthetic export / real access right | A fictional test artifact and an applicable legal entitlement or authorized disclosure are different. Avoid promising full exports or mentioning fixture timing as public policy. |
| Early Partner / verified organization | Early Partner is a future bounded pilot designation. A verified organization has completed the currently supported scoped review. No automatic conversion or benefit follows. |

## Authority across documents

The [Terms](TERMS_DRAFT.md) describe the agreement and common capacities. The [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md) describes information handling. Specialized policies explain feature scopes and procedure. The [Community Guidelines](COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES_DRAFT.md) describe conduct and enforcement across resources. Applicable mandatory rights prevail. Do not elevate internal governance, research, test evidence or future annexes into contractual terms. Keep approved versions, dependencies, effective dates and translations aligned.
