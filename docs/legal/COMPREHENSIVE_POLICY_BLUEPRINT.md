## Community Guidelines and moderation revisions

- Keep the conduct rule, decision criteria and action separate. Address threats, harassment, impersonation, fraud, unauthorized fundraising, misleading projects, private records and precise sensitive locations, manipulation of voters, fictitious or coordinated accounts, spam and exploitation or abuse involving vulnerable people. Preserve legitimate criticism and good-faith disagreement.
- Explain current report and block/mute controls without suggesting that reports or badges prove a fact or that an account block hides all public content. Use an approved private contact for matters outside current report controls. Where policies would introduce a new reporting queue, takedown route, ban review or response target, require operational and technical proof first.
- Develop a severity matrix and independent reviewer separation. Consider history, harm, evidence reliability and scope. For suspected urgent harm, limit access, preserve necessary evidence in a restricted case, escalate to designated people and follow counsel's approved obligations by market. A user-facing policy should avoid promises of automatic verification, continuous monitoring or fixed turnaround without verified coverage.
- State accurately the two in-app appeal flows and filing windows. Create an appropriately staffed escalation plan for limitations affecting account access or institutional/group scope. Log reasons and decisions and test conflict checks, user notice, confidential reporter information, record holds and repeat violations.
