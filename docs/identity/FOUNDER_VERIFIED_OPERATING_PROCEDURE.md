# Founder Verified operating procedure

The uniquely recognized, active Founder opens a case for an eligible member by username from the original-session private Administration workspace. A regular identity application is optional. With the standing policy active, the Founder starts eligible cases directly in Ayinou. Each member still grants consent and confirms account control. Check recent TOTP and confirm that the relationship presents no disqualifying conflict. Stop or record a denial when identity cannot be confidently established. Declare a conflict when independence or safety is in doubt.

The member signs in to the invited account and accepts in Verification. Choose Confirm account control, read the declaration, select the checkbox and confirm in the same signed-in session within two minutes. Keep that session active until the Founder finishes. An authenticator is recommended and optional for ordinary members. The member chooses public disclosure. A member with an eligible pending application may separately opt in to closure of that exact revision. Declining the offer or withdrawing consent uses the member case controls. A case proceeding without closure consent leaves independent review available.

Meet the known member in person or conduct a live interactive video call. Verify through meaningful visual and verbal interaction that this is the intended person. Keep account ownership separate from recognizing the person in the meeting. Use the structured private attestation to confirm the method, the identity match, the account-control match and absence of a conflict. The authorized service binds the attestation to the Founder, case, current account, receipt, time and active issuance policy. Avoid collecting documents, photos, recordings or meeting content. Do not ask for the member's TOTP code.

Issue the grant only with a current receipt and exact case version. Verify the member history, badge disclosure choice and any application closure receipt. A later gold revocation preserves the original application history.

A member may withdraw or request correction in Verification. A correction on a granted case holds the designation and opens a private appeal. An authorized database operator assigns an eligible independent verification reviewer using a separately recorded approval reference. Reviewers use their own S2C workspace and fresh MFA. The Founder, original issuer and subject are ineligible to adjudicate. Review only the allowed case context. An upheld hold requires a later authorized release. If review is unavailable, leave the hold in place. Hold suspected impersonation or account compromise promptly and use the existing authorized account safety or recovery process. Record an identity mismatch as an insufficient-proof denial before issuance or hold and independently review a disputed grant. Document personal conflicts through the case action and keep gold suppressed while the issue is unresolved. Honour voluntary withdrawal through the member case and preserve the receipt.
