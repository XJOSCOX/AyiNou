# Founder Verified identity eligibility

The private `personal_identity_provenance(subject)` distinguishes independent review, Founder-direct blue review and gold Founder Verified. Gold requires a currently eligible account, trusted Founder issuer, a granted and unconflicted case, current consent, approved live proof and the member receipt. The shared eligibility check accepts independently reviewed identity or live gold. Disclosure controls badge visibility separately from identity eligibility. Synthetic evidence remains ineligible.

| Effective capability | Unverified | Gold only | Blue only | Both | Intended boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal account activity, ordinary polls or volunteer actions with no identity gate | Existing role and membership checks | Same | Same | Same | Identity never supplies a role or access assignment. |
| Ordinary non-government organization setup | No | Eligible with current setup requirements | Same as gold | Same as gold | Current session and authority declaration still apply. |
| Existing organization ownership claims and protected organization corrections | No | May qualify | Same as gold | Same as gold | Institutional declaration, evidence and independent organizational review still apply. |
| Project submission and publication | Draft only where allowed | May qualify | Same as gold | Same as gold | Independent proposal review, publication consent and current owner authority still apply. |
| Volunteer managers, supervisors, sensitive tasks and project execution | No when identity required | May qualify | Same as gold | Same as gold | Scoped roles and project execution requirements still apply. |
| Verified and high-trust polls | No | May qualify | Same as gold | Same as gold | Scope, electorate, ballot privacy and frozen eligibility stay in force. |
| Founding Ambassadors | No | May qualify | Same as gold | Same as gold | Program acceptance, visibility and nomination rules still apply. |
| Administrator appointment and governance succession | No | May qualify | Same as gold | Same as gold | Target acceptance, fresh MFA and governance approval still apply. |
| Reviewer, institutional representative, professional or program authority | Separate approval | Separate approval | Separate approval | Separate approval | A personal identity grant conveys no evidence access, role, legal status or credential. |

The individual proposal workflow checks identity at submission, approval and public read time. The recognized platform Founder qualifies for their own proposal with active account standing and a verified authenticator. A held or revoked identity record prevents fallback to gold. Project execution, verified volunteer operations and polls use this identity check and continue to enforce their own action-specific requirements. Discover includes disclosed, eligible gold badges in its Verified only filter. Gold members can request independent identity review through the member workspace. The request requires two eligible independent reviewers and stays open if a later gold grant is issued.

Public Founder, Administrator, blue Identity Verified and gold Founder Verified retain different labels and colors. Holding both identity sources does not create duplicate claims or independent reviewer events.
