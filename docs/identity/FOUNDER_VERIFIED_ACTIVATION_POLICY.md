# Founder Verified issuance policy

The standing issuance policy lets the recognized Founder verify any eligible, consenting member directly in Ayinou. The owner approved that scope. An operator records one standing policy for the intended environment with its approval reference, the recognized Founder, case policy version, and permitted identity methods. Individual cases need no database cohort additions. Founder-driven case, consent, proof and grant events remain mandatory. The bounded cohort policy remains available for legacy development tests before standing activation. Applying the schema does not create a standing policy or authorize public launch.

Before standing activation, verify the exact project, tested release, recognized active Founder, permitted in-person and interactive live-video methods, and existing case and audit counts. Under the trust decision transaction lock `pg_advisory_xact_lock(20261120,300)`, record the separately approved operation with scope `eligible_members`, both gates enabled and no expiry. Recheck that both gates select this single policy before commit. Record the approval reference and activation evidence. The prior test approval and member rows remain historical. No application role or service API can configure or stop policies.

An emergency stop records the stop timestamp and a separate approval reference under the same transaction lock. Once a standing policy has started, its stop cannot fall back to an old test cohort. Recovery uses a new approved policy. Pending receipt and attestation work must be reconfirmed under the current policy when the policy changes. Grants issued under a valid past policy retain provenance and remain subject to current eligibility, consent, disclosure, holds and revocation.

Runtime proof binds one allowed method and one live policy to a case and original authenticated member session. The member confirms once in the same active session using the case-bound challenge. An authenticator is optional for ordinary members. The Founder still needs recent TOTP and personally attests identity. An expired or stopped policy stops proof and issuance, suppresses gold projections and removes gold-only O4 eligibility. Review or revoke as appropriate using the scoped workflow. Keep historical case, proof, grant, appeal and application closure references.

Apply the existing records and hold policy. Proposed review points are seven days for expired account-control session binding and twelve months after case closure for decision provenance, subject to existing holds and applicable requirements. There is no automated purge in this release. Do not collect raw identity evidence. Retain disposition and audit references through approved lifecycle tools.
