# R1 role and permission matrix

Founder and Administrator are platform leadership roles. Blue and gold are identity outcomes. A badge, name, email, profile property, navigation link or claim in a browser never substitutes for a live server or SQL permission check.

| Authority and status | Establishment, appointment and revocation | Assurance and action scope | Read scope, UI and audit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Founder, implemented | Reserved one-time bootstrap produces singleton provenance and matching platform audit. Ordinary role changes cannot create another Founder. Protected succession and recovery remain separate. An inactive account loses effective authority. | Six-hour absolute session, current TOTP for privileged operations and original-session admin workspace. Inherits routine Administrator operations. Can authorize appointments and directly decide eligible regular blue applications, and issue gold under its distinct policy. | Platform administration, Trust and verification and Founder identity cases. Live conflict, evidence and action scopes still apply. Native platform, O5, identity, security and A1A ledgers. FDR-01 through FDR-05, BLU-05, GLD-01. |
| Administrator, implemented | Eligible active assignment. Founder-direct appointment requires exact recipient acceptance and fresh MFA with one-time credential. Protected restriction/revocation uses existing governance paths. | Six-hour session and recent MFA where required. Routine platform administration, safety, moderation and eligible proposals. No direct Founder blue or gold issuance. | Platform administration, scoped reports and A1A summaries. Protected privacy, evidence, ballots and release rights use separate predicates. Platform and O5 audit. FDR-03, ACC-05, GOV-03. |
| Moderator, implemented | Scoped moderator or eligible platform role under current predicates. Eligible administration manages supported assignments. | Current active account, domain scope and applicable MFA. Moderation and eligible appeals run separate conflict and evidence checks. | Scoped feed/community/project/poll actions, with native moderation and appeal events. Platform role does not grant billing, privacy or independent review evidence. SOC-02, PRO-01, POL-04. |
| Ordinary Verification Reviewer, implemented | Current reviewer assignment plus enrolled verified TOTP and exact work assignment. Eligible administrators can assign work under conflict rules; authorized reviewer can add eligible work. | Recent TOTP to open reviewer workspace. No self or conflicted cases. Ordinary blue uses two distinct eligible approvals; rejection/revocation have separate state guards. | Reviewer queue and exact assigned evidence. Independent appeal must meet its own eligibility. Application, work, decisions, assignment and appeal audit. BLU-03, BLU-04, BLU-07. |
| Ordinary Member, implemented | Authenticated active account and member setup. Account restriction and revocation remove current access. | Original session, 12 hours or 30 days remembered; MFA optional unless a distinct operation requires it. Own profile, submissions, consent, reports and permitted scopes. | Member pages show actor-safe projections and their own application history. Session, username and domain event sources. SES-01, ACC-04, BLU-01, GLD-02. |
| Organization Owner and Manager, implemented | Created through authorized organization setup or granted within entity scope. Role boundaries, revocation and conflict checks remain native. | Active account and institution-specific eligibility. Sensitive roles/changes have separate authority, MFA and scope. Platform authority does not imply organization ownership. | Institution settings and operational activity. Membership, role and action auditing. ORG-01 through ORG-06. |
| Custom organization role, implemented | Owner/delegate creates a supported role and assignment within current grant ceiling. Revocation, expiry and deactivation invalidate effective scope. | Specific permission, live role and exact entity required. | Scoped staff management, custom role and assignment audit; no global platform authority. ORG-03, ORG-04. |
| Community or group owner, manager and moderator, implemented | Established by eligible creation, invitation or scoped staff assignment. Role and membership changes can end access. | Exact group/department, current membership and domain checks. Moderation adds actor and conflict validation. | Group management and scoped moderation. Staff and membership events remain in domain. COM-01 through COM-04. |
| Project sponsor, manager, supervisor, team member and volunteer, implemented | Authorized sponsor and project team workflows delegate specific project authority. Current sponsor and related institution eligibility are rechecked. | Separate proposal/execution roles, current independent identity where required, and applicable TOTP. | Project review, execution, volunteering, tasks and milestones only in scope. Native project ledgers. PRO-01 through PRO-05. |
| Independent appeal reviewer, implemented by domain | Appointed under case-specific authority. Founder, original decision maker or subject can be excluded by the applicable conflict predicate. | Active eligible independent reviewer and current required MFA/workspace. | Exact appeal and redacted authorized evidence, not broad administrative visibility. BLU-07, GLD-06, SOC-02. |
| Program Operator and Support Staff, partial/reserved | Early Partner operators have specific controlled program assignments. General platform Support Staff and cross-domain Program Operator remain reserved. | Case-specific operator control, eligible sessions and consent. Synthetic support/privacy workflows are separate. | Scoped program views and immutable native events. Real enrollment and real privacy operation stay gated. PRG-01, PRI-01. |
| Legal publication operator and privacy delegate, protected | Eligible leadership accesses legal controls; policy release authority remains independent. Privacy roles are scoped to controlled workflows. | Required session and evidence plus separate live gate. Platform rank does not enable real privacy processing or authorize a document release. | Legal revision/history or authorized privacy case context. Native restricted ledgers. LEG-01, LEG-02, PRI-01 through PRI-04. |

The SQL, API, UI and test references for the cited IDs are in the [source map](R1_SOURCE_TO_ENFORCEMENT_MAP.md). The [existing controls inventory](R1_EXISTING_ADMIN_CONTROLS.md) covers who can use each current administrative surface and which operations are mutable.
