# R1 owner decisions

## Current agreed baseline

- The recognized Founder inherits routine platform administration and may authorize eligible Administrator appointments with recipient acceptance and MFA.
- The Founder already may directly approve or reject an eligible blue application in the private administration workspace. Ordinary reviewers currently require two distinct eligible approvals. A configurable threshold is a proposal.
- Blue and gold stay separate. Standing development gold issuance uses separate member consent/account control and Founder attestation. The newer gold grant automatically closes an eligible pending regular application and keeps its history.
- Bootstrap, raw ballot secrecy, current session/MFA controls, protected governance, legal publication and real privacy processing keep their current authority boundaries.

## Decisions before implementation

1. Approve initial read-only registry scope and the first low-risk writable policy. Identify domain owners and who can propose, approve and activate each class.
2. Decide whether ordinary blue can ever use a threshold below two, whether increases allow transitions of open cases, and which high-trust eligibility workflows must demand existing independent provenance.
3. Decide permitted ranges, protected minima, moderation duration policy and emergency stop/recovery authority. Set which existing holds and session restrictions are always live.
4. Decide when organization or project changes affect existing applicants and who may approve exceptions or migrations. Review notices and effective dates.
5. Define treatment of legacy cases with incomplete provenance. Preserve their existing decisions and audit; avoid synthetic approvals or evidence.
6. Authorize or reject any future provider/configuration changes separately. Confirm actual hosted settings, mailbox, backup, secret management and incident recovery readiness outside the panel.
7. Decide who may inspect changed policy reasons and previews, and the retention policy for those events. Keep legal/privacy release authorities separate.
8. Approve R2 scope separately. Any hosted schema migration requires exact authorization after checks and preflight.
