## Open policy and operating decisions

| Decision needed | Type | Proposed disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legacy-session migration and provider capabilities | Owner decision and operational evidence | Confirm authoritative session start, stable IDs, refresh and revocation support. Specify reauthentication or safe treatment when trusted start data is unavailable. Assess any separate policy for sensitive domain operators independently of the confirmed limits. |
| First workflows to adopt the shared `/s` router and when to bind to the initiating session | Owner decision | Start with one account-bound draft in parallel to the legacy route, then one privileged workflow after session proof. Preserve approved F1 paths during transition. |
| Succession, compromised-account recovery and independent approver staffing for workflows that still require it | Owner decision and operational approval | Name independent roles, emergency containment and recorded thresholds for those workflows. Founder-direct Administrator appointments have no second-Administrator requirement under the revised policy. |
| Invitation delivery, lost delivery/reissue and obsolete O5 acceptance | Owner decision and operational approval | Start with separate private delivery and audited revocation. Prefer unified target acceptance and versioned reissue rather than code retrieval. |
| Founder proof procedure, privacy-safe evidence retention, appeal and independent review staffing | Owner decision and operational approval | Approve evidence source, retention/holds, restricted access, neutral appeals and staffed operators before enabling real issuance. Keep O4 parity separate. |
| Username cooldown, aliases/retirements, appeals and support staffing | Owner decision and operational approval | Compare existing daily guard to a longer cooldown. Preserve UUIDs, visibility checks and protected handles. Validate multilingual contact handling. |
| Support Staff / Program Operator generalized permissions and restricted evidence access | Owner decision | Define finite issue-specific scope and grant/revocation routes. Build on approved domain roles without inheriting Administrator or Reviewer privileges. |
| Hosted Auth/SMTP, recoverability, RPO/RTO and public release | Operational approval | Review exact deployment and recovery evidence. Named operators and verified Storage/database restoration precede public launch. Require separate approval for any optional hosted reset. |
| Real legal/privacy processing, Early Partners, donations and medical referrals | Operational approval | Keep closed or unimplemented under their separate review, operator and policy gates. Account and role work confers no approval. |
