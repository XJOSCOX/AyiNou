## Minimum event and authorization contract

At the point of the command, derive the actor from the verified live session. Validate target, assignment, MFA, scope, version, conflicts and independent decisions as applicable under the appropriate transaction/lock. Store typed event, unique ID, service timestamp, actor/system provenance, scope, target, outcome, version, reason category and receipt/correlation where justified. Keep free-text reasons restricted. Failed transactions cannot create a success event. Emit only bounded denial telemetry separately or as an explicitly committed denial. Record race outcomes and retries without duplicating material events. Keep audit read permissions independent of write capabilities.

Founder setup should reconcile reservation, unlock, registration initiation and claim. F1 records preparation, unlock and claim. Unlock is the recorded transition into setup. A distinct successful Auth signup event linked to F1 is an integration gap. Administrator history should link proposal, approval, issuance, rejection/expiry or revocation, member acceptance and role assignment. Preserve O5 reasons and separate immutable evidence for independent actors. Do not log raw credentials, code verifiers, session tokens, ballot choices, proof content or unnecessary personal data.

Future Session Security must record material login and step-up signals, remembered-session creation, revocation, promotion/role change, privileged expiry and authorized workflow creation/completion as supported by the provider. Report observed events honestly. Define read access, retention period, collection purpose, revocation/hold behavior and privacy export redactions for each event class. Reuse domain ledgers and add versioned read projections only after their scope is approved.
