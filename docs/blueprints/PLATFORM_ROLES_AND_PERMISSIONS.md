# Platform roles and permissions

Founder is the unique top platform role. Administrator has broad routine authority. Moderator, Reviewer, Support Staff and Program Operator have purpose-specific scope. Member is the default personal account. One person may hold compatible assignments. Effective authority comes from trusted provenance, active assignments and resource-level checks.

**Matrix key.** **A** is authority supplied by the listed role. **M** means recent TOTP on the applicable sensitive command (or the stated domain assurance rule). **S** means a distinct current assignment and resource scope. **I** means independent approval. **Own** means self-service under account, scope and lifecycle checks. **Closed** means the path is gated or planned. **—** means the listed role supplies no authority. All allowed operations require a live eligible actor, and sensitive mutations require an attributable reason and audit where the domain defines one.
