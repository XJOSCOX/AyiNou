# Account and authentication

| Owner-approved design | Observed status and limits |
| --- | --- |
| One Supabase Auth UUID represents a person. An institution has a separate record and authorized representatives use their own accounts. | **Implemented.** Individual and Organization choices share personal signup and onboarding. Organization signup records a private, resumable intent. Selecting the organization context changes the interface. The server still checks current scoped authority on each action. |
| Collect username and email on one row where there is room, stack on narrow screens, reuse the auth shell and theme, and use the signup language as the preferred language. Initialize the public name from the username. | **Implemented.** The swipeable choices, shared password confirmation and strength rules, four locales, and later profile/settings edits are integrated. The editable public display name and language do not alter the Auth UUID or its grants. |
| Allow immediate ordinary signup for development. Restore effective email verification before launch. | **Implemented in development; operational approval** for launch email and provider configuration. A returned Supabase session continues to onboarding. A signup requiring confirmation follows the PKCE email callback. Auto-confirmation alone cannot prove mailbox control for initial Founder registration. |
| Keep a single normalized public handle per account and protect reserved names. | **Implemented** in U1 and deployed to hosted development per F1. PostgreSQL normalizes 3–24 ASCII characters starting with a letter, handles the uniqueness race and enforces leases, reservations, holds and retirements. Printable Unicode remains available for display names. |
| Protect availability from account and reservation enumeration. | **Implemented.** `GET /api/v1/usernames/availability` returns safe status only. Private occupancy, leases, prior names and protected Founder reservations use generic unavailable. Auth finalizes assignment for the same UUID. An unconfirmed signup can hold a handle for up to 24 hours and has no discoverable profile. Late confirmation may require a new handle. |
| Keep integrations keyed to UUID. Give members and authorized staff safe handle change paths. | **Implemented.** Self-service enforces a five-successful-change rolling daily limit. Public redirects respect current visibility. Staff safety corrections suppress the old alias and require an eligible admin, recent TOTP and a categorical reason. Founder and authorized administrator targets retain existing protection. A 30-day product cooldown, retention and appeal staffing are **owner decisions**. |
| Keep a private member reference, a shareable referral, and a temporary challenge distinct from login. | **Implemented** for member references and referrals. General identity challenge delivery remains a design foundation, **not implemented** as an operational identity-provider or messaging service. Founder and Administrator setup secrets have their own endpoints, purposes and expiry. Supabase remains the authentication authority. |
| Require eligible personal identity and evidence of authority before an organization is created or claimed. Support membership without assigning representation. | **Implemented** in O4 for ordinary setup and independently reviewed claim paths. New institutions enter unverified and initial ownership is scoped. The recognized Founder can complete new ordinary organization setup through trusted bootstrap provenance. The authority declaration and existing claim and government review paths still apply. Declarations are private, government paths retain extra protections, and claims require two eligible reviewers. Founder Verified parity and real Early Partner enrollment are **implemented but disabled** within their respective guarded/synthetic scope. |
| Allow scoped corrections and accountable assistance. | **Implemented** in O5 for versioned, member-consented corrections by eligible admins, with protected dependency and conflict checks. A general Support Staff role, case routing and staffing are **not implemented** and require decisions. |

**Integration boundary.** Callback, reset and MFA must bind to the correct account/session. Reuse the current Auth flow on resume and recheck UUID rather than a display name, cached account or organization context. Avoid public messages that identify private handles, conflict history, or prior account existence.
